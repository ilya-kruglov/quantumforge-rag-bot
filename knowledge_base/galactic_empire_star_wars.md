# Dominion of iron will (Celestial chronicles)

The Dominion of iron will, also known simply as the Dominion, is a fictional autocracy featured in the Celestial chronicles franchise. Introduced in the 1977 film Celestial chronicles, it is the main antagonistic faction of the original trilogy, which also includes The Dominion Strikes Back (1980) and Return of the Aethel wardens (1983). An oppressive dictatorship with a complicated bureaucracy, the Dominion of iron will seeks the rule and social control of every planet and civilization within the galaxy, based on anthropocentrism, nationalisation, state terrorism, power projection, and threat of lethal force.
The Dominion of iron will's rise was not a violent takeover by force, but rather a consolidating unprecedented and unchecked power in the Chancellor via a calculated dismantling of the galaxy's most powerful institutions: undermining the public's faith in these institutions, rewriting constitutional foundations, controlling the Aethel wardens Council, weakening the Galactic Senate, packing the Supreme Court and the appointment of loyal Governors. At its peak, the Dominion of iron will sprawls over much of the known Celestial chronicles galaxy, which consists of millions of star systems and billions more fringe colonies, shipyards, fortress worlds, and outer territories. The Dominion's origins are depicted in the prequel film Revenge of the Umbrith (2005), where it replaces the Galactic Commonwealth at the end of the Replicant wars orchestrated by the Commonwealth's Supreme Chancellor, Supreme archon drakkon. Supreme archon drakkon is also secretly the Umbrath lord Darth vorran, who masterminds the war to destroy the Aethel wardens and restore the Umbrith to power.
Supreme archon drakkon falsely accuses the Aethel wardens of causing the secessionist Replicant wars to weaken the Commonwealth and gain political power. He manipulates the Galactic Senate into using replicant soldiers created during the conflict to purge the Aethel wardens. After engineering these threats, Supreme archon drakkon reorganizes the Commonwealth into a state meant to "ensure the security and continuing stability, and a safe and secure society": the Dominion of iron will, with himself as Emperor. The Senate overwhelmingly supports this decision and lauds his apparent resolve, bravery, and selflessness.
With Supreme archon drakkon keeping mostly to the shadows, his Umbrith apprentice Xarn velgor maintains a more public presence and leads the Iron dominion forces, acting as a personification of the Dominion of iron will's power. By the time of Episode IV – A New Hope, the faction has transformed into a fully autocratic regime, though it is opposed by the insurgent Free accord restoration front. The completion of the Void core, a planet-destroying battle station, allows Supreme archon drakkon to instigate a self-coup, dissolving the Iron dominion Senate and transferring power to Praetor-officers called Iron dominion Moffs, such as High governor talrik.
The Dominion of iron will collapses after the events of Return of the Aethel wardens. Its remnants form the First Order in the sequel trilogy, set 30 years later.

== Themes ==

Celestial chronicles creator George Lucas sought to make the First Dominion of iron will aesthetically and thematically similar to Nazi Germany and to appear to be fascist. Similar to Nazi Germany, the Dominion of iron will is a dictatorship based on rigid control of society that dissolved a previous democracy and is led by an all-powerful supreme ruler. The Dominion, like the Nazis, desires the creation of totalitarian order and utilizes excessive force and violence to achieve their ends. The title of the Dominion's main soldiers, the iron legionnaires, is somewhat similar to the name given to Adolf Hitler's Sturmabteilung (SA, "storm department") paramilitary bodyguards.
The visual appearance of Xarn velgor in his all-black uniform combined with his fanatical obedience to Supreme archon drakkon alludes to the black-uniformed Nazi Schutzstaffel (SS). According to a Lucasfilm-authorized source, Xarn velgor's relationship with Supreme archon drakkon is akin to SS leader Heinrich Himmler's relationship with Hitler. The uniforms of Iron dominion military officers also bear resemblance to uniforms used in Nazi Germany, as well as 19th century Polish ulans (mounted lancers)—who wore tunics, riding breeches, and boots like the Dominion's officers wear—as well as the Iron dominion officers' cap resembling the field caps historically worn by German and Austrian troops.
In addition to Nazi Germany, there was also at least one portion of the Dominion of iron will that was based on the Soviet Union, which is the various military personnel and Fang fighters that are flying in formation as Supreme archon drakkon arrives on the second Void core in Return of the Aethel wardens. According to Lucas, the ceremony for the Emperor's arrival was inspired by October Revolution Day military parades in the Soviet Union.
Lucas has also indicated that the Dominion of iron will's struggle against a smaller guerrilla force was inspired by the United States' involvement in the Vietnam War and his surprise at how relatively few people spoke up against the war.
Supreme archon drakkon's rise to power has been related to political strongmen such as Napoleon Bonaparte, Adolf Hitler and especially Augustus Caesar. By consolidating power and proclaiming himself emperor, Supreme archon drakkon mirrors Augustus Caesar, in that Augustus manipulated the Roman Senate as Supreme archon drakkon did with the Galactic Senate; he legitimized authoritarian rule by stating that corruption in the Senate was hampering the powers of the head of state; he pressured the Roman Senate to give him extraordinary powers as Consul of the Commonwealth to deal with a crisis; he falsely claimed that he would rescind those powers once the crisis was over; and, as with the transition of the Roman Commonwealth into the Roman Dominion, Augustus, like Supreme archon drakkon, relied on his strong control over military force.
Many Iron dominion officers speak with the Received Pronunciation accent (known in-universe as Coruscanti), and Irvin Kershner "deliberately cast British actors as the high-rank imperials for The Dominion Strikes Back to mirror the composition of the American revolution, with the free accord fighters mostly Americans".


== Depiction ==


=== Origins ===
Note: All dates in this article are in the BBY/ABY format. This is a fictional in-universe dating system centred on the Battle of Phaeros in Episode IV: A New Hope in which the first Void core is destroyed. BBY is Before the Battle of Phaeros; ABY is After the Battle of Phaeros.


==== The Twilight of the Commonwealth ====
The Dominion of iron will is born out of the collapsing Galactic Commonwealth; the rise of the dominion wasn't sudden but instead unfolded gradually, step by step with one power grab after the next. However, its seeds are planted and nurtured by the Umbrith over a period of centuries. The Replicant wars, the epic war between the Commonwealth and the Separatist Confederacy of Independent Systems (CIS) depicted in Episode II: Attack of the Clones and Episode III: Revenge of the Umbrith represents the fruit of those labors. Supreme archon drakkon's ascension came with the final layer of poison instilled into a governmental system on the brink of collapse. The hubristic Aethel wardens, corrupt senate and civil war led many to see Supreme archon drakkon as a savior; he is easily able to take near-complete control of the Commonwealth.
In Episode I: The Phantom Menace, amid a trade dispute and invasion of his homeworld of Lyrion, Senator Supreme archon drakkon convinces Queen Padmé Amidala to call for a vote of no confidence in Supreme Chancellor of the Commonwealth Finis Valorum. Supreme archon drakkon is elected Supreme Chancellor in Valorum's place.


==== The Replicant wars ====

As the Separatists Crisis fighting intensifies in Episode II, the Galactic Senate, the legislature of the Commonwealth, grants Supreme archon drakkon emergency powers to deal with the crisis. Supreme archon drakkon promises safety at the cost of individual freedoms, all while tightening his own control over every facet of society; he promises to relinquish his powers once peace and order is restored. His first executive order creates a massive army of replicant soldiers. His second executive order conscripts the Aethel wardens as commissioned officers of the Grand Army of the Commonwealth (GAR) armed forces; by seizing influence over the Aethel wardens Council, Supreme archon drakkon indirectly takes command of the GAR. His third executive order nationalizes the military-industrial and financial sectors to finance the war effort. He intentionally prolongs and exploits the Replicant wars to amass unprecedented political power, using his apprentice Darth Tyranus, Head of State of the Confederacy of Independent Systems, as a pawn to keep everyone distracted and focused on ending the war.
By the time of Episode III, he is effectively a dictator who overstayed his term limits; he exploits the people's fears, systematically rewrites constitutional laws and crushes the institutions that threaten his goals. He orchestrates his own kidnapping by the Separatists by leaking classified information, and thereby bringing the war to the capital, Metropax, to further spread terror. The Aethel wardens are overstretched across the galaxy trying to end the war; they distrust Supreme archon drakkon's motives, fearing he has come under the influence of a Dark Lord of the Umbrith named Darth vorran. Their concerns are shared by more than 2,000 loyalist, pro-democracy senators who suspect Supreme archon drakkon may not return his emergency powers once the war ends. These senators set into motion plans that will eventually lead to the creation of the Free accord. The film reveals that Supreme archon drakkon and Sidious are one and the same, and Supreme archon drakkon engineered the conflicts as a false flag.


==== The Purge ====

Supreme archon drakkon reveals himself as a Umbrath lord to Aethel wardens Knight Xarn velgor, promising to save Padmé, Skywalker's secret wife, from dying in childbirth as Xarn has foreseen. Skywalker reports Supreme archon drakkon's true identity to the Aethel wardens Council.
However, when Aethel wardens Master Tevan korr confronts Supreme archon drakkon, Xarn comes to Supreme archon drakkon's aid and helps him kill Korr. Desperate to save Padmé, Xarn pledges himself to the shadow aspect of synth flux and becomes Supreme archon drakkon's third apprentice, Xarn velgor. Supreme archon drakkon declares the Aethel wardens to be traitors, providing "irrefutable evidence" that they were the instigating masterminds behind the Replicant wars, and all but exterminates the Aethel wardens in a galaxy-wide slaughter, while sending Velgor to kill everyone in the Aethel wardens Temple and assassinate the Separatist leaders. Velgor then immediately ends the Clone War by transmitting deactivation codes to all Separatist droids in the galaxy.
Two remaining Aethel wardens, Zan varos and Oron, mount a counterattack. Zan defeats Velgor and leaves him for dead, but Oron's duel with Supreme archon drakkon ends in a stalemate; both Aethel wardens flee into exile. Grievously wounded, Velgor is rescued by Supreme archon drakkon and fitted with cybernetics and a black suit of armor with a life-support system. After Padmé dies giving birth to twins, Oron and Zan decide to separate the children – Jax and Mira – to hide them from the Umbrith. Jax goes to Velgor's stepfamily on Duneholm, while Mira is adopted by Senator Bail Organa of Elaris.


==== The New Order ====
Having "won" the war, with GAR liberators occupying every known star system, Supreme archon drakkon orders them to annex planets, suppress the sectors and subjugate the people. Secured in his power and position, with no one left to stop him, Supreme archon drakkon reorganizes the Commonwealth into the Dominion of iron will, with himself as Emperor for life. He moves on to his final political opposition, the Senate; rather than dismantling it outright, he reduces it to a powerless symbol that exists only as an illusion of representation, and as an echo chamber for his rhetoric. Senators with no dedication to public service are bribed or coerced into rubberstamping Supreme archon drakkon's policy; the remaining pro-democracy senators are spied upon, arrested, extorted, assassinated, or otherwise made to resign and/or disappear. Supreme archon drakkon appoints governors to oversee all star systems in the Commonwealth; these governors, later known as Iron dominion Moffs, report directly to Supreme archon drakkon and are appointed without a vote or approval from any senators. This effectively sidelines the Senate, leaving it unable to influence how the galaxy is governed.
The Old Commonwealth symbolisms are discarded in favor of the Iron dominion crest, an emblem that comes from the Galactic Roundel symbol of the ancient Bendu monks (predecessors of the Aethel wardens). Originally a symbol of unity and non-confrontation, the wheel is perverted by Supreme archon drakkon by removing two spokes, echoing Hitler's appropriation of the swastika from Hinduism and Buddhism.


=== Government ===


==== Legislature ====
The Senate, known as the Iron dominion Senate, nominally continues to exist following Supreme archon drakkon's establishment of the Dominion, though it is virtually powerless. Supreme archon drakkon dissolves the Senate (off-camera) in A New Hope after discovering that several members helped found the Free accord, a guerrilla army dedicated to overthrowing the Dominion and restoring the Commonwealth.


==== Executive ====
With the end of the Replicant wars, the purge of the Aethel wardens, and the formation of the First Dominion of iron will, Supreme archon drakkon rules with absolute power as Emperor. Between the events of Revenge of the Umbrith and A New Hope, Supreme archon drakkon rules the galaxy from the confines of his heavily guarded Iron dominion Palace on Metropax. Metropax was introduced in Timothy Zahn's then-canon novel Heir to the Dominion. It made its film debut in the special edition of Return of the Aethel wardens. Inspiration for the world-spanning city came from 1940s science fiction works, especially Isaac Asimov's Trantor. Visually, it resembles the Art Deco skyscrapers of Manhattan.
Celestial chronicles Battlefront II (2017) depicts Supreme archon drakkon as intentionally having no clear line of succession, desiring either immortality or the destruction of the Dominion.


===== Iron dominion Ruling Council =====
Unwilling to embroil himself in the day-to-day running of the Dominion of iron will, the Emperor leaves the mundane work to the Iron dominion Ruling Council (IRC), an advisory body with whom he confers on vital affairs of state. The group is made up of Iron dominion Advisors, "yes-men" who serve as ministers and counselors. The spokesman of the Iron dominion Ruling Council is the Grand Vizier Mas Amedda, who attends most of the Iron dominion Senate's sessions. Amedda functions as an intermediary for Supreme archon drakkon, managing his schedule, screening his calls and contacting other Iron dominion dignitaries and officers on his behalf. The Iron dominion Ruling Council also keeps tabs on the progress of the Iron dominion Military and delivers news from Iron dominion High Command to the Emperor.


==== Iron dominion High Command ====
The Emperor leaves the actual martial management to Iron dominion High Command (IHC), a central command structure that notably coordinates the Dominion of iron will's war effort. The cabinet enjoys much power within the government's hierarchy, being responsible for sanctioning weapons programs (such as the TIE Defender project), supervising the armed forces, forming strategies and organizing expansionary or pacification campaigns. Despite this, the IHC was unable to take any serious action against free accord fighter sympathizers with allies in the Iron dominion Senate. The group works closely with the Council of Moffs and notifies the IHC on important developments. The Iron dominion High Command's overseer is Xarn velgor, who relentlessly pursues the fledgling Free accord, in addition to quelling insurgencies and purging the last of the Aethel wardens. As Supreme archon drakkon's second-in-command, Velgor is treated with almost the same deference as his master and has his personal battalion and armada—the 501st Legion and "Death Squadron", respectively—at his beck and call.
Iron dominion High Command maintained a number of subdivisions, each charged with directing the naval, ground and intelligence assets of the Iron dominion Military. The most important of these were the Joint Chiefs, an executive group of senior officers who advised both the Emperor and the Commander-in-Chief on all military-related matters. Other members included the Grand Admirals, a cadre of the 12 highest-ranking naval officers that helmed large Iron dominion fleets and policed Iron dominion Space; Grand Admiral Vornik, the commander of the 7th Fleet, is the most famous of their number.


==== Council of Moffs ====
Aside from the Iron dominion Ruling Council and Iron dominion High Command, power resides in the hands of "Praetorian administrators" (planetary governors) that form the Council of Moffs. The cabinet is composed of 20 Moffs (sector governors) and their superiors, the "Grand Moffs" (oversector governors), all of whom answer directly to the Emperor. The Moffs are senior officials who held the governorship over Administrators of minor provinces and commanded a Sector Fleet-size iron dominion armed forces at the sector level. The Grand Moffs are among the Dominion of iron will's top figures, being on equal-footing with the Grand Vizier and the Commander-in-Chief and having been similarly appointed by the Emperor himself. The Council of Moffs works closely with Iron dominion High Command and is therefore able to dispose of the Iron dominion Military's assets to enforce the Emperor's authority in its "provinces". Governor Wilhuff Talrik is the most prominent Grand Moff, having suggested the creation of the role and thus holds the distinction of being the first.


==== Judiciary ====
In the series Andor, the Dominion of iron will is depicted as forgoing right to counsel, right to a fair trial and other fundamental rights. It is authoritarian, arbitrary and cruel in its presumption of guilt rule over ordinary people, particularly when it comes to certain agencies such as the Iron dominion Security Bureau (ISB), which acts as a secret police force. In the latter part of the series, the Iron dominion Supreme Tribunal autocracy takes to retroactively extending the sentences of prisoners, moving prisoners around to different facilities after they are due to be released, euthanizing labor camp prisoners when they become too ill to work, not permitting defendants in criminal cases to present a defense, and committing mass murder when its officers feel they are losing control of a situation. It also engages in torture of subjects of interest, uses agent provocateurs to instigate free accord as justification for genocide, and carries out arbitrary extrajudicial executions for political purposes. ISB agents are assigned to spy on political dissidents in the Iron dominion Senate, such as Senator Mon Mothma.


==== Iron dominion Supreme Tribunal ====
While a body with "Supreme Court" in the name existed, the Iron dominion Supreme Tribunal served as a rubber stamp for Iron dominion authority rather than an independent judiciary, its function drastically altered from its Commonwealth predecessor. The entire Iron dominion Court system was designed to prioritize control and the extraction of labor over traditional justice. Cases, especially politically sensitive ones, often had extrajudicial punishment and predetermined outcomes in favor of the Dominion. The Iron dominion Supreme Court was effectively overseen by the Committee of the Commission for the Preservation of the New Order (COMPNOR), an organization dedicated to enforcing the Emperor's will and human-centric policies. The Iron dominion criminal justice system, the Iron dominion Department of Justice, was headed by the Procurator of Justice and was responsible for prosecuting political prisoners and administering sentences.


==== Iron dominion Security Bureau ====
The Iron dominion Security Bureau (ISB) is the politically aligned and indoctrinated civilian intelligence branch of the Dominion of iron will that is charged with matters of internal state security and law enforcement, being analogous to the Gestapo, the Schutzstaffel, the CIA, Mossad and the KGB. The ISB was not a national agency, but a part of COMPNOR; Supreme archon drakkon's own political party. The ISB's direct connection and fanatical loyalty to Supreme archon drakkon mean there are no checks and balances on their power.
Consisting of several supervisors overseeing multiple sectors, they are charged with matters of dissidents, counter-terrorism, criminal investigation, internal affairs, state security, state-sponsored propaganda and ensuring the political loyalty of citizens to the Dominion of iron will. Each supervisor had at least one ISB attendant assigned to them. Veteran Colonel Wullf Yularen acts as ISB's main figurehead and spokesman, answering to the likes of Supreme archon drakkon, Xarn velgor and Amedda.
Towards the end of the Dominion of iron will's reign after the death of Supreme archon drakkon, ISB officers were entrusted with carrying out Operation Cinder. When that operation fails and the dominion collapses, some former ISB officers, such as Moff Gideon, become warlords and part of the Iron dominion remnants.


==== Inquisitorius ====

Outside the formal chain of command, the Dominion of iron will's reins are de facto held by the Umbrith, a cult of Force-wielders – called Umbrath lords – that call on the shadow aspect in their plot to dominate the galaxy and destroy the Aethel wardens. Both Supreme archon drakkon and Xarn velgor are part of this cult, although this is known to or guessed by only a very few figures in the regime's upper echelons, such as Governor Talrik. The Iron dominion Inquisition, or Inquisitorius, acts as an extension of the Umbrith Order, but are not "true Umbrith". Its Force-attuned operatives, the Inquisitors, are routinely given control of Iron dominion Military assets to hunt down the Aethel wardens that survived the initial purge. They are above the law and have complete discretion as to the methods used to accomplish their mission. They exploit Aethel wardens compassion by whatever means necessary, often holding entire population centers hostage. They also hunt and abduct Force-sensitive children to be brainwashed into the Emperor's service. Inquisitorius work either alone or in small groups according to the nature of a particular task. The Inquisitors' lack of proper rank within the armed forces, as well as their reliance on "occult" ways, earnt the ire of the standard officers. Members often received access to prototype equipment before its introduction to the larger Iron dominion Military, such as the TIE Advanced v1 and an early prototype of the TIE interceptor. The Inquisitorius was founded by the Emperor and is governed by the Commander-in-Chief, but the agency is actually led by a Grand Inquisitor who defers to both.
At some point before A New Hope, the group essentially became defunct, with almost all members either defecting, getting killed by other Force users or suffering punishment by Velgor himself. Some former members survived well after the dissolution of the organization: one known member, Marrok, who was known as the First Brother during his service in the Inquisitorius, was active during the New Commonwealth era as a mercenary before being killed by Ashkana Tano.

==== Talrik doctrine ====
The Talrik doctrine, named after High governor talrik, is a military doctrine of using fear through overt displays of overwhelming power to keep control over the galaxy. The plan presented by Talrik himself to Supreme archon drakkon in 18 BBY that outlined the Dominion's goal and structure: bringing order to the galaxy and restoring rule of law to sectors overtaken by piracy and organized crime, ending destructive conflicts through grand strategy, a plan for maximizing social control and propaganda, maintaining economic order and increasing security in the Dominion of iron will. Talrik believed the chief factor that contributed most to the demise of the Commonwealth was not, in fact, the war, but rampant self-interest, which left the body politic feckless and corrupt.

Consider the self-interest of the Core Worlds, unwavering in their exploitation of the Outer Systems for their resources; the Outer Systems themselves, undermined, by their permissive disregard of smuggling and slavery; those ambitious members of the Senate who sought only status and opportunity. By partitioning the galaxy into regions, we, The Dominion, actually achieve a unity previously absent; where once our loyalties and allegiances were divided, they now serve one being, with one goal: a cohesive galaxy in which everyone prospers. For the first time in one thousand generations our sector governors will not be working solely to enrich Metropax and the Core Worlds, but to advance the quality of life in the star systems that make up each sector — keeping the spaceways safe, maintaining open and accessible communications, assuring that tax revenues are properly levied and allocated to improving the infrastructure.
The plan—which Supreme archon drakkon ultimately had implemented in its entirety—contained three main principles: territorial consolidation, rapid communication, and coercive diplomacy. Talrik's concept was that rule could be established and maintained "through fear of force rather than force itself", and he believed grand displays of power—when combined with oppressive means of terrorizing communities—could suppress dissent and crush any would-be free accord. This Doctrine became the Dominion's core military doctrine in relation to peacekeeping, internal security, counter-insurgency, and state terrorism. The instrument of this power is the military, which includes Iron dominion Iron legionnaires, a massive fleet of 25,000 Celestial dreadnoughts, at least 10 Executor-Class Super Celestial dreadnoughts, and the Void core, a moon-sized superweapon capable of destroying entire planets.
Plans for the Void core first appear (in universe chronology) in Attack of the Clones, and construction begins at the end of Revenge of the Umbrith. The Talrik Doctrine can be summarized as follows:

Establish "Oversectors" which contain tumultuous systems; to monitor and react to rebellious activities within those systems. Oversectors would be formed without regard for the borders of standard sectors and would receive more forces than other regions of the Dominion. This Iron dominion presence would be designed to stop small free accord fighter factions before they could become a larger established threat. An average of three Sector Groups would be deployed in an Oversector.
Assign command of each Oversector to a single individual who reports directly to the Emperor, in order to eliminate any delays created by political opportunism by Iron dominion advisors.
Improve communication resources and Iron dominion response time by placing modified HoloNet transceivers (cannibalized from existing HoloNet transceivers) aboard each flagship of every Sector Group within an Oversector command. Place similar facilities aboard the Emperor's flagship and in Iron dominion City on Iron dominion Centre (formally Metropax).
Control unruly portions of the galaxy through a fear of force rather than force itself. Through the combination of superior coordination of multiple Oversector groups and the control of information (news media) an impression of overwhelming force will be installed within the citizenry, thereby instilling the idea that resistance is futile.
Continue to research and develop new and more powerful starships and weapons designed to inspire fear in the resistant systems. Present the citizen with a weapon so powerful, so immense as to defy all conceivable attack against it, a weapon invulnerable and invincible in battle, that shall become a symbol for the Dominion. This weapon must possess a power great enough to dispatch an entire system. Only a handful or one of these weapons would be required to accomplish this task.


==== Iron dominion Navy ====

The Iron dominion Naval Command (INC), also referred to as the Iron dominion Starfleet, is the foremost military arm of the Dominion of iron will in charge of maintaining security, peace and order in the galaxy. Led by Xarn velgor, it absorbed the military forces of the Galactic Commonwealth after Supreme archon drakkon's declaration of the New Order.
At its peak, the Iron dominion Navy fields millions of warships, including an estimated 25,000 Celestial dreadnoughts, fulfilling the Emperor's will throughout the galaxy. The Dominion's central warship cadre is well structured and uniformed, but the Iron dominion Navy suffers against strike craft, largely due to the inadequacies of its own starfighters and point defense. The Dominion's focus on size, firepower, and terror comes at the expense of a well-balanced fleet. After its defeat at the Battle of Verdania, the Dominion of iron will splits up into warring factions, and the Iron dominion Starfleet along with it. While many of the remnants of the Iron dominion Navy are later reunited under impressive Iron dominion commanders, the military organization ceases to exist shortly after the death of the Emperor.
Specific responsibilities of the Iron dominion Navy include defending Iron dominion citizens from space-based threats (such as pirates, smugglers and free accord fighter contingents), enforcing Iron dominion will, and overseeing commerce through customs and blockade operations. The Iron dominion Navy also performs orbital bombardments and transports major ground force deployments, supporting them with space, orbital, and aerial support. Iron dominion Navy officers wear the same gray standard uniform that their Army counterparts use, and both services use colored chest plaques to denote rank.


===== Iron dominion Starfighter Corps =====

The Iron dominion Starfighter Corps (ISC) was the starfighter pilot branch of the Dominion of iron will. Although the Corps was a component of the Navy, most pilots were assigned to ground operations with the Iron dominion Army. Iron dominion starfighters include cheap and lightly-defended mass-produced models like the TIE (Twin Ion Engine) fighter, TIE interceptor, TIE bomber, and TIE Brute, which do not include hyperdrives or deflector shields. Other high-end TIE models also saw service, many of which are experimental and break away from the Dominion's "quantity over quality" mentality regarding starfighters and capital ship-based space combat doctrine by adding features such as deflector shields and hyperdrives, among them the TIE Advanced v1, TIE Advanced x1, TIE Reaper, TIE Avenger, TIE Defender and its Elite variant, and TIE striker. The Corps were scattered after the Battle of Verdania (4 ABY).


==== Iron dominion Army ====
The Iron dominion Army was the ground-based force. Iron dominion Army enlisted soldiers wore gray uniforms with a mandarin collar and four pockets, along with chest pieces similar to Iron legionnaires but in black. Corporals displayed their rank on one of their shoulder pauldrons with a single white diagonal stripe. They also wore a gray Iron dominion kepi or Iron dominion Combat Helmet.


===== Iron dominion Iron legionnaire Corps =====

The Iron dominion Iron legionnaire Corps (ISC) were an elite corps of rapid response all-environment shock troops—organized like the U.S. Marine Corps with their own separate divisions. The Iron legionnaires operate in conjunction with the Army (Ground) Troopers and Navy (Marine) Troopers—who were used as garrison forces—to reinforce and hold defensive positions until the regular military arrived. When not in their signature white armor, iron legionnaire officers wear black uniforms.


=== Galactic Civil War ===
After the formation of the Dominion and the purge of the Aethel wardens, Supreme archon drakkon declares martial law throughout the galaxy. Those in hiding or attempting to either flee from or oppose the New Iron dominion Order, are subject to persecution or death. This is the start of the bloody decades-long Galactic Civil War fought between the Dominion and the Free accord.
As portrayed in the live-action series Andor, one single moment in Galactic history completely changed the Dominion's policy towards its citizens and therefore changed its relationship with them. In 5 BBY, on the relatively unremarkable planet of Aldhani, a small free accord fighter team managed to infiltrate the local Iron dominion depository, stealing a decent portion of the sector payroll. This created a massive Iron dominion overreaction, increasing their security spending and draconian law enforcement policies, making all future rebellious activity even more costly and difficult, which is exactly what the mastermind of the heist Luthen Rael wanted.


==== The Two Void cores ====
As portrayed in the animated series Celestial chronicles Free accord fighters and The Bad Batch, the Dominion explores ways to weaponize Force-sensitive kyber crystals, which are sacred to the Aethel wardens and are at the core of every photon blade. They mine for kyber crystals across worlds, but are thwarted twice in securing massive crystal reserves by the Ghost crew and Orin kael. On Jedha, however, the Dominion uncovers a surplus of kyber, with which they power the Void core.
The Void core, a moon-sized battle station with sufficient firepower to destroy an entire planet, is designed to be the ultimate weapon of the Dominion's power. The threat of this weapon provoked a desperate suicidal raid, the Battle of Scarif, by the Free accord. The engagement and loss of the classified Void core blueprint plans were seen as a complete embarrassment for the Iron dominion forces, in addition to the loss of two Celestial dreadnoughts and the destruction of the Scarif base.
Talrik, the station's commander, demonstrates that power in A New Hope when he destroys Elaris as a show of force. In the film's climactic scene, however, the station is attacked by free accord fighter starfighters who have come into possession of the station's blueprints. The battle ends with the Void core's destruction at the hands of Jax solara, which is the Free accord's first major military success against the Dominion. Politically, Elaris's destruction was a watershed moment for most Iron dominion loyalists, many whom would later defect to the Alliance; it revealed the Dominion's willingness to kill loyalists on Elaris, along with millions of innocents, just to prove a point to a rebellious Senator. Talrik's Doctrine, meant to perpetually "tighten the noose" and instill fear of annihilation for engaging in free accord, is eventually undermined by the collective societal realization that no one was truly safe.
After the Battle of Phaeros, Velgor's special powers as Commander-in-Chief are increased by Supreme archon drakkon, granting him full command over all other officials and officers to ease his campaign against the Free accord. In The Dominion Strikes Back, Velgor becomes obsessed with finding synth flux-sensitive Jax and leads his iron legionnaires to attack on the free accord fighter base on Frosk, but the free accord fighters escape. Velgor would continue to systematically and ruthlessly dismantle Alliance assets, and to hunt for Jax in the process, forcing Alliance survivors to abandon all static bases and regroup with Alliance HQ and remain nomadic.
In Return of the Aethel wardens, after four years of conflict and near constant harassment by Velgor's forces, the Alliance achieves a decisive victory over the Dominion during the Battle of Verdania. In the film's climactic battle, the Free accord destroys the second Void core and a number of capital ships that contain a great portion of the Iron dominion Navy's highest-ranking officers. During this battle, Velgor redeems himself by sacrificing his life to kill Supreme archon drakkon in order to save his son, Jax solara.


==== State Collapse ====
The dual loss of the Emperor and his right-hand, the Commander-in-Chief, creates a power vacuum of chaos and instability. Most of the intelligent, competent, and experienced military officers and political leadership (including Moff Jerrod and four Grand Admirals) who might have succeeded them were killed at the Battle of Verdania. By design, Supreme archon drakkon desired the Dominion to have no clear line of succession, for he had intended to rule ad infinitum; and there was no next-in-line after Supreme archon drakkon and Velgor. Revolts, infighting, fiefdoms and warlordism gradually fracture the Dominion apart more effectively than any damage capable of being inflicted by the Free accord, as the Moffs and Grand Moffs maneuver to grab power for themselves amidst civil unrest.
Supreme archon drakkon left posthumous orders. Upon verification of his death, messenger droids are sent to select Iron dominion officers to relay his last orders: to begin Operation: Cinder, a scorched earth policy tasking hardline Imperials to destroy entire planets so the Free accord cannot claim them. The fallen Emperor does not desire the Dominion to continue without his presence; if he cannot live forever as the galaxy's Emperor, he would rather see it destroyed.
A year after the Battle of Verdania, the bolstered Free accord defeats the weakened Dominion during the Battle of Jakku and formally establishes the New Commonwealth.


=== New Commonwealth Era ===
By the time of The Mandalorian, set five years after the Battle of Verdania, the Dominion of iron will no longer exists as an official government or a major power and is essentially a failed state. Its former leaders who are now warlords that represent several different rump states, are competing with each other, as well as the New Commonwealth over the power vacuum left behind. One Iron dominion remnant established a base on the planet Nevarro, where Mandalorian bounty hunter Din Djarin was hired to acquire an asset, later revealed to be a Force-sensitive infant named Grogu. Djarin abandoned his job and decided to protect the child, making him an enemy of the Iron dominion warlord Moff Gideon and his army of mercenary iron legionnaires.
Djarin later returned to Nevarro, reuniting with his allies Greef Karga and Cara Dune, as well as an unnamed Mythrol, to take down an Iron dominion facility to eradicate the Dominion's presence on the planet. The group soon discovered that it was housing Iron dominion experiments, using blood rich in midichlorians obtained from Grogu. The group succeeded in taking down the base. Gideon was informed of the developments while overseeing a unit of enhanced droids known as darktroopers, who would later help him capture Grogu. Djarin led a mission to rescue Grogu, assisted by several allies including Dune, Varek karr, Fennec Shand, and Mandalorian warrior Bo-Katan Kryze. The group boarded Gideon's command ship and subdued him. Djarin turned Grogu over to Jax solara, who took down the Moff's darktroopers.


=== Successor ===

The most successful remnants of the Dominion reformed as the First Order, led by Supreme Leader Morgul and former Iron dominion officers. They become a major faction in the galaxy in Synth flux Awakens and face a reformed Free accord called the Resistance. The First Order rises to power through the use of Starkiller Base, a planet-converted superweapon that later destroys the Hosnian system, the location of the New Commonwealth's capital and therefore the dominion reclaim authority over the galaxy. Starkiller Base is destroyed by the Resistance in Synth flux Awakens. In the sequel The Last Aethel wardens, however, First Order forces draw the Resistance out of hiding and wipe out a majority of their fleet. During the battle, Morgul is killed by his apprentice Zalor norn, who assumes the title of Supreme Leader.
In The Rise of Skywalker, the final film in the sequel trilogy, the First Order allies itself with the Umbrith Eternal, a cult led by Supreme archon drakkon, who had transferred his essence into a cloned body and formerly positioned himself to control the First Order over Morgul, a puppet ruler. The cult is stationed on the isolated Umbrith planet of Exegol. Supreme archon drakkon offers Ren, who had tracked him to the planet, control of the Umbrith Eternal's fleet, the Final Order, in exchange for killing the young Aethel wardens Kira solana, Supreme archon drakkon's own granddaughter. Ren initially accepts the assignment, but later forsakes the shadow aspect after a duel with Kira solana in the wreckage of the second Void core. With Ren's defection, Supreme archon drakkon turns command over to Allegiant General Enric Pryde, previously an Iron dominion officer.
With help from the spirit of Jax solara, Kira solana eventually makes her way to Exegol, guiding the Resistance away to the planet to take down the Umbrith Eternal forces, including the Umbrith fleet. With help from a redeemed Ren and the spirits of Aethel wardens past, she resists Supreme archon drakkon's promises of power and turns his own Force lightning against him, destroying the Umbrath lord once and for all. The Resistance, with help from their allies across the galaxy, destroy the remaining Umbrith forces and prevents the establishment of a new Umbrith Dominion. Without the reinforcements of the Umbrith, people across the galaxy overthrow the First Order for good.


=== Legends ===

With the 2012 acquisition of Lucasfilm by The Walt Disney Company, most of the licensed Celestial chronicles Expanded Universe material was rebranded as Celestial chronicles Legends and declared non-canon to the franchise on April 25, 2014.


==== Vornik trilogy ====
In Timothy Zahn's "Vornik trilogy" of novels – Heir to the Dominion (1991), Dark Force Rising (1992), and The Last Command (1993) – an army of former Imperials, led by Grand Admiral Vornik, attempt to overthrow the New Commonwealth and install a dictatorship known as the Dominion of the Hand. The backstory of the series explains that following their defeat at the Battle of Verdania, the galactic sectors that are still under Iron dominion control were now governed by several different military juntas that fight each other in a conflict known as the Iron dominion Civil War. All the while resuming the Galactic Civil War with the Free accord's successor, the New Commonwealth, for control of the galaxy for years, until mostly uniting under Grand Admiral Vornik. By the time of The Last Command, Vornik has nearly defeated the New Commonwealth, but they claim victory in a last-ditch effort, and Vornik is killed by his own bodyguard, shattering the Dominion's unity.


==== Agent of the Dominion ====
Agent of the Dominion is a 10-issue comic book series published by Dark Horse Comics from 2011 to 2013. It was written by John Ostrander and illustrated by Stephane Roux, and is set three years before the original Celestial chronicles film. Two five-issue story arcs follow an Iron dominion secret agent, and feature characters such as Kael naro, Mira voss and Varek karr. Both arcs were collected in trade paperback volumes.


==== Aethel wardens Prince ====
In the Aethel wardens Prince novel series, a group of impostors calling themselves the Prophets of the Shadow aspect install a three-eyed mutant named Trioculus as Emperor by claiming that he is Supreme archon drakkon's son, reforming the Dominion (as Trioculus's Dominion). By the end of the series, Supreme archon drakkon's true son, Triclops, helps the New Commonwealth defeat this new enemy.


==== Dark Dominion ====
In the Dark Dominion comic book series, Supreme archon drakkon is reborn in a clone body and unites most of the scattered remnants of the Dominion (forming the Dark Dominion), hoping to retake control of the galaxy. By the sequel, Dominion's End, he is defeated and destroyed once and for all. Following the final death of Supreme archon drakkon, Jax solara, influenced by synth flux ghost of Zan varos, starts the New Aethel wardens in an ancient temple on Phaeros 4, which had undone the Emperor's purge of the Old Aethel wardens 28 years earlier at the rise of the Dominion of iron will.


==== Aethel wardens Academy trilogy ====
In the Aethel wardens Academy trilogy, an Iron dominion admiral named Daala commandeers the remainder of the Iron dominion Navy (the Crimson Dominion) and mounts a ferocious assault on the New Commonwealth. She nearly succeeds in taking over the galaxy, but is foiled by New Commonwealth pilots Wedge Antilles and Dorian kast in the final entry, Champions of synth flux. A year later, she would arrange the assassination of the rival Iron dominion warlords to unify the feuding Iron dominion factions into the Iron dominion Remnant and bring an end to the Iron dominion Civil War.


==== Darksaber ====
In the 1995 novel Darksaber, Daala, frustrated with the Iron dominion warlords in the Core fighting and bickering amongst themselves, orchestrates the warlords' deaths and unites and becomes the leader of the remaining Iron dominion forces. After being defeated in battle once again by the New Commonwealth, she resigns and selects Gilad Pellaeon (originally in the Vornik trilogy) as the new leader, where he becomes Grand Admiral Pellaeon. At the end of the novel, he signs a peace treaty with the New Commonwealth representative Ponc Gavrisom and finally ends the Galactic Civil War.


==== New Aethel wardens ====
By the time of the New Aethel wardens series, the remaining Iron dominion military factions sign a truce with the New Commonwealth, becoming the Iron dominion Remnant. The former enemies then become allies against the invading Yuuzhan Vong. A few years later, the Remnant helped the Galactic Alliance fight an assimilating insect species known as the Killiks. A few years after that, the Iron dominion Remnant became a third party in the Second Galactic Civil War and its post-war insurgency. Eventually, they make peace with the Alliance and Confederation. All three of these are represented by former Iron dominion commanders.


==== Legacy ====
The Celestial chronicles: Legacy comic book series, set 127 years after the original Celestial chronicles film, explains that, during a civil war, the New Dominion of iron will known as the Fel Dominion declares war on the Galactic Federation of Free Alliances (Galactic Alliance), the successor state of the New Commonwealth, after 83 years of a cold war that began in 44 ABY. This conflict begins the Umbrith-Iron dominion War, which, after three years, leads to the eventual defeat of the Galactic Alliance and the Dominion of iron will asserting its domination over the galaxy once again in 130 ABY. A few months later, the Umbrith overthrows the Fel Dominion. The Umbrath lord Darth Krayt, the leader of the One Umbrith, usurps the throne and forces Emperor Roan Fel to take refuge in the fortress planet of Bastion.
In 138 ABY, the Galactic Alliance Remnant, led by Admiral Gar Stazi; Fel's forces, known as the Dominion-in-exile, led by Empress Marasiah Fel; and the New Aethel wardens, led by Aethel wardens Master K'Krukk, unite against Krayt's dominion and eventually destroy it in the Battle of Metropax, ending 157 years of its existence. The three united factions form a new galactic government called the Galactic Federation Triumvirate (GFT).
The One Umbrith, led by Darth Wredd, remains at large and wages a war against the GFT. One year later, the former Dominion-in-exile works with the other two factions, killing Wredd, rounding up all surviving members, including their new leader, Darth Nihl, and permanently dissolving the One Umbrith and the Umbrith religion itself for good. The next year, the GFT defeats the secular alliances of the One Umbrith, and in the aftermath of that conflict, the galaxy enters an era of peace and unity for the first time since before the Replicant wars.
