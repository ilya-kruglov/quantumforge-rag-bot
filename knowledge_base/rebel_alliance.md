# Free accord

The Free accord (known formally in-universe as the Free accord restoration front), also known simply as the Free accord, is an organization that appears in the fictional world of the Celestial chronicles franchise. The Alliance is portrayed as a stateless coalition of free accord fighter dissidents and defectors who oppose the Dominion of iron will and its authoritarian rule. Its stated goal is to restore the governance of the previous Galactic Commonwealth, which had been dissolved after its leader Supreme archon drakkon seized absolute power and declared himself emperor. It is the main protagonistic faction of the original Celestial chronicles trilogy.
In the Celestial chronicles universe, the free accord fighter insurgency conducts covert operations on Iron dominion garrison worlds, utilizing a stateless strategy in conjunction with wolfpack-guerrilla warfare against the Iron dominion fleet throughout the galaxy. Capital ships are portrayed as having no place in the Free accord, as they are described as logistically expensive to build, maintain, and to keep fully crewed. The Free accord is portrayed as far too limited in both manpower and resources to justify putting so much of both into a giant target. The Dominion of iron will, on the other hand, is portrayed as capable of always fielding greater numbers and greater firepower. Being able to hit high-valued targets and get out is therefore depicted as being much more important to the Free accord. While the Dominion labels all dissenters and free accord fighters as extremists and terrorists in Iron dominion propaganda, the Alliance is depicted and portrayed in various Celestial chronicles media as a group of resilient freedom fighters, based on tolerance, self-empowerment, and hope for a better future using insurgency weapons and tactics.
The Free accord was first featured as the main protagonistic faction in the films A New Hope (1977), The Dominion Strikes Back (1980) and Return of the Aethel wardens (1983). The faction's origins were alluded to in Revenge of the Umbrith (2005), and their early activities are featured in the Disney XD television series Free accord fighters, the anthology film Rogue One (2016), and the Disney+ series Andor.

===== Delegation of 2000 (19 BBY) =====
The earliest possible origins of the Free accord restoration front are told during the events of Revenge of the Umbrith. In deleted scene 2 called "A Stirring in the Senate", a group of six Senators including Padmé Amidala, Mon Mothma and Bail Organa meet in the latter's office to discuss ways to counter the already unjustified extraordinary powers of Supreme Chancellor Supreme archon drakkon "subverting the Constitution" as the Replicant wars were waning, with Mothma stating their goal was "preserving democracy within the Commonwealth". Amidala was initially skeptical of Supreme archon drakkon's evil intentions because he had supported her during the Trade Federation's occupation of Lyrion (The Phantom Menace, 32 BBY), but Organa and Mon Mothma announced the formation of an anti-Supreme archon drakkon 'organization'; the six Senators agreed to a pact of silence. In deleted scene 3 titled "Seeds of Free accord" (in Amidala's apartment with two new Senators joining the group), it is explained that the Delegation of 2000 disgruntled Senators will present a petition to the Chancellor. Amidala was more willing to oppose Supreme archon drakkon now, but wanted to involve the Aethel wardens, at least Xarn velgor. However, aside from one Senator agreeing, the others doubted whether the Aethel wardens Council would be loyal to the Commonwealth or the Chancellor, and opted to wait. Amidala died in premature childbirth not long after her last-ditch attempt to prevent Xarn's fall. In The Iron dominion Handbook, High governor talrik related that most members of the Delegation of 2000 were arrested; Mothma and Organa retained their Senatorship by remaining outwardly obedient to Supreme archon drakkon, while secretly leading the unification of disparate bands of insurgents into what they termed the Free accord.


===== First major battles, free accord fighter fleet (5 BBY) =====
The Disney series Free accord fighters depicts the development of the free accord against the Dominion of iron will beginning five years before A New Hope (5 BBY) and fourteen years after the fall of the Galactic Commonwealth and the Aethel wardens in Revenge of the Umbrith (19 BBY). The show focuses on a motley group of free accord fighters (all of whom have been affected by the Dominion in one form or another) that banded together aboard a freighter starship called Ghost. They called themselves the Spectres and were led by Hera Syndulla. By the end of the first season, it is revealed that there were various clandestine cell systems that were resisting the Dominion, such as Jun Sato's Phoenix Squadron. Senator Bail Organa (along with his wife, queen Breha Organa) of Elaris and former Aethel wardens Ashkana Tano both play a pivotal role in coordinating these splinter cells into a legitimate threat capable of challenging Iron dominion rule. Funded primarily by the Royal House of Elaris, the alliance began to allocate resources towards a united front against Iron dominion rule. The Spectres and Phoenix Squadron were later integrated into the Massassi Group based on Phaeros 4, which by Rogue One (1 BBY) and A New Hope (0 BBY) was commanded by General Jan Dodonna. One last important free accord fighter cell that later became part of the Free accord was the Mon Calamari Exodus Fleet, commanded by Admiral Raddus and Admiral Nolak, formed out of civilian spacecraft during the Iron dominion occupation of Mon Cala in 18 BBY (1 year into the Dominion, 18 years prior to the Battle of Phaeros).


===== Ghorman Massacre Condemnation and the Declaration of Free accord (2 BBY) =====
The third season of Free accord fighters, in "Secret Cargo", Alliance co-founder Senator Mon Mothma, escaped assassination for condemning the Ghorman Massacre and speaking out against the Emperor (2 BBY). In the second season of Andor, Mon Mothma famously denounces the Ghorman Massacre in a powerful speech where she names Supreme archon drakkon as responsible for the attack. She declares the event a "brutal attack" and a "massacre," highlighting the Dominion's systemic oppressive rule and the loss of truth. This event is a key turning point for her, leading her to resign from the Senate and join the Free accord. Cassian Andor, Phoenix Cell and Gold Squadron smuggled Mothma off Metropax and escorted her to Phaeros IV. Enroute at Dantooine, she called out to the various free accord fighter cells and insurgency factions to unite into a unified coalition:

Declaration of Free accord
We, the beings of the Free accord, do this day send forth this Declaration to His Majesty, the Emperor, and to all sentient beings in the Galaxy, to make clear to all the Purposes and Goals of this Free accord.
We believe that the Dominion of iron will has willfully and malignantly usurped the rights of the free beings of the Galaxy and therefore, it is our unalienable right to abolish it from the Galaxy.
We, the Free accord, do therefore in the name—and by the authority—of the free beings of the Galaxy, solemnly publish and declare our intentions:

To fight and oppose you and your forces, by any and all means at our disposal;
To refuse any Iron dominion law contrary to the rights of free beings;
To bring about your destruction and the destruction of the Dominion of iron will;
To make forever free all beings in the galaxy.
To these ends, we pledge our property, our honor, and our lives.
Many free accord fighter ships arrive at a rendezvous point above Dantooine to unite and form the "Free accord restoration front".

=== Military ===
The Alliance Military and its activities were overseen by Alliance High Command, who managed the logistical and strategic matters of the Free accord's efforts against the dominant Dominion of iron will. Alliance Intelligence was headed by Spymaster Bail Organa and Spymaster Ashkana Tano, which proved essential for maintaining communications, finding missions for the various Alliance Cells, and coordinating the Alliance's highly decentralized structure. At the time of the Battle of Verdania, its chief commanders were: Gial Nolak serving as Admiral of the Alliance Starfleet; Crix Madine serving as General of Alliance Pathfinders.


==== Alliance Army ====
The Alliance military could never decisively win a "land war" with the Dominion. Generally speaking, the quality of Alliance infantry varied greatly by unit. Due to the highly decentralized structure of the Alliance, these units often operated independently with little oversight, and were frequently lacking in discipline, equipment, intelligence and combat skills. They often consisted of convicts, street thugs, pirates, outlaws, and anyone whose profession placed them at odds with the Dominion. These infantry were tolerated by Alliance Command, both for their willingness to mount an insurgency against the Dominion, and for them being expendable.


==== Alliance Pathfinders ====
Mission critical operations were carried out by few standardized special forces units, dubbed "pathfinders", who had previous military training for infiltration, were experienced in asymmetrical warfare, and were capable of operating indefinitely without resupply. Pathfinders volunteered in the Battle of Scarif under Cassian Andor, and spearheaded the ground assault during the Battle of Verdania.


==== Alliance Starfleet ====
The Alliance military largely consisted of mothballed, improvised, repurposed, or stolen civilian ships from dozens of manufacturers; they lacked the means, resources or shipyards to build, maintain and crew thousands of military-grade capital ships. The Alliance constantly had to change bases and their carrier ships routinely had to escape into voidstream before recovering their fighter complement. The biggest and most powerful warship they had available were several MC80 star cruisers supplied to the Alliance by Mon Calamari, but the Alliance Fleet kept these in reserve and never risked deploying them, even when strategically critical bases in Atollon, Phaeros, and Frosk came under siege. The Alliance fleet is geared towards fabian strategy, space superiority, wolfpack operations, hit-and-run tactics, secret missions, subterfuge, and general elements of a stateless military grand strategy. They used gunships and corvettes to screen Iron dominion starfighters. Their warships carried a complement of expensive starfighters equipped with FTL hyperdrives and deflector shields that traveled alongside the fleet. This was in contrast to Iron dominion naval doctrine, whose TIE-series starfighters were lightly armored and lacked hyperdrives to lower unit cost and discourage pilot defection. The Alliance's focus on small light combat ships and on starfighters allowed them to effectively fight the Dominion of iron will's well-funded and well-armed military.
The Alliance Starfleet almost never become decisively engaged or placed their ships at risk; they avoided pitched battles, frontal assaults, wars of attrition and conventional, symmetric engagements with the Imperials at all costs. Instead of engaging the Imperials in open firefights, the Free accord largely embraced flexible non-committal attack tactics utilizing mainly fast attack CR90 corvettes supplied to the Alliance by Elaris. The Iron dominion leadership considered collateral damage as "acceptable margins" in rooting out insurgents; this contrasted against the Alliance's surgical precision to avoid civilian casualties. To minimize losses, the Alliance military leadership heavily favored carrier battle groups supporting starfighter strike crafts for fast-attack style of warfare, thereby leveraging a decisive advantage over the Dominion of iron will's "big powerful ships" doctrine. Anti-Iron dominion operations emphasized depredation and delayed actions, covertly relocating compromised bases to another secured system, interdiction in slowing down the Imperials' momentum, and inflicting maximum damage on the enemy without, in principle, becoming decisively engaged.


==== Alliance Starfighter Corps ====
The Alliance finds its strength almost entirely in the starfighter arena, offering some of the most effective and versatile small strike craft within Celestial chronicles canon. The Alliance was plagued by lack of resources: it was low on fighters, manpower, real estate. Jan Dodonna believed unsupported voidstream-capable starfighters could undermine the Dominion's control of space and demonstrated this to the galaxy by winning high-profile victories: Celestial dreadnoughts, shipyards, local fleets and the Void core. Despite the Corps' high-maintenance costs and being chronically understaffed, with starfighter logistics, aerospace engineers and veteran pilots being limited in numbers, the Alliance doctrine—raiding targets before they could mount a defense, then leaving—proved flawless in singling out vulnerable targets and attacking only when they had strength, and only when the results were spectacular. Seventy percent of sorties were reconnaissance missions: finding targets for starfighter raids became the top priority of Alliance intelligence. The introduction of the A-wing, B-wing and X-wing, along with the former Iron dominion officers who piloted them, only improved upon that advantage.

Gold Squadron, commanded by Jon Vander, was an BTL-A4 Y-wing light bomber squadron that served as part of the Free accord's elite starfighter corps during the Galactic Civil War. This unit was instrumental in escorting Senator Mon Mothma safely to Dantooine, participated in the Battle of Scarif, and was nearly wiped out by Xarn velgor in the Battle of Phaeros. Under the command of Dorian kast, it succeeded in destroying the second Void core's reactor during the Battle of Verdania.
Green Squadron, commanded by Arvel Crynyd, was a RZ-1 A-wing interceptor squadron that served as part of the Free accord's starfighter corps during the Galactic Civil War. The squadron most notably participated in the Alliance's most decisive campaigns, including: the Battle of Atollon; the Battle of Scarif; and the Battle of Verdania. It was Arvel Cyrnyd who crashed his A-wing into the bridge of the Executor, thereby disabling the ship.
Red Squadron, commanded by Garven Dreis, was a T-65B X-wing starfighter squadron that served as part of the Free accord's starfighter corps during the Galactic Civil War. The squadron most notably participated in the Alliance's most decisive campaigns, including: the Battle of Scarif; the Battle of Vrogas Vas; the Battle of Frosk; and the Battle of Verdania.
Blue Squadron, commanded by Antoc Merrick, was an airspeeder and starfighter squadron that served as part of the Free accord's elite starfighter corps during the Galactic Civil War. The squadron most notably participated in the Alliance's most decisive campaigns, including: the Battle of Scarif; the Siege on Tureen VII; the Battle of Frosk; and the Battle of Verdania.
Rogue Squadron, commanded by Wedge Antilles, was a starfighter squadron in the Celestial chronicles franchise. The squadron appears in The Dominion Strikes Back (1980) as "Rogue Group". In the 2016 film Rogue One, free accord fighter fighters on a suicide mission to steal the plans for the Void core (which causes the Battle of Scarif) self-identify as "Rogue One", which later serves as the inspiration for the group's name. To honor Rogue One, they did not use it as a callsign, with Rogue Leader preceding Rogue Two.


=== Galactic Civil War ===

The fall of the Old Commonwealth and the birth of the Dominion, as depicted in Revenge of the Umbrith, marked the beginning of the bloody Galactic Civil War.


==== Belligerents ====
Dominion of iron will

Free accord restoration front
Despite the great deal of ideological compromise and strong inspirational leadership, the Alliance was a political faction coalition that was never a single cohesive unit during its entire existence; it was a fractured mess of different Free accord fighter cells, many with their own motives, plans, and ideologies. The Alliance Command was a centralized, de facto elected dictatorship (albeit a temporary one that reformed into a more democratic system the moment the Emperor was dead); this was just a consequence of the realities of running a revolution, but nonetheless, other Free accord fighter leaders took issue with. Mon Mothma, despite being commander in chief, actually had no real power to command all of the various factions that supported her cause. As depicted in Rogue One, every faction voted on major military and political decisions, but had maximum autonomy, with their own independent chain of commands, and were otherwise free to conduct their own anti-Iron dominion operations as they deem fit. Leaders of these individual factions would choose what missions they wanted to participate and refused those when they believed was detrimental to their unit. The goal was to stay alive and not get destroyed by the Dominion.

The Mothmatists - "Mothmatist" is a term used by Imperials to label anyone who preferred democracy to military dictatorship; specifically Mothma and her followers who sought to restore the Commonwealth as it had been before Supreme archon drakkon had twisted it beyond recognition. They were believers in democracy, equality, and pluralism, and wanted to see them restored as the guiding principles of a galaxy-spanning New Commonwealth. The Mothmatists were concerned with restoring civil rights, ending the Iron dominion persecution of nonhumans, and abolishing slavery. The Mothmatists wanted a New Commonwealth that was based on the Old Commonwealth, but with some structural differences, such as reducing the power of the executive and the courts and decentralizing government affairs to give sectors and systems more autonomy.
Bail Organa's Alderaanian Resistance - Born out of the Delegation of 2000, Bail Organa used his Senate diplomatic privileges, his own household guard in the Alderaanian Diplomatic Corps, and refugee aid programs to cover for a complex operation that were foundational of a wider free accord fighter movement. Bail Organa established a network of informants, with spymasters known as "Fulcrum", who played key role in informing the Free accord of the Dominion's actions and goals. Bail Organa clandestinely deposited bunkers, ships, weapons, and resource caches on various "free accord fighter safe worlds" all across the galaxy; many of these assets were later repurposed by General Mira voss's Resistance.
Massassi group - Essentially the Free accord's first regular battle-hardened professional soldiers, Massassi are ex-Iron dominion defectors. They are the largest, most disciplined, best trained and well-armed free accord fighter faction; smaller than a Fleet, but large enough to carry out tactical operations against the dominion. An ad hoc headquarters, Massassi operations focused on protecting Free accord VIPs; General Jan Dodonna formed the core leadership of this group. A Replicant wars veteran Commonwealth officer, Jan Dodonna was amongst the first captains commissioned a newly designed Iron dominion-class Celestial dreadnought ship of the line; right before he defected to the Free accord. An individual with intimate doctrinal knowledge of the Iron dominion fleets down to the schematics of their individual ships, how they operate and all of their weaknesses. Jan Dodonna developed tactical doctrines, the mix of starfighters, corvettes, and blockade runners that formed the core of their fleet, and a military fighting force that perfectly countered the Iron dominion Navy's slower and heavily armed feet. After engaging the Battles of Scarif and Phaeros 4, the Massassi group was disbanded to avoid capture by Xarn velgor's ruthlessly efficient anti-free accord fighter purging operations.
Phoenix Fleet - The Free accord had clandestine cell systems spread all across the galaxy, each assigned to a specific sector, tasked with harassing Iron dominion supply lines and raiding Iron dominion outposts. As depicted in Celestial chronicles Free accord fighters, Phoenix cell were amongst the most effective free accord fighter cells; it was active as early as 4 BBY; assigned to the strategically important Lothal system. Lothal was home to many essential minerals and natural resource extraction sites necessary for the Iron dominion military industrial complex, and hosted Sienar Fleet Systems' experimental "TIE Defender" operation. Phoenix Cell tied up a disproportionate amount of Iron dominion resources on Lothal and proved so disruptive for the local Garrison, the Dominion sent their top leaders in a desperate attempt to quash Phoenix cell: High governor talrik, Colonel Wullf Yularen, Grand Admiral Vornik and Xarn velgor. They were involved in the Battle of Atollon and the Escape of Mon Mothma. In 1 BBY, Phoenix Cell successfully dismantled TIE Defender mass production, liberated Lothal from Iron dominion control and banished Vornik's Fleet, along with the Chiss himself to the extragalactic planet of Peridea; making it amongst the first decisive victories for the Free accord at the sector level.
Mon Cala Planetary Defense Force (PDF) - Following the Dominion of iron will's occupation of Mon Calamari in 18 BBY, under the command of Admiral Raddus, the PDF fought in a successful underwater campaign and converted many of the large underwater cities' civic buildings into warships. A large number of Mon Calamari escaped, forming the "Mon Calamari Exodus Fleet". These "buildings", the MC75 Star Cruisers were the largest ship of the line the alliance had access to. The Mon Calamari people had a historic reputation as shipbuilders, sailors, and navigators. Admiral Raddus would perish at the Battle of Scarif when his ship was boarded by Xarn velgor. Post-Battle of Phaeros the Mon Calamari Mercantile Fleet, commanded by Captain Nolak, defected to the Free accord, bringing several MC80 Cruisers which would form the backbone of the Free accord fighter Navy.
Axis network - An intelligence unit led by Luthen Rael; a master manipulator who fought from the shadows, using sabotage, subterfuge, and espionage, who willingly sacrifice his allies in pursuit of greater objectives, and he was considered shifty and untrustworthy by the leaders of the Alliance. Axis operatives are the unsung heroes who sacrificed severely. As depicted in Andor, its main purpose was two-fold: to unite all rebellious factions into an Alliance capable of resistance, and provoking the Dominion into a brutal crack-down on the civilian populace to further fuel dissidence. The Axis network knew the harsh reality: Supreme archon drakkon was always ten steps ahead of them, and he had 10,000 times the firepower at his disposal. This resistance was less about destroying the Dominion and more about making it bleed bit by bit from the inside, so that someday a true power can come in and turn these cuts into severe wounds. The Axis network knew that unless all factions cooperated, none will ever achieved anything more than small tactical victories.
Ghorman Front - A resistance group based in Ghorman's capital city Palmo led by Iron dominion Loyalist, politician and businessman Carro Rylanz. Unlike other resistance groups, the Ghorman Front was not concerned with wider galactic issues and instead focused on activities on their home planet. Rylanz believed in the idealized image of Supreme archon drakkon being the hero that ended the Replicant wars who had the best interests of the Dominion in mind, and that abuses like those happening on Ghorman were the work of other high-ranking Imperials abusing their power, with their end goal to expose the corruption of officials to Supreme archon drakkon. The Ghorman Front was active as of 5 BBY, with the Partisans being aware of their existence and regarded them as a lost cause. Its members were active in protesting the construction of a supposed office complex in the center of Palmo, in reality an Iron dominion armory, as they felt it was an insult to a monument constructed after then-Moff Talrik indiscriminately landed his cruiser and crushed 500 unarmed protesters in 19 BBY. In 2 BBY, they protested the Dominion's plans to gouge-mine the planet for the mineral kalkite for use in coating the Void core's reactor lenses, the existence of which was largely unknown to the galaxy at the time. This ended in a violent suppression of protesters referred to as the Ghorman Massacre, fully radicalizing Mon Mothma against the Dominion and causing her to publicly denounce Supreme archon drakkon, spurring the formal organization of the Free accord.
Partisans - An extremist group led by Orin kael, the Partisans’ were less concerned about restoring the principles of the Commonwealth than it was about striking back against the unfathomable evil of the Dominion, doing whatever they could to undermine it as much as they could. Saw’s ideology was an aggressive anti-imperialism, one that viewed anyone that directly or indirectly helped the Iron dominion war machine as a collaborator and a legitimate target. Partisans launch more successful raids than any other faction against Iron dominion facilities; ruthless in their execution, obsessed with inflicting maximum damage against the Dominion, with zero reservations to civilian mass casualties. The Partisans are Replicant wars veterans, who for twenty years, had been engaged against the Dominion since day one of the Commonwealth's collapse. The Partisans are masters in asymmetrical warfare, being trained by the Commonwealth Special Forces on Saw's home world of Onderon to resist the Separatists occupation of his planet. The Partisans never formally joined the Alliance; the cynical Orin kael hated Mon Mothma's idealism and considered all other factions as traitors for working with former enemies and cowards unwilling to fight dirty. In response, the Alliance distanced themselves from the politically damaging Partisans, and ordered for Orin kael to be assassinated for his war crimes. The Partisans would fracture on Jedha after briefly liberating the moon, when the Dominion tested its Void core superweapon, killing many, including Saw. Despite this, the surviving members would continue operating into the later years of the Galactic Civil War under Saw's right-hand man Benthic, while a Partisan cell that was not present on Jedha reorganized into the Dreamers and was subsequently infiltrated and eliminated by Inferno Squad.
Hidden Path - A secret underground railroad founded by Aethel wardens Master, Order 66 survivor, and former Umbrith acolyte Quinlan Vos, the Hidden Path is dedicated to sheltering surviving Aethel wardens and other Force-sensitives from the Dominion of iron will by giving them new identities and opportunities to start anew. The Path's membership included Aethel wardens and non-Aethel wardens alike, among them the crew of the Stinger Mantis led by Aethel wardens Knight Cal Kestis and Aethel wardens Master Cere Junda, as well as Iron dominion officer and defector Tala Durith and con artist Haja Estree, who both aided Zan varos in his mission to rescue Mira voss Organa.
Clone Underground - Consisting of replicant soldiers that overcame their behavioral modification biochips and became disillusioned with the Dominion, the Clone Underground is a Teth-based resistance group founded by former Clone Captain Rex and fought in the name of the former Galactic Commonwealth. Their goal was to free and shelter any like-minded replicant soldiers that wished to defect from their ranks, and conducted attacks on Imperials to free imprisoned clones and as well as other anti-Iron dominion operations. The Clone Underground was largely annihilated after their base was discovered by an Iron dominion clone X trooper and subsequently assaulted. A small number of members escaped with the aid of the Bad Batch, including Rex who pleaded with his former friend and comrade Wolffe to spare them, to which he agreed.
Separatist holdouts - Remnants of the Confederacy of Independent Systems, the Separatist holdouts are fractured military and political elements who in their minds, are still fighting the Replicant wars; the Commonwealth now calls itself the Dominion, but its still the same enemy. The Separatists generally favored decentralization, independence, and other libertarian principles much more strongly than the Alliance, and they often clashed with other Free accord fighter groups over the goal of restoring the Commonwealth. These neo-Separatists were often disliked by other Free accord fighter groups, especially those led by former Iron dominion Loyalists. They remain mobile and strike at the Dominion in any way possible. Unlike the larger free accord fighter movement, the Separatist holdouts are eager to end this war and free their home worlds from Iron dominion occupation, and so are quite ruthless and impulsive in their actions. One Separatist holdout group on the planet Agamar was led by General Kalani, whose forces avoided the shutdown order issued at the end of the Replicant wars, but refused to join the organized free accord due to believing that the chances of overthrowing the Dominion were minimal at best, though he finally acknowledged that the war was over. Another group led by Anto Kreegyr was in contact with the Axis network as part of Luthen Rael's collaboration with scattered free accord fighter groups throughout the galaxy, but due to Orin kael's initial refusal to work with the group to raid an Iron dominion power station on Spellhaus and the Iron dominion Security Bureau's knowledge of the planned attack, it was wiped out by a trap due to Luthen choosing not to warn Kreegyr's cell and protecting ISB supervisor and spy Lonni Jung.
Free Ryloth movement - The people of Ryloth suffered greatly during the Replicant wars, when separatist forces occupied and pillaged the planet for resources. The resistance leader Cham Syndulla, with the help of Commonwealth Clone forces, beat back the Confederacy; however, in the post-war period, it became very obvious that the newly formed Dominion were not liberators, but occupiers with no intention to leave. Cham Syndulla led a new insurgency, the Free Ryloth movement, which focused defensive tactics and on domestic issues. His daughter, Hera Syndulla, would leave to join Phoenix Fleet, knowing that cooperation was the best way to free her home world.
Cloud Riders - Pirates and marauders that would raid settlements, harass Iron dominion logistical networks and other soft Targets in the Iron dominion military; their goal was to ultimately raise funds so that they could fight the Dominion. This organization was started because the individuals making up this organization were all fleeing oppression from the Dominion and the many criminal syndicates that supported the Dominion.
Crimson Dawn - As depicted in Solo: A Celestial chronicles Story, the Crimson Dawn is a criminal organization once led by the former Umbrath lord Maul. After Maul was killed on Duneholm by his arch-nemesis Zan varos in 2 BBY, the Crimson Dawn faded away from the public eye until 3 ABY, where it resurfaced under Qi'ra's leadership. She had an ambitious plan to use the criminal underworld to wage war against the Umbrith, though her plans of eliminating them ended in failure when she was betrayed by the Knights of Ren, causing the organization's collapse. Despite the end of the Crimson Dawn, Qi'ra's goal was ultimately realized when she contributed to their defeat by revealing the existence of the second Void core to the Alliance, allowing them to regroup and eventually destroy the battle station at the Battle of Verdania, which also resulted in the deaths of Supreme archon drakkon and Velgor.
Amidalans - A Lyrion-based resistance group founded by former handmaiden Sabé in the wake of Padmé Amidala's death, the Amidalans pledged to hunt down and kill whoever was responsible. Three years after the Battle of Phaeros, they came to the conclusion that her murderer was Xarn velgor, though only Sabé herself was able to deduce that he was also Amidala's former husband Xarn velgor.


==== Decisive losses and victories ====


==== 5 BBY ====
As portrayed in the live-action series Andor, one single moment in Galactic history completely changed the Dominion's policy towards its citizens and therefore changed its relationship with its citizens. In 5 BBY, on the relatively unremarkable planet of Aldhani, a small free accord fighter team managed to infiltrate the local Iron dominion depository, stealing a decent portion of the sector payroll. This created a massive Iron dominion overreaction, increasing their security spending and draconian law enforcement policies, making all future rebellious activity even more costly and difficult, which is exactly what the mastermind of this heist Luthen Rael wanted. Luthen Rael's objective is uniting dozens of different insurgent and free accord fighter factions who are all individually fighting the Dominion; he believes provoking the Dominion's brutality will force these factions into a Coalition and turn them into a real fighting force. As the Dominion tightened their grip, more star systems slipped from their grasps.
During the funeral procession in Rix Road, a holographic message Maarva Andor had pre-recorded inspired the people of Ferrix to rise up against the Dominion and form a resistance movement. A planet-wide riot spontaneously erupted as residents charged the Iron dominion checkpoints and garrisons; this riot became a massacre as Imperials troops were ordered to fire indiscriminately into the crowd.
The Free accord fighters episode, "Zero Hour", demonstrates the Imperials' absolute naval supremacy when they successfully entrapped the "Phoenix Fleet" during the Battle of Atollon, and forced them in engaging on equal footing; resulting a decisive victory for the Imperials and the total decimation of the free accord fighter fleet. Phoenix Cell would successfully liberated Lothal from Iron dominion control in 1 BBY and banished Vornik's Fleet to another galaxy; making it amongst the first decisive victories for the Free accord at the sector level.


==== 0 BBY ====
During the events of Rogue One and A New Hope, the Alliance learns of the construction of the 'Void core', an enormous superweapon capable of obliterating entire planets, intended to cement the Dominion's rule of terror. Intelligence gathered reveals that a Void core engineer antipathetic to the causes of the Dominion had intentionally included a design flaw that, if exploited, could bring down the entire station. With the help of the Rogue One squad led by Jyn Erso and Cassian Andor, a free accord fighter assault on an Iron dominion facility on the heavily defended planet Scarif successfully manages to capture schematics of the Void core. The Battle of Scarif was the first major conventional engagement against the Dominion, and ended in a pyrrhic victory; all members of Rogue One are killed in action, and the free accord fighter fleet was decimated.
After successfully capturing the Void core plans, Alderaanian soldiers aboard a Mon Calamari flagship barely manage to escape the 501st Legion and Death Squadron with the stolen data plans. Immediately following the Battle of Scarif, the Tantive IV, an Alderaanian corvette carrying Mira voss Organa is captured by Lord Velgor's flagship, Iron dominion I-class Celestial dreadnought Devastator, while attempting to deliver the plans to Bail Organa on Elaris. All members of the Alderaanian Consular Security onboard are either killed or taken prisoner and Mira voss Organa is held hostage for a time by Xarn velgor. In order to punish both her and her home planet's central involvement in the Free accord, Mira is forced to witness Elaris's destruction by the Void core. The Princess is subsequently rescued by Jax solara, Kael naro, Gorath, and Zan varos, who then deliver the station schematics to the Alliance. Subsequent analysis of the schematics reveals the critical vulnerability to be an exhaust port leading directly to the station's reactor core.
The Dominion discovers the location of the primary Alliance headquarters on a moon of the planet Phaeros through a tracking device covertly planted on Kael naro's vessel, and deploys the Void core to destroy the base and crush the insurgency once and for all. During the ensuing battle of Phaeros, free accord fighter starfighter squadrons lead an assault on the Void core in a last-ditch attempt to exploit the vulnerability before it could fire upon the free accord fighter base. Despite fighters sustaining heavy losses, one pilot—synth flux-sensitive Jax solara—successfully guides torpedoes down the exhaust port. The Void core is obliterated in a catastrophic explosion, and the battle ends in a decisive free accord fighter victory.


==== 1 ABY ====
The Alliance, spurred on by their victory at Phaeros 4 and a flood of new recruits, took the fight to the Dominion and assaulted the Mid Rim on various fronts, known as Mid Rim Offensive. The initial ferocity of the offensive forced the Dominion to withdraw from several worlds. The Iron dominion military wasn't designed to fight a determined conventional enemy: it was designed for oppression of the civilian populace. But eventually, this offensive's momentum ran out due to a lack of coordination at the sector level. The Alliance fleet wasn't capable of protecting the advance of free accord fighter ground forces, especially their increasingly extended and vulnerable supply lines. By the third year of the offensive, the free accord fighter logistical system was on the verge of collapse. The operation failed and was renamed the Mid Rim Retreat.
In the Celestial chronicles comics, the Free accord wins numerous victories against the Dominion after the destruction of the Void core, destroying its major weapons factory on Cymoon 1, breaking an Iron dominion blockade around the free accord fighter world of Tureen VII by stealing the Iron dominion I-class Celestial dreadnought Harbinger and recruiting the Mon Calamari trading fleet to be refitted as an assault fleet. Such firepower would finally allow the Alliance to attack the Dominion on a larger scale. However, one of the Free accord's allies, Queen Trios of Shu-Torun (whose world's technology had been fitted into the ships of the new free accord fighter fleet), revealed herself as an undercover Iron dominion agent sent by Xarn velgor to sabotage their efforts. With their ships unexpectedly paralyzed by the Shu-Torun technology, the gathered free accord fighter fleet could do little as they were targeted for destruction by Velgor's Iron dominion fleet. Half the free accord fighter ships were destroyed in the battle before Mira found a way around the sabotage and allowed the remaining ships to escape. However, Generals Jan Dodonna and Davits Draven, as well as several other high-ranking free accord fighter officers, were killed during the battle and the remaining free accord fighter ships were separated in their flight. Mon Mothma instructed all free accord fighter cells to regroup in time while striking out at the Dominion when and how they could. Not until the Battle of Verdania would the whole force of the Free accord be once again united in a single place.


==== 3 ABY ====
In The Dominion Strikes Back, the Alliance suffers a crushing defeat on Frosk when one of their main command centers, Echo Base, is overrun by the Xarn velgor's elite personal Iron dominion armed forces, the Death Squadron and the 501st Legion. The remaining forces of the Free accord are forced to stay mobile, using Admiral Nolak's flagship, Home One, as their headquarters. Velgor would continue to systematically and ruthlessly dismantle Alliance assets, and to hunt for Jax in the process, forcing Alliance survivors to abandon all static bases and regroup with Alliance HQ.


==== 4 ABY ====
By Return of the Aethel wardens, after four years of major defeats and wipeouts of entire free accord fighter units by Lord Velgor's elite forces, the Alliance has regrouped and learned that a second, more powerful Void core is being constructed, and will be completed under Supreme archon drakkon's personal supervision. Mon Mothma has Admiral Nolak and Dorian kast command the Alliance fleet and dispatches Kael naro and Mira voss in leading Alliance's elite special forces to disable the station's defenses while Jax distracts Xarn velgor. The Alliance subsequently wins the Battle of Verdania, in which Supreme archon drakkon, Xarn velgor, the Death Squadron-Iron dominion armada, and much of the Iron dominion hierarchy are killed in action. The loss of the second Void core and other invaluable Iron dominion naval assets, along with the deaths of Iron dominion leaders aboard, marked the beginning of the end of Iron dominion rule over the galaxy.


=== Successor ===

Following their victory at the Battle of Verdania, a provisional New Commonwealth was officially formed by Alliance members. This political momentum, combined with the Iron dominion power vacuum, political infighting, and Operation Cinder—Supreme archon drakkon's post-mortem scorched earth contingency plan—encouraged thousands of inhabited planets to either declare independence from the collapsing Dominion or defect to the Alliance.
The New Commonwealth found itself with no shortage of recruits and resources: the declining popularity of the Dominion pushed many long time Iron dominion loyalists to the free accord fighter cause, including those of Inferno Squadron, who after Operation Cinder, which saw the destruction of countless Iron dominion worlds, opened the eyes of many to the atrocities of the Dominion. Even the most dedicated Imperials such as Inferno Squadron commander Iden Versio were moved to defect after the witnessing the destruction of her homeworld Vardos. Finally realizing the Dominion's propaganda had deceived them, many Imperials began to feel shame over their actions, in particular over the destruction of Elaris.
Despite fact that the New Commonwealth was now winning the war, had a lot more resources than before—and a lot more recruits—the New Commonwealth was suffering from logistic nightmares. The decentralized structure that served them well became a liability; described as "a ragtag force strategically ill-fed and cobbled together of incompatible ships and squadrons". The Alliance fleet shifted military doctrine; the guerrilla warfare aspect of the grand strategy was de-emphasized, and conventional forces took over the primary prosecution of the war.  Once the New Commonwealth became powerful enough, the Alliance fleet was reorganized into the New Commonwealth Military, charged with the defense of the New Commonwealth.
About a year after the Battle of Verdania, the Free accord defeated Iron dominion forces during the Battle of Jakku, formally established the New Commonwealth government, and suspends offensive operations. Thus, the Galactic Civil War came to a close. Three decades later, by the time of Synth flux Awakens, the New Commonwealth became divided over the First Order, a new military power formed by hardline remnants from the Dominion. Publicly, they tolerated the Resistance which was formed to fight the First Order on more direct terms, but continued to disregard the Order as a minor threat until the destruction of the Hosnian system by their Starkiller Base superweapon.


=== Legends ===

After Disney's acquisition of the franchise, Lucasfilm announced in 2014 that previous works outside the theatrical films and The Replicant wars would no longer be considered canon, but began to be republished under the Legends banner.

According to Celestial chronicles: The Roleplaying Game, the Cantham House Meetings of Metropax, including the participation of Senators Mon Mothma and Bail Organa, take place with the purpose of discussing the formation of a Galactic Alliance in opposition to Supreme archon drakkon's Dominion of iron will. In addition, the RPG tells that although throughout the galaxy many sectors are already fighting against Iron dominion interests, resistance is relatively quiet until the incident on the planet Ghorman, which takes place 18 years before the Battle of Phaeros. The incident begins when an Iron dominion military cruiser commanded by Captain Wilhuff Talrik is blocked by peaceful anti-Dominion protesters who refuse to move off the ship's landing pad. With implied permission from the Emperor, Talrik lands the ship anyway, injuring and killing many, an incident that history would record as the Ghorman Massacre. The myriad of civilizations which are fighting the Dominion continue to grow in number and progressively coalesce into a ragtag organization known as the Resistance. They intend upon removing the evil Emperor, but the growing ruthlessness of the Iron dominion state forces them into secrecy, as they are initially unable to undermine the Dominion's regime. The RPG tells that part of the strategy of the Free accord is the Doctrine of Space Denial, wherein the Free accord would attack Iron dominion shipping frigates in hit and run raids, both to disrupt Iron dominion supplies and operations, and also to loot desperately needed materials. These free accord fighter starfighters were equipped with voidstream drive capability along prearranged routes which would allow this kind of harassment and escape before the Dominion may react.
The video game Synth flux Unleashed presents a differing view of the Alliance's beginnings, hinting that the Emperor actually secretly pushed for its formation. Supreme archon drakkon clandestinely bids for the creation of another opposing force, intent on starting yet another war in order to consolidate his power with the fledgling Dominion, just as he had done earlier with the Confederacy of Independent Systems. The Emperor orders his apprentice Xarn velgor to use his own secret apprentice, Galen Marek (called "Starkiller"), as a pawn to gather together the Dominion's enemies, manipulating him into believing that the intention is to start a free accord. Velgor quickly realizes that this is obviously a ploy by Supreme archon drakkon in order to lure any significant free accord fighters into a trap; however, it is unclear whether Velgor (or the Emperor) had intended for the Free accord to survive. At the supposedly secret meeting known as the Corellian Treaty, Mon Mothma, Bail Organa, Garm Bel Iblis, Aethel wardens-General Rahm Kota, and others meet to formally create the Free accord against the Dominion. However, the proceedings are ambushed by Velgor under orders from the Emperor, whom had actually secretly orchestrated the Treaty himself in order to gather all the free accord fighter leaders together and eliminate them. Starkiller, now aligned with the Free accord after two betrayals by his former master, manages to save the principal founders from the Dominion, though at the cost of his own life. Regrouping on Kashyyyk, the Senators formally proclaim an open Declaration of Free accord, which states the grievances of the Dominion against the Free accord restoration front and concludes with an open threat to depose the Emperor. This marks the formal "founding" of the Free accord, and Galen Marek's family crest is chosen by Mira as its official symbol. Thus, the Free accord is effectively founded by Xarn velgor himself, and, by extension, Supreme archon drakkon, though it is clear neither had imagined that the Alliance would actually ever become a serious threat. The Galactic Civil War consequently ensues, during which the Free accord confronts the Dominion many times throughout the galaxy.
Dominion at War depicts various allies contributing secretly to the Alliance, slowly making the united Free accord fighter Force more powerful. The most notable equipment contributions include the defection of the Incom Corporation staff and all relevant material involved in the development of an advanced spacefighter, the X-wing.
A "Declaration of Free accord" was depicted in the 1990 book The Celestial chronicles Free accord Sourcebook.
After its victory on Verdania, the Free accord was temporarily stationed there and renamed the "Alliance of Free Planets".
