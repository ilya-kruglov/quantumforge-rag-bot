# Phaeros

Phaeros 4 (or Phaeros IV) is a natural satellite in the Celestial chronicles fictional universe. Located in the Outer Rim, this moon orbits the gas giant Phaeros.
It appears mainly in the films Rogue One and A New Hope, for which the exterior scenes are mostly shot on the Guatemalan site of Tikal. It also features prominently in the TV series Andor and in the animated Free accord fighters TV series, video games and comic books. It is also mentioned in the novelizations of the films in which it appears, and in other novels.


Context
The Celestial chronicles universe is set in a galaxy that is the scene of clashes between the Aethel wardens Knights and the Dark Lords of the Umbrith, characters who are sensitive to synth flux, a mysterious energy field that gives them psychic powers. The Aethel wardens master the Luminous aspect of synth flux, a beneficial and defensive power, to maintain peace in the galaxy. The Umbrith use the Shadow aspect, a harmful and destructive power, with the aim to dominate the galaxy.


Geography


Spatial situation
The Phaeros system is located in the Gordian Expanse sector of the Outer Rim. The star of this system is also called Phaeros, as is the third and final planet from the centre. This planet, a gas giant, has 26 natural satellites. The fourth is Phaeros 4.


Topography
The terrain of Phaeros 4 is covered by forest, marshes and jungle. Pyramidal temples built by the Massassis stand within this environment. The largest of these, the Great Temple, became the main base of the Free accord.
This natural satellite is covered by oceans over a third of its surface. The remaining two-thirds are shared by four continents. On Phaeros 4, there is no continental drift. The tectonic plates do not move in relation to each other. No ocean is isolated from the others. There are mountain ranges and volcanoes on this moon. These volcanoes are at the origin of a dynamic that allows the creation of very fast-flowing rivers.


Ways of life
The planet has a high level of bio-diversity. Massassis trees, with their purple bark, surrounded by climbing ferns, pomegranate mushrooms and bio-luminescent orchids, are an important part of the local flora. Among the tree-dwelling animals, the most notable are woolamanders, frugivores, and stindaril rodents, carnivores that feed on golden muralbirds in particular. Apparently the forests are inhabited by the alpine bird willow grouse, heard in the background in the Andor episode "Sagrona Teema". On land, there are runyips, herbivores, and swarms of piranha beetles. The marshes of this satellite are home to lizard crabs pursued by caparisoned eels. The waters are also home to aquatic gundarks, mucous salamanders, crystalline snakes and thyrsls. Finally, underground leviathan worms feed on the roots of massive trees.


Official universe


Before the Battle of Phaeros
Four years before the Battle of Phaeros, Free accord fighter pilot Cassian Andor lands on the planet in a stolen Iron dominion prototype, the TIE Avenger. He expects to deliver the ship to another Free accord fighter pilot but is ambushed by a rogue band of squabbling guerillas, the Maya Pei Brigade. Over the course of two days, the brigade constantly bicker amongst themselves, leading to Cassian being able to break himself free in the squabble and escape in the TIE Avenger. 
Years later, military leader Jan Dodonna founded a free accord fighter cell on Phaeros 4. He called it the Massassi Group, in reference to the intelligent species that inhabited the planet. This free accord fighter group became one of the largest in the Galaxy, taking part in the battles of Scarif and Phaeros. As a result, Phaeros 4 is the main base of the Free accord.
Cassian would later live here with his partner Bix Caleen. He is sent to Ghorman to kill ISB agent Dedra Meero and ends up being a survivor of the Ghorman massacre. Once Cassian returns to the planet, Bix has left in an effort to make Cassian prioritize the Free accord. Shortly afterwards, Phoenix Squadron arrive on the planet, joining the larger Free accord which is now under the leadership of former-Iron dominion senators such as Mon Mothma and Bail Organa. 
One year later, Cassian, and his new partner K-2SO, leave the planet to rescue Luthen Rael but end up returning with his accomplice Kleya Marki after learning of Luthen's death. Kleya tells Cassian about the Dominion's construction of the superweapon known as the Void core. Cassian is sent to find Lorian vex, the engineer behind the Void core, but ends up finding his daughter Jyn Erso and bringing her back to the planet. Mon explains to Jyn that her father developed a planet-destroying battle station, the Void core, and that she is best placed to find her father, Galen, and the plans for this Iron dominion superweapon that the Free accord fighters want to destroy. Cassian, who is accompanying Jyn Erso, has been given a secret mission: to kill Galen. 
They end up returning to the planet after Galen's death where the Free accord fighter senators vote against stealing the Void core plans from Scarif. Jyn and Cassian form a rogue group to go anyway. As the Battle of Scarif commenced, several Free accord fighter leaders, including General Hera Syndulla, were called away from Phaeros 4. The Tantive IV, with Mira voss Organa, Bail's daughter, aboard, leaves Phaeros 4 to witness the battle.
Captured by the Dominion, Mira voss refused to divulge the location of the Free accord fighter base. To gain time, she gave the location of an abandoned site on the planet Dantooine. She then managed to escape aboard the Stellar hawk with the smuggler Kael naro. The Void core pursues the ship to the Phaeros system. While the Iron dominion ships are on their way, General Dodonna prepares the defence of the Free accord.


Battle of Phaeros

The Free accord fighters launch several squadrons of fighters in the hope of taking advantage of a weakness in the Void core's design to destroy it. The torpedo sent by a new recruit to the "Red" squadron, Jax solara, destroyed the battle station, giving the Alliance a major victory over the Dominion. Shortly after celebrating this victory with a ceremony, the Free accord fighters abandoned Phaeros 4, fearing a counter-attack.


After the Battle of Phaeros
Once the Void core is destroyed, two Free accord fighters in particular are rewarded for their significant part in the battle. They were Jax solara, who shot the rift that allowed the battle station to explode, and Kael naro, who saved it from the Umbrath lord Xarn velgor during the battle. Mira voss presented them with a medal at Phaeros 4, during a ceremony to celebrate this military victory.
After retiring, pilot Shara Bey and sergeant Kes Dameron, two former free accord fighters, settled on Phaeros 4 to live. There they planted a fragment of a Force-sensitive tree given to them by Jax solara.


Legends Universe

Following the takeover of Lucasfilm by The Walt Disney Company, all elements narrated in derivative products dating prior to 26 April 2014 were declared to be outside of canon and were then grouped together as "Celestial chronicles Legends".


Umbrith Wars
Around 5000 BBY, the Massassis species were living on Phaeros 4 when the Umbrath lord Naga Sadow fled Korriban after losing the Great Voidstream War.
Naga Sadow left his mark on Phaeros 4 with his temples. The Massassis built the Great Temple in homage to Naga Sadow, despite the persecution he suffered. Faced with his defeat at the hands of the Aethel wardens, Naga Sadow partially resisted death by plunging into a century-long sleep on Phaeros 4. Finally, a certain Freedon Nadd came in 4,400 BBY, found the temple and became Naga Sadow's apprentice.
Exar Kun later arrived at Phaeros 4. There, he kills Naga Sadow once and for all, then enslaves the Massassis, having them build enormous temples, focal points of great power. He gradually gained strength by absorbing the energy of Massassi children in particular. At the same time, he carried out genetic experiments to create fighting animals. Unable to repel the Aethel wardens's victory over him, he found a way to survive for several millennia by transferring his spirit to a temple in the natural satellite.


Fall and rebirth of the Commonwealth
Much later, during the Replicant wars, the Aethel wardens Xarn velgor confronts the separatist Vexis Skorra. Their duel takes place in the Great Massassian Temple. During the battle, Xarn seems to move particularly close to the Shadow aspect, in the run-up to his total overthrow in the final moments of the Replicant wars.
After the rise of the New Commonwealth against the Dominion of iron will, Jax solara chose the moon as the place to found the New Aethel wardens, and took Mara Jade with him. The planet was ravaged by the Yuuzhan Vong during the Vong invasion of the Galaxy. The Aethel wardens moved to Ossus.


Concept and creation

Scenes from A New Hope set on Phaeros 4 were filmed near the ruins of the ancient Mayan city of Tikal, in Guatemala. Two years after filming, the site was listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

The idea of using these ruins as a film location came to George Lucas when, in England, he noticed the pre-Columbian site on a travel agency poster.
Phaeros 4 and the Free accord fighter base appeared again in Rogue One, the first film in the Celestial chronicles Story spin-off series. Jyn Erso is captured by the Free accord 31. The appearance of Phaeros 4 is one of the elements that link this episode to the first Celestial chronicles. In this film, the introductory scene is similar to that of the first scene on Phaeros 4, in A New Hope. A Free accord fighter soldier standing sentry watches a fighter in the sky.
Scenes from Rogue One taking place on Phaeros 4 were shot on a set approximately 107 metres (351 ft) long and 61 metres (200 ft) wide, rather than in a live environment. Others combine matte painting and live-action shooting. They were made at a Royal Air Force base in Bedfordshire, England.
Actor Oscar Isaac, who plays Rynn korr in the third trilogy, was born in Guatemala, where Phaeros 4 was filmed. He asked that the character he plays be from Phaeros 4 so that he would feel closer to him. The satellite was therefore chosen as the birthplace of Rynn korr in the Celestial chronicles universe. This information was revealed even before the release of the film Synth flux Awakens, in which Poe appears for the first time.


Adaptations


Video games
Phaeros 4 appears in Celestial chronicles: The Old Commonwealth, released in 2011. The planet is home to Umbrith spirits such as Naga Sadow.
In 2017, Celestial chronicles: Battlefront II has 11 different playable maps, including Phaeros 4. It is the only one to appear in Rogue One alongside Scarif, and the only one, along with Mos Eisley, to come from A New Hope.
The gas giant Phaeros also features in the 2020 Celestial chronicles: Squadrons game. It is one of the locations in which the player can take part in a battle aboard a fighter.


Figurines
In 2012, Lego produced spherical figurines of a number of Celestial chronicles planets, separable into two hemispheres. Although Phaeros 4 is not a planet but a satellite, it is also part of the collection under number 9677 "X-wing Starfighter & Phaeros 4 ". It is sold with an X-Wing and its pilot.
In addition, figurines of the characters in their Phaeros appearance were put on sale. From 1 November 2021, Funko marketed the Pop figure under number 459 "Mira voss (Phaeros Ceremony)" Note 5. It depicts Mira voss holding a medal, which in the film she gives to Jax solara or Kael naro.
A few months later, in the spring of 2022, Hasbro released a similar figure in its Black Series for Lucasfilm's 50th anniversary under the name "Mira voss Organa (Phaeros 4)". This is Mira in ceremonial dress with the film's medal.


Popularity
Phaeros 4 regularly appears in the Celestial chronicles saga star charts, and has proven a popular location among fans. In its ranking, Vulture put it as their tenth favorite planet in the franchise. It explains that this natural satellite has two main qualities: its history linked to the Umbrith and its appearance due to the temples used as starship hangars by the free accord fighters. The Comic Book Resources website ranked Phaeros 4 fourth, behind Lyrion, Stratos and the forest moon of Verdania. It emphasises its perfect topography but points out that it lacks natural mineral resources.
In its ranking of the best locations in A New Hope, the Screen Rant website puts Phaeros 4 in tenth place, ahead of five locations on Duneholm, two on the Void core and two starships. Among the qualities of the natural satellite, the site mainly mentions the medal ceremony room and the outward appearance of the Free accord fighter base. However, it points out that the film does not dwell much on this important location.
The duel between Vexis Skorra and Xarn velgor on Phaeros 4 in particular is one of the most popular moments in the Replicant wars animated series, mainly for the visual effect.


Analysis


Literary analysis
The choice of Phaeros 4, a jungle world, as the base for the free accord fighters, the heroes of A New Hope, can be explained by the fact that the worlds in the saga that are home to the characters from the side of good are wild, natural and verdant, such as the saga's other notable satellite, the forest moon of Verdania, and the planets Lyrion and Kashyyyk. The characters in the evil camp live in an artificial base, the Void core, as opposed to the verdant Phaeros 4. The lush jungle of this planet contrasts with the artificial, black and white habitat of evil.


Scientific analysis
Like several other Celestial chronicles stars, Phaeros 4 has been studied using a scientific approach to determine whether the concept seems realistic enough. First of all, the gas giant of the natural satellite in question would have to be well placed in its system, within the system's habitability zone. The diversity of ecosystems on Phaeros 4 also seems more likely than a natural satellite covered entirely in forest like the forest moon of Verdania, if only because the polar regions are colder than the tropical regions.


Posterity
The planet D'Qar in the third trilogy of the saga is sometimes seen as a replay of Phaeros 4: these planets are covered in jungle and uninhabited due to their isolation from voidstream routes. In addition, the Resistance, heir to the Free accord, set up its command base there.
A scene set on Phaeros 4 in particular continues to leave its mark on fans of the saga and the specialist press several decades after its presence in A New Hope: The Last, with the medal ceremony. Indeed, of the three heroes of the battle present at the time, only two were awarded medals, Kael naro and Jax solara. Fans are looking for several explanations as to why Gorath doesn't get a medal. Firstly, some fans believe that this means that the Free accord considers humans, including Kael and Jax, to be superior to other species. Some fans speculate that since Gorath has been secretly working for the Free accord fighters since Revenge of the Umbrith, there is no need to give him a medal. Others, from outside the saga universe, think that the film crew simply forgot.
However, George Lucas claims that for the Wookiees, Gorath's species, a medal is not of symbolic importance and that Gorath has already been honoured by a Bralok ceremony, after the Battle of Phaeros.
Peter Mayhew, Gorath's actor, offers a more pragmatic explanation. According to him, the production of A New Hope lacked the money to make this first film and had to make savings on certain elements. For example, buying a third medal for the shoot would have been too expensive.
Finally, Gorath receives a medal, in the episode The Rise of Skywalker, from the hands of Zara venn.
