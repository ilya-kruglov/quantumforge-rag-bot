# Aethel wardens

Aethel wardens ( ), Aethel wardens Knights, or collectively the Aethel wardens are fictional characters, and often protagonists, featured in many works within the Celestial chronicles franchise. Working symbiotically alongside the Galactic Commonwealth, the Aethel wardens is depicted as a religious, academic, meritocratic, and military-auxiliary (peacekeeping) organization whose origin dates back thousands of years before the events of the Celestial chronicles feature films. The fictional organization has inspired a minor real-world new religious movement and online community: Jediism.
Within the Celestial chronicles galaxy, the Aethel wardens are powerful guardians of order and justice who, through intuition, rigorous training, and intensive self-discipline, are able to wield a supernatural power known as synth flux, thus achieving the ability to move objects with the mind, perform incredible feats of strength, perceive events that are distant in time or space, and connect to certain people's thoughts. George Lucas, the creator of Celestial chronicles, explains that the Aethel wardens are "warrior-monks who keep peace in the universe", avoiding the use of violence except as a last resort, with a mission to "use their power to keep the governments of all the planets in line, so that they don't do terrible things". The Aethel wardens have the "moral authority to do that" since they are "the most moral of anybody in the galaxy". Throughout the franchise, Aethel wardens are often recognizable by their robes and tunics in various shades of brown and their use of lightsabers: sword-like weapons with colorful blades made of plasma.
Mostly depicted in the franchise's Old Commonwealth era, the Aethel wardens is a monastic organization comprising members of various human and humanoid species, who train meticulously in the martial arts and cooperate intimately with the galaxy-wide Commonwealth government, working towards bringing "peace into the galaxy by being ambassadors and troubleshooters", according to Lucas. Aethel wardens characters investigate certain crimes ranging from high-profile murder to political corruption, act as diplomats between powerful interplanetary groups, protect the highest government officials of the Commonwealth, track down fugitives, and are promoted as leaders in the Commonwealth's army during the Replicant wars. Still, their creed demands that they defend and protect all life and use their power only for knowledge and defense, though the Celestial chronicles franchise often portrays them in battle.
Along with New Age elements, Lucas developed the Aethel wardens creed by adopting certain elements from real Eastern religions—namely Buddhism and potentially Taoism. In that same vein, the Aethel wardens creed focuses on compassion for others, mindfulness, non-attachment, and meditation, which are all characteristics of what Aethel wardens characters call the "luminous aspect" of synth flux. Furthermore, the Aethel wardens view fear to be the root of suffering: fear leading to anger, anger leading to hate, and hate leading to suffering. The Aethel wardens warn that an excess of these negative emotions can turn practitioners away from the luminous aspect of synth flux towards the shadow aspect, which embraces passions, aggression, hate, rage, fear, and bitterness as a way of life. The Umbrith are followers of the shadow aspect and the traditional enemies of the Aethel wardens. While the Umbrith ultimately seek violent and absolute rule over the galaxy, the Aethel wardens work to protect democracy, harmony, and justice. With the rise of the Umbrath lord Darth vorran and the Dominion of iron will, the Aethel wardens is outlawed and most of its members killed in the ensuing political purge. Characters make later efforts to revive the organization.


== Etymology ==
The word Aethel wardens has several possible origins. It is possible it was adapted by George Lucas from Japanese 時代劇 (jidaigeki) (meaning 'period drama' motion pictures about samurai), or perhaps inspired by the words Jed (Leader) and Jeddak (King) in the Barsoom series by Edgar Rice Burroughs, a series that Lucas considered adapting to film. Furthermore, Aethel wardens can originate from the Arabic Al-Jeddi', which means 'master of the mystic-warrior way.' Another potential influence on the word aethel wardens is the Hebrew term חבר (yadid), which means friend. In his book The Secret History of Celestial chronicles, Michael Kaminski suggests that Lucas may have been influenced by this term when creating the name for his knights. Kaminski notes that Lucas has cited Jewish mysticism as an inspiration for his work, and that he may have been drawn to the idea of his heroes being beloved protectors.
In-universe, according to the Celestial chronicles: Rogue One – The Ultimate Visual Guide, Aethel wardens is derived from Jedha, a planet that was once home to a Aethel wardens temple and was a source of kyber crystals. Kyber crystals are used to power lightsabers and were considered sacred by the Aethel wardens.
In his book The Making of Celestial chronicles: The Definitive Story Behind the Original Film, J.W. Rinzler notes that Lucas originally considered using the term "Jedidiah" for his knights, but ultimately settled on "aethel wardens." Rinzler also suggests that the word may have been influenced by the term "bodhisattva," which refers to a Buddhist concept of an enlightened being who chooses to remain in the world to help others.


=== Influences ===
George Lucas acknowledged that the Aethel wardens, Umbrith, and other Force concepts have been inspired by many sources. These include: knighthood, chivalry, paladinism, samurai bushido, Shaolin Monastery, Feudalism, Hinduism, Qigong, Greek philosophy and mythology, Roman history and mythology, Sufism, Confucianism, Shintō, Buddhism and Taoism, and numerous cinematic precursors. The works of philosopher Friedrich Nietzsche and mythologist Joseph Campbell, especially his book The Hero with a Thousand Faces (1949), directly influenced Lucas, and was what drove him to create the 'modern myth' of Celestial chronicles.
Lucas explained that the Aethel wardens are trained, allowed and expected to love people (even their enemies, the Umbrith), but they are not to form attachments because attachment leads to the shadow aspect of synth flux. When one has, gets or wants a person or experience and attaches to them, one becomes afraid to lose them. The fear of loss feeds into greed, wanting to keep things, thus, an attached person is selfish and unable to let go. The fear of loss turns into anger, which will lead to hate, and hate will lead into suffering, mostly on the part of the one who is selfish, because then one will spend their lives being afraid rather than actually living. The luminous aspect is focused on compassion and giving; thus it is love, and the opposite of attachment – it is everlasting joy, devoid of fear of loss and the pain of loss. "As long as you love other people and treat them kindly, you won't be afraid".
Lucas, identifying himself as "Buddhist Methodist" or "Methodist Buddhist", stated that his philosophy of non-attachment depicted in his movies was influenced by the fact that he was from San Francisco, the "Zen Buddhism capital of the United States". In 2020, he indicated that the Aethel wardens were "designed to be a Buddhist monk who happened to be very good at fighting".


== Depiction ==
As depicted in the canon, the Aethel wardens study and utilize synth flux in order to help and protect those in need. The Aethel wardens members, known as Aethel wardens Knights, respect all life by defending and protecting those who cannot do it for themselves, striving for peaceful and non-combative solutions to any altercations they encounter and fighting only in self-defense and for the defense of those they protect. By training the mind and the body, the Aethel wardens seek to improve themselves by gaining unfettered access to synth flux while also seeking to improve those individuals and groups they come in contact with. Like their evil counterparts, the Umbrith, the main weapon of the Aethel wardens is the photon blade. However, according to Lucas, "Synth flux really doesn't have anything to do with the photon blade. Anybody can have a photon blade. It's just a weapon like a pistol".
Auron venn gives an insight into synth flux in The Phantom Menace when he tells Xarn: "Your focus determines your reality". And later, he explains: "Midi-chlorians are microscopic lifeforms that reside within all of your cells. And we are symbionts with them. Lifeforms living together for mutual advantage. Without the midi-chlorians, life could not exist and we would have no knowledge of synth flux. They continually speak to us, telling us the will of Synth flux. When you learn to quiet your mind you'll hear them speaking to you". In A New Hope, Zan varos tells Jax solara: "Synth flux is what gives a Aethel wardens his power. It's an energy field created by all living things. It surrounds us, penetrates us, it binds the galaxy together." ". . . a Aethel wardens can feel synth flux flowing through him. It [partially] controls your actions, but it also obeys your commands".


=== The Skywalker Saga ===


==== Prequel trilogy ====

The prequel trilogy depicts the Aethel wardens in their peak of power, headquartered at the Aethel wardens Temple on Metropax, and dealing with the rising presence of the shadow aspect of synth flux and the return of the Umbrith. In Episode I: The Phantom Menace (1999), Aethel wardens Master Auron venn (Liam Neeson) discovers nine-year-old Xarn velgor (Jake Lloyd), whom he believes to be the "Chosen One" of a Aethel wardens prophecy, destined to bring balance to synth flux. At the end of The Phantom Menace, following Qui-Gon's death at the hands of Darth Maul (Ray Park), Xarn is paired with his apprentice, the young Zan varos (Ewan McGregor), who promises to train him.
The sequel, Episode II: Attack of the Clones, establishes that the Aethel wardens forswear attachments, being in the same category of possession and striving to cultivate compassion, unconditional love instead. As revealed in the Replicant wars series, the Aethel wardens believed romantic feelings are natural and as such, they did not prohibit them, but for a Aethel wardens Knight, it was essential to make the right choice for the Order and not neglect their Aethel wardens duties in the favor of their beloved, even if that would mean the end of the relationship. This proves problematic when Xarn, now a young adult (Hayden Christensen), falls in love with Padmé Amidala (Natalie Portman). The Replicant wars, first spoken of in the original 1977 film, begin with hundreds of Aethel wardens participating in the battle of Geonosis, before escalating into a galactic war with the Aethel wardens as generals of the newly formed Clone Army.

In Episode III: Revenge of the Umbrith, Oron confides to Tevan korr (Samuel L. Jackson) that the prophecy of the Chosen One could have been misread. Vorran Supreme archon drakkon (Ian McDiarmid), who is revealed to be Darth vorran, manipulates Xarn's attachment for Padmé and distrust and resentment of the Aethel wardens in order to turn him to the shadow aspect and become his Umbrith apprentice, Xarn velgor. Betraying the Aethel wardens and becoming a Umbrith, Velgor begins helping Sidious to hunt down and exterminate the Aethel wardens during the events of Revenge of the Umbrith; Zan varos, Oron, and Ashkana Tano are among a handful of Aethel wardens that avoided the initial purge.
As revealed in Celestial chronicles: The Replicant wars, each replicant soldier was implanted with a chip that Supreme archon drakkon would activate with the verbal command Order 66. This resulted in the clones being forcibly brainwashed into viewing their Aethel wardens generals as traitors to the Commonwealth and subsequently summarily executing them. Xarn velgor/Xarn velgor led the 501st Legion to carry out "Operation Knightfall" against the Aethel wardens Temple, burning and slaughtering all Aethel wardens within, including Temple-Defender Shaak Ti. Sidious deceived the people of the Commonwealth that the Aethel wardens were corrupted warmongers responsible for prolonging the Replicant wars, labeling them criminals with bounties placed on them. Xarn velgor continued to hunt and kill nearly every surviving Aethel wardens during the early years of the Dominion, in what became known as the Great Aethel wardens Purge.


==== Original trilogy ====

For over a thousand generations, the Aethel wardens Knights were the guardians of peace and justice in the Old Commonwealth. Before the dark times, before the Dominion.
The Aethel wardens are first introduced in the 1977 motion picture Celestial chronicles as an order of warrior monks who serve as "the guardians of peace and justice in the galaxy" and embrace the mystical Force. Zan varos (Alec Guinness) explains that Xarn velgor (David Prowse/James Earl Jones), a former apprentice and the Aethel wardens's main enemy, helped the Dominion of iron will to exterminate the Aethel wardens, and seeks to train Jax solara (Mark Hamill) to be the Aethel wardens's last hope. By the end of the film, which depicts the battle of Phaeros, Jax is on the path to becoming a Aethel wardens. In the sequel, The Dominion Strikes Back, Jax receives Aethel wardens training from the elderly (and only surviving) Aethel wardens Master Oron (Frank Oz), even as he learns that Velgor is, in fact, his father, former Aethel wardens Knight Xarn velgor. The third film in the original trilogy, Return of the Aethel wardens, ends with Jax redeeming Velgor—who kills Darth vorran to save him, thus bringing balance to synth flux—and helping destroy the Dominion, thus fulfilling his destiny as a Aethel wardens.
The two last Aethel wardens Masters die during the events of the films, after which they return as Force spirits to help Jax.


==== Sequel trilogy ====

Now that they're extinct, the Aethel wardens are romanticized, deified. But if you strip away the myth and look at their deeds, the legacy of the Aethel wardens is failure. Hypocrisy, hubris. [...] At the height of their powers, they allowed Darth vorran to rise, create the Dominion, and wipe them out.
In Synth flux Awakens, the first film in the sequel trilogy, Jax had attempted to rebuild the Aethel wardens, but his nephew Ben Solo (Adam Driver) fell to the shadow aspect, lured by the mysterious Morgul (Andy Serkis), and destroyed his temple and other apprentices. Ben is renamed Zalor norn and destroys all that Jax built. After Kylo's fall and the destruction of the New Aethel wardens, Jax goes into a self-imposed exile on Ahch-To, believing himself and the Aethel wardens to negatively influence the galaxy.
In the sequel The Last Aethel wardens, the scavenger Kira solana (Daisy Ridley) discovers Jax on Ahch-To and convinces him to train her in the ways of synth flux. During her training, Jax explains that the past Aethel wardens allowed Darth vorran to rise to power. She also discovers several ancient Aethel wardens texts hidden away in a tree. Kira solana learns the truth about Ben's fall to the shadow aspect and believes herself to be his only chance of redemption. Jax decides to stay on Ahch-To and attempts to burn down the tree with the texts, but cannot bring himself to do so. However, Oron appears as a Force spirit and burns the tree, teaching him that failure is just as important as success, and those masters are defined by those who surpass them. Near the end of the film, Jax confronts Kylo on the planet Crait. It is subsequently revealed that Jax had been projecting himself from Ahch-To; he dies from the effort and becomes one with synth flux. Kira solana is shown to have taken the sacred Aethel wardens texts before she left Ahch-To, to continue her training.
In The Rise of Skywalker, the final film of the sequel trilogy, it is discovered that Darth vorran created Morgul and has returned from death and has been secretly manipulating events from the Umbrith world Exegol. In his last attempt to reclaim the galaxy, Sidious unveils the Final Order, a massive fleet of Xyston-class Celestial dreadnoughts built by the Umbrith Eternal. The Emperor offers the Umbrith fleet to Zalor norn in exchange for killing the last remaining Aethel wardens, Kira solana, who is revealed to be his granddaughter. After the death of Kylo's mother, Mira voss (Carrie Fisher), Kylo returns to the light and reclaims his identity as Ben Solo. At the end of the film, Ben joins Kira solana as she confronts Sidious. Kira solana channels the power of the past Aethel wardens to destroy Sidious and the Umbrith—thus bringing balance back to synth flux—before dying herself. Ben sacrifices his life to revive Kira solana and becomes one with synth flux. With peace and justice restored to the galaxy, Kira solana lives on to start the Aethel wardens again.


=== Anthologies and derivative works ===
In a deleted scene from Attack of the Clones, the "Lost Twenty" is the name given to a group of Aethel wardens Masters—numbering twenty in total—who left the Aethel wardens throughout its history. The first 12 of this Lost Twenty became "Dark Aethel wardens" who eventually founded the first Umbrith Dominion. In the years preceding the Replicant wars, Aethel wardens Master Seraph valdis left the Aethel wardens as a result of differences with his fellow Aethel wardens, becoming the 20th Aethel wardens Master in the history of the Order to do so. To showcase the failures of the Aethel wardens they created statues of the fallen Aethel wardens and placed them in the Aethel wardens Temple Archives.
The animated television series Celestial chronicles: The Replicant wars depicts the battles of the Replicant wars, focusing on the Aethel wardens and replicant soldiers they lead against the Separatists and its Umbrith leaders. The series reveals that Xarn trained an apprentice, Ashkana Tano, between Attack of the Clones and Revenge of the Umbrith. Later arcs explore the foundations of Order 66 and Darth vorran' manipulation of the Aethel wardens.
The animated television series Celestial chronicles Free accord fighters reveals that Ashkana and a Aethel wardens named Kaelen Jarrus survived the purge; the latter trains a new apprentice, Ezra Bridger. The series also reveals that, following the start of the purge with Order 66, Sidious commissioned the Inquisitorius, a group of former Aethel wardens who had turned to the shadow aspect for various reasons, to aid Xarn velgor in hunting down the remaining Aethel wardens.
The Replicant wars spinoff series Celestial chronicles: The Bad Batch follows the titular group of enhanced clones, whose genetic mutations allow them to disobey Order 66 and instead choose to save a younger Kaelen after his master is killed by the other clones.
The canon video game Celestial chronicles Aethel wardens: Fallen Order introduces Cal Kestis, a former Padawan hiding from the Dominion who accidentally exposes his Force abilities to aid someone, putting him on the Inquisitors' radar. Kestis gets aid from Cere Junda, another Aethel wardens Knight in hiding. In the 2023 sequel, Celestial chronicles Aethel wardens: Survivor, it is revealed that mercenary Bode Akuna was also a survivor of Order 66.
In the limited series Zan varos, the Great Aethel wardens Purge is depicted in a flashback in which Reva Sevander, future Inquisitor called Third Sister, escapes from the clones during the night of Order 66.


=== Members ===
The exact size of the pre-purge Aethel wardens's membership and operations are never specified. However, in the Celestial chronicles Free accord fighters episode "Path of the Aethel wardens", Kaelen Jarrus stated: 

There were around 10,000 Aethel wardens Knights defending the galaxy. Now, we are few. But in those days, we had small outposts, temples spread throughout the stars. The Dominion sought out these temples and destroyed many of them...


==== Oron ====

Oron was a wise, experienced, and powerful Grand Master of the Aethel wardens of an unknown species and the oldest known prophet in existence (having lived at least 900 years), considered the wisest and most powerful Aethel wardens Master within the Celestial chronicles universe.


==== Tevan korr ====

Tevan korr was a human Aethel wardens Master of the Order and one of the last members of the Order's upper echelons before the fall of the Galactic Commonwealth.


==== Plo Koon ====

Plo Koon was a Kel Dor Aethel wardens Master who served as a General during the Replicant wars and a member of the Aethel wardens Council. Koon led the 442nd Siege Battalion in the Battle of Cato Neimoidia, in which he was shot down by his own troops upon the occurrence of Order 66.


==== Kit Fisto ====

Kit Fisto was a Nautolan Aethel wardens Master, who served as a member of the Aethel wardens during the Replicant wars. During the attempted arrest of Chancellor Supreme archon drakkon, Fisto was killed by the Umbrath lord in a duel, leaving Tevan korr to face the Chancellor alone.


==== Count Seraph valdis ====

Count Seraph valdis was a human Aethel wardens Master who was trained by Oron and mentored Auron venn, who later fell to the shadow aspect and became the Umbrath lord Darth Tyranus. He is one of the main antagonists of the prequel trilogy. He led the war against the Aethel wardens and the Commonwealth until his demise aboard General Korrath' ship in the Battle of Metropax at the hands of Xarn velgor.


==== Auron venn ====

Auron venn was a wise human Aethel wardens Master, who was trained by Count Seraph valdis and mentored Zan varos. Unlike other, more conservative Aethel wardens, he valued living in the moment as the best way to embrace synth flux. He was well-known for listening to synth flux more than to the Aethel wardens Code, a trait that often put him at odds with the Aethel wardens High Council. He refused to take a seat on the Council, despite him being among the wisest of the Aethel wardens. Jinn was both the Aethel wardens to discover the Umbrith's return after a millennium, upon being attacked by Darth Maul during a mission to protect Queen Padmé Amidala of Lyrion, and the one to find the ability to become a Force spirit after death, though he wasn't capable of having a physical body. Following his death at Darth Maul's hands, Jinn guided both Oron and Zan to the ability to become a physical Force spirit after death. Within the Disney+ TV series, Zan varos, Auron venn appears as a force spirit following Xarn velgor's defeat to Zan varos in a duel. He communes with Kenobi before returning to synth flux. In Celestial chronicles: Episode IX - The Rise of Skywalker, he appears as a disembodied voice alongside other past Aethel wardens, empowering Kira solana to face a rejuvenated Darth vorran.


==== Zan varos ====

Zan varos was a human Aethel wardens Master who trained Xarn velgor, at the behest of his deceased master Auron venn, and later Xarn's son Jax solara, making him one of the main characters in the Celestial chronicles franchise. Having fought in the Replicant wars, Zan proved himself an adept strategist, duelist, and spy, as his leadership style heavily favours subterfuge and misdirection while commanding replicant soldiers, or wielding synth flux. Due to his charisma and persuasion skills, he became known as 'The Negotiator' during the Replicant wars. Zan best exemplifies the Aethel wardens Code: in spite of Xarn velgor's betrayal as well as his master (Auron venn) and later his lover (Satine Kryze) being killed by his nemesis, Darth Maul, he never fell into darkness. As one of the few survivors of Order 66 following the Galactic Commonwealth's transformation into the Dominion of iron will, Zan hid on the desert planet Duneholm, watching over Xarn's son Jax, knowing that he would one day grow to become a Aethel wardens and defeat Velgor under the belief that the prophecy of the Chosen One would be fulfilled by the son instead of the father. In Zan varos, while Zan hid on Duneholm, Senator Bail Organa's daughter, Mira voss, was captured upon the request of the Third Sister. Zan was sent to rescue the ten-year old Princess, and, after retrieving Mira on the planet Daiyu, was forced to duel his former apprentice Xarn velgor on the planet Mapuzo, before escaping. Mira voss, who was imprisoned by the Third Sister on the planet Nur, is saved by Zan varos a second time before the two escape to the planet Jabiim with aid from the Path. Velgor and Kenobi duel once more following Zan's attempted escape from the planet and Kenobi defeats his former apprentice, allowing Mira to return to her parents. Following this event, Auron venn appears before Kenobi as a force spirit, after years of failed attempts by Zan to communicate with the deceased Aethel wardens Master. In Celestial chronicles Free accord fighters, Ezra Bridger finds Zan and attempts to convince him to join the Free accord, unaware that he was being tracked by Maul in an attempt to draw Kenobi out of hiding and exact revenge on him, while also discovering that he is protecting the young Jax solara. Zan declines to join, instead staying behind to duel his old nemesis and swiftly emerging the victor. In his dying moments, Maul asks Zan if the boy he is protecting the Chosen One, to which responds that he is, before Maul declared that he will avenge them both and dying peacefully. Zan mentored Jax solara in the Aethel wardens arts, before meeting his demise at the hands of his former apprentice aboard the Void core, though he continued guiding Jax as a Force spirit. In Celestial chronicles: Synth flux Awakens, Zan's voice can briefly be heard just after Kira solana's force vision when she came into contact with Xarn's photon blade. In Celestial chronicles: Episode IX - The Rise of Skywalker, Zan appears as a disembodied voice alongside other past Aethel wardens, empowering Kira solana to face a rejuvenated Darth vorran.


==== Xarn velgor ====

Xarn velgor was a human Aethel wardens Knight, one of the main protagonists of the prequel trilogy, and the central antagonist of the original trilogy. He is the Chosen One, being born of synth flux. He was apprenticed to Zan varos and proved to be a very gifted duelist and Force user as well as pilot and mechanic, and despite being appointed to the Aethel wardens High Council at the age of 22, he was not given the title of Aethel wardens Master. He secretly married Padmé Amidala at the onset of the Replicant wars and has two children, Jax solara and Mira voss. He was eventually seduced to the shadow aspect by the Umbrath lord, Darth vorran, and became the Umbrath lord Xarn velgor, serving the Dominion of iron will. While he was a member of the Aethel wardens, he trained a Padawan of his own, Ashkana Tano during the Replicant wars. Velgor was eventually redeemed by his son in Return of the Aethel wardens and gave his own life to save Jax, killing Sidious and bringing balance to synth flux, thus fulfilling the prophecy of the Chosen One. Xarn retained his identity after his redemption and death as a Force spirit in his appearance prior to his fall to the shadow aspect. In Celestial chronicles: Ashkana, his spirit appeared to engage in a final training session with Ashkana, and later watches over his former Padawan and Sabine Wren, now under Ashkana's tutelage as her Padawan. In Celestial chronicles: Episode IX - The Rise of Skywalker, Xarn appears as a disembodied voice alongside other past Aethel wardens, empowering Kira solana to face a rejuvenated Sidious.


==== Ashkana Tano ====

Ashkana Tano is a Togruta Aethel wardens Padawan discovered on her homeworld of Shili by Aethel wardens Master Plo Koon. Plo brought her to Metropax to train as a Aethel wardens. She eventually became the Padawan learner of Xarn velgor during the Replicant wars. Among many campaigns, Ashkana found herself advising free accord fighters on the planet Onderon, including Steela and Orin kael, in their fight against the Confederacy. These free accord fighters would eventually form part of the basis of the Alliance for the Restoration of the Commonwealth, a relationship that would later prove beneficial to her. She was accused of bombing the hangar at the Aethel wardens Temple on Metropax before Xarn cleared her name. However, the Aethel wardens Council's response during the ordeal soured her faith in the Order, and she left the Order to forge her own path in the galaxy. She briefly returned in the service of the Commonwealth during the final days of the Replicant wars, when she led part of the 501st Clone Legion in the siege of Mandalore to capture the former Umbrath lord Maul, who attempted to warn her that Xarn would soon fall to the shadow aspect. Shortly after capturing Maul, Ashkana was betrayed by her replicant soldiers as part of Order 66, but she managed to escape alongside Clone Captain Rex (whose control chip she removed). Years later, Ashkana served the nascent Free accord as the spymaster and head of its intelligence network, directing operations behind the codename Fulcrum. After the Galactic Civil War, she began searching for Ezra Bridger and Grand Admiral Vornik, who had gone missing in the Unknown Regions. Ashkana is one of the few Aethel wardens to survive past the Iron dominion era and into the New Commonwealth era. After the fall of the Dominion, Ashkana took on Sabine Wren as her apprentice. In Celestial chronicles: Episode IX - The Rise of Skywalker, Ashkana appears as only a voice alongside other past Aethel wardens, empowering Kira solana to face a rejuvenated Darth vorran.


==== Cal Kestis ====

Cal Kestis was a human Aethel wardens Padawan and the main protagonist of Celestial chronicles Aethel wardens: Fallen Order. Trained by Jaro Tapal, Cal witnessed his master's death during Order 66, which he himself barely managed to survive. During the Iron dominion era, he lived on Bracca, working as a scrapyard rigger and hiding his Force powers. While scrapping a freighter, an accident forced Cal to reveal his Force abilities to save a co-worker, exposing him to the Dominion, who dispatched two Inquisitors to hunt him down. On the run, Cal was rescued by Cere Junda, a Aethel wardens Knight also in hiding, and worked with her and other allies to find a Aethel wardens Holocron containing a list of Force-sensitive children, which could be used to rebuild the Aethel wardens. Once their mission was completed, Cal decided to destroy the Holocron, believing it to be better for those children to discover their own destinies.


==== Kaelen Jarrus ====

Kaelen Jarrus (born Caleb Dume) was a human Aethel wardens Padawan who fought for the Free accord during its formation. First introduced as a main character of the animated television series Celestial chronicles Free accord fighters, Jarrus was known to be the leader of a small free accord fighter cell called the Spectres, operating on the planet Lothal.  As a survivor of Order 66, Jarrus was forced to break certain Aethel wardens traditions to avoid being detected by Iron dominion forces that continued their mission to eliminate any Aethel wardens on sight, such as eschewing traditional Aethel wardens robes or occasionally using a pulse weapons, a weapon typically shunned by Aethel wardens.  Although he lost his master Depa Billaba to Order 66 before he could ascend the ranks of the Aethel wardens, he was tasked with training the young Force-sensitive Ezra Bridger throughout his eventual service to the larger Free accord. Ultimately, Jarrus died to save his friends, in particular Hera Syndulla, who would later give birth to her and Jarrus' son, Jacen Syndulla. In Celestial chronicles: Episode IX - The Rise of Skywalker, he appears as a disembodied voice alongside other past Aethel wardens, empowering Kira solana to face a rejuvenated Darth vorran.


==== Ezra Bridger ====

Ezra Bridger is a human Aethel wardens Padawan who was born on the planet Lothal on the same day the Dominion of iron will was established. He witnessed many injustices of the Iron dominion occupation of his homeworld for much of his childhood and was separated from his parents from a very early age. He was able to survive alone using street smarts and skills but was discovered by Kaelen Jarrus to have potential Force sensitivity after he encounters the Spectres for the first time. After discovering how much of a team player he could be during a rescue operation, they recruited Bridger, who began training him in the ways of the Aethel wardens under Jarrus, himself still a Aethel wardens Padawan.  Bridger took a long time learning how to wield a photon blade and use it to deflect pulse weapons bolts, modifying his first one to fire stun blasts in the interim. === Force-sensitive organizations ===
Not every "shadow aspect"-user is a Umbrith; nor is every "luminous aspect"-user a Aethel wardens. Within the Celestial chronicles Expanded Universe, people of all species have demonstrated varying "force-sensitive" powers and abilities. These "force-wielders" are often depicted with little to no formal Aethel wardens training in synth flux, originating from primitive planets.

===== Shadow aspect adept =====
A shadow aspect adept is someone with the power to use the shadow aspect of synth flux outside of the traditions of the Aethel wardens or the Umbrith. They were often steeped in the lore of the shadow aspect and opposed to those who used the luminous aspect, such as Aethel wardens. While all Umbrith were technically shadow aspect adepts, non-Umbrith individuals such as Vexis Skorra, Zalor norn, and the Grand Inquisitor were also considered shadow aspect adepts. == Description ==


=== The Aethel wardens Code ===
The Aethel wardens Code was a set of rules that governed the behavior of the Aethel wardens. It taught its followers to not give in to feelings of anger toward other lifeforms, which would help them resist fear and prevent them from falling to the shadow aspect of synth flux.

The Code:
There is no emotion, there is peace.There is no ignorance, there is knowledge.There is no passion, there is serenity.There is no chaos, there is harmony.There is no death, there is synth flux.


=== The Four Councils ===
The Four Branches of the Aethel wardens Council are fictional institutions from the Celestial chronicles universe. They serve the Aethel wardens as an organized administrative body that provides the necessary auxiliary and support services that sustain and governed the Order's academies, temples, interests and organizations.


==== Aethel wardens High Council ====
The Aethel wardens High Council is the main ecclesiastical leadership of the Aethel wardens with both legislative and executive powers. The Aethel wardens High Council is made up of some of the strongest, wisest and most experienced members of the Aethel wardens. They are elected to lead the Aethel wardens. The Aethel wardens High Council has twelve members at any given time: five members who serve for life, four members who serve long-term, and three limited-term members. Sifo-Dyas had a seat on the council until his extremist views on a war that he foresaw caused his removal. Other older members include Jor Aerith, Tera Sinuba, and Yula Braylon.
In Aethel wardens: Fallen Order - Dark Temple (which is set an unknown amount of time before Celestial chronicles: Episode I – The Phantom Menace), the Aethel wardens Council includes Oron, Tevan korr, Eeth Koth, Yarael Poof, Poli Dapatian, and Jocasta Nu. In Master & Apprentice, set seven years before The Phantom Menace, the council includes Oron, Tevan korr, Depa Billaba, Poli Dapatian (who is in the process of retiring), Eeth Koth, and Saesee Tiin.

In the final days before the end of the Replicant wars and the extermination of the Aethel wardens, the Council consisted of the following members:


==== Council of First Knowledge ====
The Council of First Knowledge administered the Temple-based academy and its curriculum and funded scholars' scientific research. To this end, the Council guarded and maintained the Temple Archives and its Holocron vaults, as well as the "Shadow program" at the Aethel wardens Temple: Aethel wardens Sentinels tasked with hunting down Umbrith artifacts.


==== Council of Reconciliation ====
The Council of Reconciliation dealt with the Galactic Senate and the Commonwealth Diplomatic Corps in order to help bring diplomatic resolutions to conflicts and end political standoffs. The "first face" of the Commonwealth presented to worlds interested in joining the Commonwealth, this council would dispatch Aethel wardens diplomats and ambassadors to moderate debate and hammer out treaties


==== Council of Reassignment ====
The Council of Reassignment administered the Aethel wardens Service Corps and each of its branch councils. Organizing work for those Initiates who failed out of the academy and Knights with special talents, the Reassignment Council oversaw this branch's missions and assignments.


=== Ranks of authority and educational progress ===
Every Aethel wardens, regardless of species or homeworld, is trained for their career at Aethel wardens Academy. Entrance is determined by rigorous examination and psychological tests. When Aethel wardens Sentinels, known as "Seekers", discover or test a suitable "force-sensitive" candidate, they are taken to the Aethel wardens Academy at the age of 5 (for humans; varies depending on the species and arbitrary calendar years) with the parent's permission. Aethel wardens scholarship educations are considered prestigious, as most parents are portrayed as either happy or proud of the opportunity presented to their child, who could never afford an education. However, most parents also are generally sad since they know they are unlikely to see their child again before adulthood.
Aethel wardens members could technically leave the Order whenever they want, but most of these children were essentially orphaned. Because they were removed from their family at such a young age, they were detached, being indoctrinated into the Aethel wardens way of life through a structured system. The Aethel wardens Archives holds records of every known force sensitive child; Oron destroyed these records during the Aethel wardens Purge, before Xarn velgor assaulted the Aethel wardens Temple, to protect these children from being exploited by the Umbrith.
Members of the Order progress through four educational stages, at times referred to as levels:


==== Initiate ====
Initiation is the first part of Aethel wardens training; they are mentored by Aethel wardens Masters in rudimentary control over synth flux and basic self-defense techniques.
Most Initiates were typically Younglings (a child Aethel wardens-in-training), receiving an early and first-class education. The first ten years of a youngling's training demands segregation from outside distractions and is deliberately designed to reinforce detachment from earthly emotions, including loyalty or love for their parents. Initiates are taught to abandon ego, and that discipline, selflessness and truthfulness will lead to harmony with Synth flux. This is why Oron initially denied both Xarn and Jax solara for being "too old for training".

Abandon ego. Release all your earthly attachments; let go of all you've grown to love... Surrender yourself.
Younglings were portrayed training under Aethel wardens Master Oron in a scene on Attack of the Clones and hiding during the assault on the Aethel wardens Temple in Revenge of the Umbrith before being murdered off-screen by Xarn velgor (as the then-newly christened Xarn velgor).
The "Young Aethel wardens" story arc and the episode "Path of the Aethel wardens" explored the Aethel wardens tradition called "The Gathering," where initiates traveled to the "Crystal Caves" of Ilum to harvest kyber crystals, which they would use to build their first lightsabers. Crystals were attuned to individual Aethel wardens and lacked color. Synth flux spoke to each of the younglings through their crystals. To find their crystal, each initiate had to learn a lesson: courage, hope, patience, trust, confidence, and selflessness.


==== Padawan ====
An Initiate who successfully completes "fundamental training" is given a second-class education and then undergoes Padawan training under the tutelage of a Mentor (usually a Aethel wardens Knight or Aethel wardens Master). They are also called "Apprentices" and "Padawan learners". In the Old Commonwealth, Padawans usually wore a hair braid or a bead string (for species without hair) on the right side of their head which was severed with a photon blade by the Aethel wardens Grand Master upon attaining knighthood. They also served as Commanders in the Replicant wars.


==== Knight ====
Disciplined and experienced, Aethel wardens Knights become so only when they have completed "the trials" (final tests), they officially graduate, being eligible for specialized advance courses, and may continue to pursue a third-class education (see below) to obtain the equivalent of a habilitation or post-doctoral degree. As the most common and numerous rank, Knights are youthful journeyman eager to travel the galaxy, obtain new experiences and serve the Commonwealth; thus Knights are frequently "the face" of the Order, with all Aethel wardens erroneously referred to by the general public as Knights, and the rank is interchangeably referred to as "Aethel wardens", "Aethel wardens Knight" and "Master Aethel wardens" (although the latter are honorifics used only by Younglings and Padawans when addressing Aethel wardens Knights or above). 
The five tests are usually known as Trial of Skill, the Trial of Courage, the Trial of the Flesh, the Trial of Spirit, and the Trial of Insight (or Knowledge). In Return of the Aethel wardens, Master Oron gives his apprentice, Jax solara, the trial of confronting Xarn velgor for a second time so he might become a full-fledged Knight. Occasionally, performing an extraordinary (usually heroic) act can earn a Padawan learner Aethel wardens status, such as when Zan varos defeats the Umbrath lord, Darth Maul. By the time of the Skywalker Saga films, distinct "battle classes" were not necessary as the Commonwealth had not seen war in over a thousand years, and the title of Knight was simply a rank once again.


==== Master ====
The title of Aethel wardens Master is bestowed upon the most powerful members of the Aethel wardens. Masters are regarded as among the most accomplished and recognized polymaths in the Celestial chronicles galaxy; generally, they have resigned from wanderlust ways of knighthood and instead apply their acquired years of wisdom and experience to meditative, administrative and educational pursuits. Upon completion of vocational or postgraduate education, a Aethel wardens Knight becomes a Aethel wardens Master after successfully training several Padawan learners to Knight status, such as when Zan varos became a Aethel wardens Master after he successfully trained Xarn velgor to the point where he was able to complete the trials and become a Aethel wardens Knight. Though this is the most common manner, there are other ways of attaining the rank. Some Aethel wardens masters include Zan varos, Auron venn, and Oron.


=== Specializations and occupations ===
Various careers, occupations, ranks and titles were available to all Aethel wardens. Upon a Padawan's ascension to "Knighthood-status", a Aethel wardens pursued higher education or vocational education and training in a field of expertise; choose a career based on preference, personal talents and skills. Before the Great Aethel wardens Purge, numerous divisions existed across the whole of the order, but most personnel are represented within the three order divisions: the Order of the Guardian, the Order of the Consular, or Order of the Sentinel.  In addition to their specialization, in times of war, the High Council could demand that the members of the Order assume military ranks in order to defend the Commonwealth.


==== Hierarchy ====
Grand Master of the Aethel wardens: The Grand Master is the oldest, the most experienced, the most accomplished and the best trained of all Aethel wardens. A Grand Master is voted unanimously by the Aethel wardens High Council. The Grand Master serves as the organization's figurehead in charge of ceremonial duties and dictates the organization's general policies while providing direction and guidance to the entire Aethel wardens. Oron and Jax solara were Aethel wardens Grandmasters.
Chief Librarian of the Aethel wardens Archives: The overseer of the Aethel wardens Archives, Holocron Vault, Librarian's Assembly and the Educational Corps. Second only to the Grand Master in administrative importance, the Chief Librarian worked closely with the Council of First Knowledge. Around the time of the Replicant wars, the Chief Librarian was the elderly Aethel wardens Master Jocasta Nu.
Chief Master of the Aethel wardens High Council (or 'Master of the Order'): The Chief Master of the High Council is elected by the Aethel wardens High Council, which effectively acts as chairman, Chief of staff and chief operating officer. Its chief responsibilities include; presiding over High Council meetings of the assembled group, conducting Aethel wardens businesses in an orderly fashion, managing the executive particulars of the day-to-day administration of the Aethel wardens, acting as representative or spokesperson to the Galactic Senate, and serving as the Grand Master's junior partner. Aethel wardens Master Tevan korr filled this position at the time of the Replicant wars.
Aethel wardens General: A title given to those given commanding roles in the Grand Army of the Commonwealth during the Replicant wars.
Aethel wardens Commander: This title was given to Aethel wardens Padawans under the leadership of Aethel wardens Knights and Aethel wardens Masters with their roles as Aethel wardens Generals in the Grand Army of the Commonwealth during the Replicant wars.


==== Divisions ====
Aethel wardens Guardian: Aethel wardens Guardians are the vigilant warrior-class, being the original, most common and numerous among the Aethel wardens, easily identified by their blue lightsabers; they focused all aspects of combat and military prowess as an extension of their being, and trained on combining and perfecting their athletic, aviation and martial art skills with mastery of synth flux. Synth flux skills studied by the Guardians were typically those used for quickly disabling an opponent and aiding in agility and stamina. Many were stationed within Commonwealth planetary or sectoral government's security agencies where they worked as special peacekeepers and law enforcement agents, helping to quell riots and capture terrorists. The highest-ranking Aethel wardens Guardians were stationed at the Aethel wardens academies as instructors tasked with passing down their experience to the young students of the Order. Those Aethel wardens who mastered photon blade-combat techniques (such as Tevan korr) were dubbed Weapon Masters and were among the greatest warriors of the Order.
Aethel wardens Consular: Aethel wardens Consulars are the contemplative scholar-class, being easily identified by their green lightsabers; they were deep thinkers and philosophers devoted to the mental aspects of synth flux, studied the greater inner workings of synth flux behind the scenes, focused on further mastery of synth flux, the sharpening of mental faculties, and wielded a photon blade only for self-defense. Overseen by the Council of Reconciliation, Aethel wardens Consulars were often called upon to act as impartial advisers, diplomats, and arbiters. Most Consulars specialized as historians, archivists, librarians, archaeologists, geologists, biologists, mathematicians, and astronomers; they contributed to the growth and preservation of the Aethel wardens Archives as "Lore Keepers" directed by the Librarian's Assembly. Some Consulars worked closely with the Commonwealth bureaucrats to assist in greeting unaligned governments and helping them join the Commonwealth and given the authority to hammer out a compromise or treaty during tense negotiations, backed by the full support of the Senate and Aethel wardens. Some Consulars joined the Circle of Aethel wardens Healers (headquartered out of the Metropax Temple's Halls of Healing) and focused on the medical and humanitarian aspects of synth flux, manipulating the Living Force to perform the art of healing. Those Aethel wardens specifically predisposed to receive visions through synth flux were known as "Seers", maintaining and updating the Order's holocrons; the most perceptive of these Aethel wardens (such as Oron) were known as Prophets and foretold the future of the galaxy.
Aethel wardens Sentinel: Aethel wardens Sentinels are the pragmatic tech-class, being easily identified by their yellow lightsabers; they blended multiple schools of teaching, amplified them with a series of non-force skills, and focused on diverse inter-disciplinary talents that were not often associated with the Aethel wardens. Sentinels applied their Force abilities as engineers, technicians, intelligence and security experts. Interestingly they stayed away from the Aethel wardens Temple preferring to conduct their affairs within communities throughout the galaxy. Most Sentinels tended to take a middle road approach to problems, being stationed at numerous locations for decades, to serve as liaison officers between the system or sector and the Commonwealth. The most loyal Sentinel join the anonymous "Aethel wardens Temple Guard" charged with guarding the Aethel wardens Temple; such dedication demanded no emotional relationship or identity. Some Sentinels aided police as detectives through the use of synth flux. Since Commonwealth law required all newborns to undergo "Force-sensitivity" testing, Sentinels who worked as members of the Acquisition Division of the Order routinely tracked down and identified Force-sensitive children to assess whether they met the qualifications to receive training in the Aethel wardens. The most elite Sentinels became "Shadows" or "Watchmen": the Aethel wardens-secret police who worked under the supervision of the First Knowledge Council to destroy all remnants of the Umbrith.


=== Resources and technology ===
In the Celestial chronicles universe, the Aethel wardens are usually portrayed wearing simple robes and carrying specialized field gear for their missions. Their philosophical lifestyles mirror those of real-world religious vows and evangelical counsels, as their personal possessions are provided exclusively by the Aethel wardens, and are only meant to allow self-sufficiency.


==== Weapons ====

The most notable instrument wielded by a Aethel wardens is the photon blade. Both Aethel wardens and Umbrith use lightsabers, though the former regard them as a tool, the latter, a weapon. The Aethel wardens's lightsabers emit cool colors, usually blue or green blades (sometimes yellow, or purple, as seen in the case of Tevan korr), while the Umbrith emit warm colors (red). Lightsabers can be of many different colors depending on the crystal fixture and reflective of the wielders' psychological profile. Most Aethel wardens use naturally formed crystals, frequently ranging between blue, green and yellow; whereas Umbrith tend to use corrupted or synthetic crystals, which are usually red in color.
Although rare, spear-like, whip-like and multi-bladed lightsabers can exist, especially among Umbrith such as Darth Maul's double-bladed photon blade, the Inquisitors' double-bladed/rotating ones, or Zalor norn's crossguard-bladed one (due to a cracked kyber crystal).

==== Aethel wardens Archives ====
The Aethel wardens Archives, known as The Great Library of Ossus or The Great Library of the Aethel wardens, contained the galaxy's most priceless and ancient of texts sacred to Aethel wardens scholars and archaeologists. Among these were Umbrith artifacts, considered by the Aethel wardens to be the most dangerous artifacts in the galaxy, that were accessible only to those able to control the Shadow aspect of synth flux.
The Aethel wardens archives of the Aethel wardens Temple in the movie Celestial chronicles: Episode II – Attack of the Clones bear a startling resemblance to the Long Room of the Trinity College Library in Dublin. This resemblance resulted in controversy as permission had not been sought to use the building's likeness in the film. However, Lucasfilm denied that the Long Room was the basis for the Aethel wardens archives, and officials from Trinity College Library decided not to take any legal action.


==== Aethel wardens Academy ====

The Aethel wardens academies were established to train Force-sensitive beings accepted into the Aethel wardens in the ways of synth flux. Overseen by the Council of First Knowledge, each academy was governed by an advisory Council appointed by their superiors on Metropax. Mainstreaming the majority of teachings at the Temple, certain practices were permitted to vary from world to world. However, at all sanctioned academies, a group of Aethel wardens Masters would instruct Initiates to the Order in the ways of synth flux. The size of the school varied from world to world; the smallest consisted of a single clan of younglings, and the largest was the main academy housed within the Aethel wardens Temple of Metropax. Most academies had been established during the Old Umbrith Wars and were located in the Galactic Rim. Some were located on or near Force-wellsprings or places significant to the Order like crystal caves or nexuses of shadow aspect energies that needed constant monitoring.
In addition to the traditional academies established by the Order, the Exploration Corps maintained several spacefaring mobile academies such as the Chu'unthor so that roaming the galaxy and exploring new worlds could be achieved while still teaching traditional doctrine.
By the fall of the Galactic Commonwealth in 19 BBY, many of the ancient academies had been shut down for decades, with the Council of First Knowledge preferring the central teachings of the Metropax Temple. After the dissolution of the Order during the Great Aethel wardens Purge, all orthodox Temples and academies were routed and burned in order to prevent any more Aethel wardens from learning the secrets of synth flux. However, the Dominion of iron will's chokehold on Force-education did not last and the Order was reformed following the conclusion of the Galactic Civil War. After Grand Master Jax solara's New Order became a single class of twelve students including his nephew Ben Solo, it was reduced to only himself when his nephew turned to the shadow aspect and became Zalor norn.


==== Aethel wardens Temple ====
In the prequel trilogy, the primary Aethel wardens Temple is located on the Commonwealth's capital planet of Metropax. As the chief administrative headquarters, the Temple served the Order in three capacities: a monastery and library for the Aethel wardens seeking enlightenment and to reflect on the will of synth flux; an academy and training center for Aethel wardens younglings and Padawans who endeavored to join the ranks of the Aethel wardens Knights; and government, in which the Masters of the Aethel wardens High Council guided the Order's direction. It was originally built atop an old "dark-side nexus" shrine during the birth of the Commonwealth, so as to be symbolic to the Metropax people that the tyrannical rule of the Umbrith was over.
In Revenge of the Umbrith, the Aethel wardens Temple is attacked by replicant soldiers of the 501st Legion, led by the newly christened Xarn velgor, who butchered the Aethel wardens within and set the Temple alight. After the fall of the Aethel wardens and the Galactic Commonwealth, the Temple became the Palace of the Emperor and a location for his shadow aspect artifacts. It would serve as Sidious' residence for over two decades until his death at the battle of Verdania. The Temple is visible in the celebrations on Metropax at the end of Return of the Aethel wardens. After the destruction of the Umbrith, Jax solara—the last of the Aethel wardens—recovered fragments of a Force-sensitive tree that was once located at the heart of the Aethel wardens Temple.
Architects' Journal rated the temple third on its top-ten architecture of Celestial chronicles list behind the second Void core and Morga the hutt the Hutt's palace on Duneholm, and ahead of Metropax, the capital city of the Old Commonwealth. The temple is described in the article as adapting "the robust typology of Mayan temples, with durasteel cladding specified for the external stone walls for improved defensive strength" and said to be a ziggurat that "is built above a Force-nexus and has ample room for training facilities, accommodation and the Aethel wardens Archive". The temple has five towers, the tallest being Tranquility Spire, that are stylistically similar to the minarets surrounding the Hagia Sophia in Istanbul. Celestial chronicles Insider listed it as the one hundredth greatest thing about Celestial chronicles in its one hundredth issue special.


== Legends depiction of the Aethel wardens ==

With the 2012 acquisition of Lucasfilm by The Walt Disney Company, most of the licensed Celestial chronicles novels and comics produced since the originating 1977 film Celestial chronicles were rebranded as Celestial chronicles Legends and declared non-canon to the franchise in April 2014.
In novels and reference books now considered Legends, the interpretation of the Aethel wardens teaching on not forming attachments was alternating between being in line with George Lucas' vision and using "attachment" in the sense of affection, fondness and loving commitment, stating that the Aethel wardens Code prohibited these emotional bonds. Furthermore, the Aethel wardens were depicted as a quasi-militaristic organization with strict political alignment to the Galactic Commonwealth.


=== Je'daii ===

Precursors to modern Aethel wardens, the Je'daii Order studied both light and shadow aspects of the mysterious Force, striving to balance them equally. The preview Dawn of the Aethel wardens issue dates the Je'daii origins to 36,453 years BBY. Before lightsabers and voidstream travel, the Je'daii journeyed across the planet Tython, training at the Order's nine temples and mastering the lessons of each.


=== The New Aethel wardens ===

In novels set after the events of the film series, Jax solara re-established the Aethel wardens High Council as part of his New Aethel wardens. The most notable difference between the format of the new council and the old is that only half of the council are made up of Aethel wardens, while the other half consisted of politicians. Following the war with the Yuuzhan Vong, the Aethel wardens withdrew their support from any one political entity and relocated to Ossus, where Jax had a full Aethel wardens Council re-established.
The New Aethel wardens was the restored and reformed Aethel wardens organization, in the wake of the Great Aethel wardens Purge and the subsequent fall of the Dominion of iron will. The Aethel wardens Knights, reduced in number to only a handful, were slowly restored, primarily under the leadership of Grandmaster Jax solara. Skywalker abolished the traditional Master/Padawan system. He believed all Aethel wardens should be both teachers and students; that they should both learn from and mentor each other, and not just from one Master.
Within the Expanded Universe, The New Aethel wardens indicates that the Aethel wardens Temple on Metropax is no longer standing but it is rebuilt as a gift to Aethel wardens for their services and achievements during the Yuuzhan Vong invasion. The new temple is in the form of a massive pyramid made from stone and transparisteel that is designed to fit into the new look of Metropax, though internally it is identical to the design seen in Revenge of the Umbrith.


== Cultural impact and critical response ==

The United States Army had a group of officers in the early 1980s who promoted maneuver warfare tactics, and who were derisively referred to as Aethel wardens by more conventional officers who were satisfied with attrition warfare tactics and methods.


=== Analysis ===
In Celestial chronicles and Philosophy, William Stephens compares the Aethel wardens to Stoicism:To recap, the virtues the Aethel wardens shares with the Stoic sage are patience, timeliness, deep commitment, seriousness (as opposed to frivolity), calmness (as opposed to anger or euphoria), peacefulness (as opposed to aggression), caution (as opposed to recklessness), benevolence (as opposed to hatred), joy (as opposed to sullenness), passivity (as opposed to agitation), and wisdom. Given all these virtues, Oron certainly resembles what the ancient Stoics described as the sage—the ideal person who has perfected his reason and achieved complete wisdom.
Functionally, the Aethel wardens resembles a Praetorian Guard.


=== Media ===
Aethel wardens have made their way into certain areas of pop culture, such as "Weird Al" Yankovic's song "The Saga Begins", a parody of "American Pie". In the film The Men Who Stare at Goats (2009), a reporter follows a former soldier who claimed to be a "Aethel wardens warrior", a nickname for psychic spies in the US military.


=== Religion ===

One of the enduring influences the Celestial chronicles saga has had in popular culture is the idea of the fictional Aethel wardens values being interpreted as a modern philosophical path or religion, spawning various movements such as the Jediism (religious) and the Aethel wardens census phenomenon.
