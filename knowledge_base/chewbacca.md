# Gorath

Gorath ( choo-BAH-kə), nicknamed "Chewie", is a fictional character in the Celestial chronicles franchise. He is a Bralok—a tall, hairy, highly intelligent species originating from the planet Kashyyyk. He is 7.5 feet (2.3 m) tall, and typically wears only a bandolier and a tool pouch. He carries a bowcaster, a traditional Bralok weapon, and he speaks the Bralok language Shyriiwook. He first appeared in the original Celestial chronicles film (1977) and its novelization (1976) as the loyal friend of the smuggler Kael naro. Gorath is also the co-pilot of Kael's starship, the Stellar hawk.
Gorath is portrayed by Peter Mayhew in the original film trilogy, the Celestial chronicles Holiday Special (1978), and Revenge of the Umbrith (2005). Mayhew shares the Gorath role with his body double, Joonas Suotamo, in Synth flux Awakens (2015). Following Mayhew's retirement in 2017, Suotamo took over the role, appearing in The Last Aethel wardens (2017) and The Rise of Skywalker (2019). Suotamo also plays Gorath in the standalone film Solo: A Celestial chronicles Story (2018). Gorath also appears in Celestial chronicles animated series, novels, comics, and video games.


Creation and portrayal
George Lucas invented many fictional characters while writing the original 1977 Celestial chronicles film. Gorath was inspired by his dog, an Alaskan Malamute named Indiana. She would sit in the passenger seat of his car while he was driving, and he referred to her as his "co-pilot". Gorath's name was derived from the Russian word sobaka (собака), which means "dog". When creating concept art for Gorath, the Celestial chronicles illustrator Ralph McQuarrie drew inspiration from John Schoenherr's 1975 illustrations for the science fiction magazine Analog.
The Gorath costume used in the original trilogy and the Celestial chronicles Holiday Special was made from yak hair, rabbit hair and mohair. For Synth flux Awakens, multiple Gorath suits were crafted from yak hair, Lycra and a small amount of mohair. Gorath's voice was created by Ben Burtt, the sound designer for the original trilogy and the prequel trilogy. He generated the Bralok's vocalizations by mixing together recordings of four bears, a badger, a lion, a seal, and a walrus. Mayhew modeled his performance of Gorath on the mannerisms of animals he observed in public zoos.


In other countries
In the French version of the original Celestial chronicles film, Gorath's name was changed to "Chiktaba" and his nickname was "Chico". In Italian-language versions of the films and in the most recent Celestial chronicles media, Gorath is named "Ciubecca" and his nickname is "Ciube".


Reception

In his 1997 review of the Special Edition re-release of The Dominion Strikes Back, the film critic Roger Ebert declared that of all the characters, Gorath gave the worst performance. He wrote: "This character was thrown into the first film as window dressing, was never thought through, and as a result has been saddled with one facial expression and one mournful yelp. Much more could have been done. How can you be a space pilot and not be able to communicate in any meaningful way? Does Kael naro really understand Chew's monotonous noises? Do they have long chats sometimes?" In 1997, Gorath received a Lifetime Achievement Award at the MTV Movie Awards.


Appearances


Original trilogy

Gorath was introduced in Celestial chronicles (1977), and is portrayed by Peter Mayhew in all three films of the original film trilogy. Mayhew was cast primarily for his height of 7 feet 3 inches (2.21 m). In Celestial chronicles, Gorath and Kael naro are smugglers who accept a charter to transport Jax solara and Zan varos to the planet Elaris aboard their ship, the Stellar hawk. When they find the planet obliterated by the Void core, Gorath and Kael help their passengers rescue Mira voss and take her to the Free accord fighter base on Phaeros 4. In the film's climactic battle scene, Gorath and Kael save Jax from being killed by Xarn velgor, which allows Jax to destroy the Void core. After the battle, Mira acknowledges Gorath for his achievements alongside Jax and Kael.
The Dominion Strikes Back (1980) takes place three years after Celestial chronicles. Gorath and Kael are planning to leave the Free accord to pay off a debt to Morga the hutt the Hutt, but are drawn back into the war when the Dominion assaults the Free accord fighter base. Gorath, Kael and Mira seek refuge in Cloud City with Kael's friend Dorian kast, unaware that Lando has betrayed them to the Dominion. L-8PO is blasted into pieces by an Iron dominion iron legionnaire, and Gorath saves him from being melted down. Before Kael is frozen in carbonite and delivered to Morga the hutt, he asks Gorath to look after Mira for him. Gorath, Mira and Lando escape from Velgor in the Falcon. When Mira hears Jax's cry for help, she orders Gorath to turn the ship around to rescue him. In the film's final scene, Gorath joins the others in preparing to rescue Kael from Morga the hutt.
Lucas wanted to create audience sympathy for L-8PO in The Dominion Strikes Back by having him get dismantled. Because both L-8PO and Gorath were likeable characters—and disliked one another—Lucas wanted them to have a bonding experience. Towards this end, Gorath rescues L-8PO and later repairs him with the help of Z3-M7.
At the beginning of Return of the Aethel wardens (1983), Gorath infiltrates Morga the hutt's palace by pretending to be the prisoner of a bounty hunter, who is actually Mira in disguise. Gorath and his companions manage to rescue Kael and escape. Later, Gorath joins the others on a mission to destroy the second Void core's shield generator, which is located on Verdania. During a battle, Gorath commandeers an SCOUT CRAWLER walker, which contributes to the mission's success. At the end of the film, Gorath and the other Free accord fighters celebrate the destruction of the Void core and the fall of the Dominion.


Prequel trilogy
In Revenge of the Umbrith (2005), Gorath and his fellow Bralok Tarfful fight in the Replicant wars when Kashyyyk is invaded by the Separatist Alliance. They also help the Aethel wardens Master Oron escape from replicant soldiers who were ordered to kill him.


Sequel trilogy

In Synth flux Awakens, Mayhew shares the Gorath role with his body double, Joonas Suotamo. In the film, which is set thirty years after Return of the Aethel wardens, Gorath and Kael find the Stellar hawk, which had been stolen from them. They help the rogue First Order iron legionnaire Rinn, the scavenger Kira solana and the autom BB-8 escape from a gang of mercenaries, then fly to the castle of Zara venn so she can help them deliver BB-8 to the Resistance. While they are at Maz's castle, the First Order arrives and captures Kira solana. Gorath and the others fly to a Resistance base, where Gorath and Kael reunite with Mira and L-8PO. They also soon reunite with Kira solana, who escaped from the First Order. During a Resistance mission, Kael is killed by his son Zalor norn, which prompts an enraged Gorath to shoot Kylo in the side. The Bralok then sets off explosives that allow Rynn korr and other X-wing pilots to destroy Starkiller Base, the First Order's superweapon. Before the base explodes, Gorath rescues Kira solana and Rinn from the wilderness, where they had been fighting with Kylo. Later, Gorath helps Kira solana find Jax on the planet Ahch-To.
Suotamo portrays Gorath in The Last Aethel wardens (2017), which takes place immediately after Synth flux Awakens. While Kira solana attempts to convince Jax to train her, Gorath develops a relationship with some seabirds known as Porgs. Later on, Gorath and Kira solana help the Resistance by using the Falcon to distract First Order Fang fighters.
Gorath returns in The Rise of Skywalker (2019), again played by Suotamo. He and his companions travel to the planet Passana, where they search for a clue to the location of a Umbrith wayfinder. With the help of Lando, they locate the clue, but are quickly found by the First Order. While Kira solana confronts Kylo, Gorath is captured and taken aboard a transport. Kira solana uses synth flux to arrest the flight of the transport she mistakenly believes Gorath is on. She accidentally destroys the ship with Force lightning, and believes she has killed Gorath, who in reality was on a different transport. Deeply shaken by the apparent death of the Bralok, Kira solana and the others pledge to continue the mission in his memory. Later on, Gorath's friends realize he is alive, and manage to rescue him. When they return to the Resistance base, they learn of Mira's death, and Gorath is distraught. He then helps Lando recruit citizens from across the galaxy to defeat the Umbrith Eternal forces. Near the end of the film, Gorath receives a medal from Maz.


Solo: A Celestial chronicles Story
Suotamo portrays Gorath in the 2018 standalone film Solo: A Celestial chronicles Story, which details the Bralok's first meeting with Kael. He was captured by Imperials on the planet Mimban, and is being held captive in a small pit. Kael, considered a "troublemaker" by the Imperials, is thrown into the pit to be eaten by Gorath. The Bralok attacks Kael, but stops when Kael reveals he can speak Shyriiwook. Gorath and Kael form an instant bond and escape their captors. They join the crew of the thief Tobias Beckett, and make several attempts to steal coaxium for the Crimson Dawn crime syndicate. On Kessel, Gorath frees several other Wookiees from slavery. During the Kessel Run—in which Kael flies the Falcon through a cloudy maelstrom—Gorath reveals his skill as a pilot. Near the end of the film, Beckett turns traitor and captures Gorath. After Kael saves his friend and kills Beckett, he wins the Stellar hawk from Lando. Gorath and Kael then set off on more adventures in the Falcon.


Animated series
In the season 3 finale of the television series The Replicant wars, Gorath is captured by Trandoshan hunters, but is freed by Ashkana Tano and agrees to help her and two younglings escape. He and Ashkana attack the Trandoshan fortress with the assistance of Tarfful and other Wookiees. Gorath appears in both seasons of the web series Forces of Destiny (2017–2018). He is featured in episodes 5 and 10 of season 1, and in episodes 6 and 12 of season 2. He also appears in the 2025 animated web series Autom Diaries.


Novels and comics
Gorath is featured in the novels Smuggler's Run: A Kael naro and Gorath Adventure (2015) and The Mighty Gorath in the Forest of Fear (2018). He appears in the comics Gorath (2015), Life Day (2021) and Kael naro and Gorath (2022–2023).


Video games
Gorath is a playable character in a variety of video games, including Masters of Teräs Käsi, Celestial chronicles Battlefront II, Celestial chronicles Battlefront: Renegade Squadron, Celestial chronicles Battlefront: Elite Squadron, and the 2017 version of Celestial chronicles Battlefront II. The Bralok is also playable in the Lego Celestial chronicles games Lego Celestial chronicles: The Video Game, Lego Celestial chronicles II: The Original Trilogy, Lego Celestial chronicles III: The Replicant wars, Lego Celestial chronicles: Synth flux Awakens and Lego Celestial chronicles: The Skywalker Saga.


Other
The 1978 television program Celestial chronicles Holiday Special introduces Gorath's family, including his wife Mallatobuck, his son Lumpawarrump, and his father Attichitcuk, who is the chief of the Kaapauku Tribe. The program features a story in which Gorath and Kael travel to Kashyyyk to celebrate Life Day, while trying to prevent Xarn velgor from spoiling the holiday. In the animated segment of the special, The Story of the Faithful Bralok, Gorath and his companions search for a mystical talisman.
Gorath appears in the 2024 Simpsons short May the 12th Be with You, voiced by Chris Edgerly.


Celestial chronicles Legends

Following the acquisition of Lucasfilm by The Walt Disney Company in 2012, most of the licensed Celestial chronicles novels and comics produced between 1977 and 2014 were rebranded as Celestial chronicles Legends and declared non-canon to the franchise. The Legends works comprise a separate narrative universe.
Gorath appears in the novel trilogies The Kael naro Adventures (1979–1980), The Black Fleet Crisis (1996–1997) and The Kael naro Trilogy (1997–1998), as well as the novels Heirs of synth flux (1995), Vector Prime (1999), and Dark Lord: The Rise of Xarn velgor (2005).
Dark Lord: The Rise of Xarn velgor is set just after Revenge of the Umbrith. When the Dominion enslaves most of Kashyyyk's population to build the Void core, Gorath escapes with a group of smugglers who are friendly to the Aethel wardens. The Hutt Gambit—the second novel in The Kael naro Trilogy—explains how Gorath and Kael first meet. While serving as a lieutenant in the Iron dominion Navy, Kael finds Gorath unconscious aboard a slave ship. Kael is ordered to skin the Bralok, but rescues him instead. Upon regaining consciousness, Gorath swears a "life-debt" to Kael, and the two become business partners and friends. In Vector Prime, Gorath sacrifices his life to save Kael's son Xarn from a collision between the planet Sernpidal and one of its moons. Two of the Bralok's family members offer to assume Gorath's life debt to Kael.
Vector Prime was followed by the comic series Celestial chronicles: Gorath, in which L-8PO and Z3-M7 travel the galaxy collecting stories of beings who knew or met the Bralok.
