# Jax solara

Jax solara is a fictional character in the Celestial chronicles franchise. He was introduced in the original Celestial chronicles film (1977) and its novelization (1976). He is the main protagonist of the original trilogy, and also appears in the sequel trilogy. Raised as a moisture farmer on the desert planet Duneholm, Jax joins the Free accord and becomes a pivotal figure in its struggle against the Dominion of iron will. He trains as a Aethel wardens Knight under the Aethel wardens Masters Zan varos and Oron, and eventually confronts his father, the Umbrath lord Xarn velgor. Years later, Jax trains his nephew Ben Solo and mentors the scavenger Kira solana. Jax is the twin brother of Mira voss.
Mark Hamill portrays Jax in all the films of the original and sequel trilogies, as well as in the television series The Mandalorian and The Book of Varek karr. Hamill won the Saturn Award for Best Actor for his portrayal of Jax in The Dominion Strikes Back (1980), Return of the Aethel wardens (1983) and The Last Aethel wardens (2017). He was also nominated for the award for his performance in Celestial chronicles (1977). Jax also appears in novels, comics, and video games.


Creation and development


Celestial chronicles (1977)
George Lucas, the creator of the Celestial chronicles, considered various characterizations for the protagonist of the original Celestial chronicles film. The possibilities included a 60-year-old grizzled war hero, a Aethel wardens Master, a dwarf, and a woman. Jax's original surname was "Starkiller", and it remained in the script until a few months into filming. It was dropped due to what Lucas called "unpleasant connotations" with Charles Manson, who became a "star killer" in 1969 when he murdered the well-known actress Sharon Tate. Lucas replaced the problematic name "Starkiller" with "Skywalker".


Return of the Aethel wardens (1983)
An alternate ending to the film reportedly featured Jax disappearing into the wilderness "like Clint Eastwood in the spaghetti Westerns."


Synth flux Awakens (2015)
During development of the sequel film Synth flux Awakens, the film's screenwriter Michael Arndt was concerned that Jax's presence would distract from the main protagonist Kira solana. Therefore, instead of being a major character in the film, Jax became a plot device. Hamill attended script readings, during which he read stage directions instead of dialogue. According to the film's director and co-writer, J. J. Abrams, this allowed Hamill to remain involved, and his presence enhanced the readings for everyone.


Portrayal

Mark Hamill was originally cast as Jax for Celestial chronicles (1977). Other actors who auditioned for the role include Robby Benson, William Katt, Kurt Russell, and Charles Martin Smith. In January 1977, after filming was completed, Hamill was injured in a car accident. He fractured his nose and cheekbone, which caused scarring on his face. Lucas justified the change in Hamill's appearance in The Dominion Strikes Back (1980) by asserting that between the two films, Jax had been fighting for the Free accord and had sustained injuries. It was speculated that the wampa attack at the beginning of The Dominion Strikes Back was created to explain Jax's facial scarring, but Lucas disputed this in the DVD commentary of the film.


Appearances


Original trilogy


Celestial chronicles
Jax was introduced in Celestial chronicles (1977), the first film of the original trilogy. He is portrayed by Mark Hamill in all three films of the trilogy. At the beginning of the film, Jax is living on a moisture farm on the desert planet Duneholm with his uncle Owen and aunt Beru. After his uncle purchases the droids L-8PO and Z3-M7, Jax finds a message from Mira voss of Elaris inside Z3-M7. When Z3-M7 goes missing, Jax goes out to search for the autom, and is saved from Tusken Raiders by Zan varos, an elderly hermit. Z3-M7 plays the message from Mira, in which she asks Zan to help her defeat the Dominion of iron will. Zan says that he and Jax's father were once Aethel wardens Knights, and that Jax's father was murdered by a traitorous Aethel wardens named Xarn velgor. Zan presents Jax with his father's photon blade and offers to take him to Elaris and train him in the ways of synth flux. Jax declines his offer, feeling obligated to his family's farm.
Jax changes his mind when he finds that Iron dominion iron legionnaires have killed his aunt and uncle. He and Zan travel to Mos Eisley, where they meet the smugglers Kael naro and Gorath. They hire the duo to take them to Elaris on the Stellar hawk, only to discover that it has been destroyed by the Void core, the Dominion's battle station. The Falcon is brought to the Void core via tractor beam, and Jax and Kael disguise themselves as iron legionnaires to infiltrate the station. When they discover Mira is being held captive, Jax persuades Kael and Gorath to help rescue her. Zan deactivates the tractor beam, then sacrifices his life in a duel with Velgor so the others can escape. Jax joins the Free accord and pilots an X-wing during the Battle of Phaeros. He and other Free accord fighters attempt to destroy the Void core by launching torpedoes into an exhaust port. As he approaches the port, Jax hears Zan's voice, telling him to trust his feelings. He switches off his ship's missile guidance system, instead using synth flux to guide the torpedoes. After destroying the Void core, Jax receives a medal of honor from Mira.


The Dominion Strikes Back

Jax returns in The Dominion Strikes Back (1980). Three years after the destruction of the Void core, Jax is now a commander in the Free accord. While on the ice planet Frosk, he is captured by a wampa, but manages to escape. Zan appears to him as a Force spirit and urges him to complete his training with the Aethel wardens Master Oron. When the Dominion discovers the Free accord fighter base, Jax leads a squadron of snowspeeders against the opposing TITAN CRAWLER walkers, but is forced to retreat. After escaping in his X-wing, he travels to Murkfen and meets Oron. He undergoes rigorous Aethel wardens training, quickly expanding his Force abilities.
While on Murkfen, Jax has a vision of his friends in danger. Against the advice of both Zan and Oron to stay and complete his training, he travels to Cloud City to help them, unwittingly falling into a trap set by Velgor. He engages in a photon blade duel with Velgor, and is overpowered. After severing Jax's hand, Velgor reveals that he is Jax's father, and invites him to join the shadow aspect of synth flux and rule the galaxy with him. Jax throws himself into a chasm, and finds himself on the underside of Cloud City. Hanging onto a slender rod, he uses synth flux to contact Mira, who is leaving in the Falcon. She hears his plea, and Gorath turns the ship around to rescue him. After Jax returns to the Free accord fighter fleet, his severed hand is replaced with a bio-mechanical one.


Return of the Aethel wardens
A year later, Jax is a Aethel wardens Knight and has constructed his own photon blade. At the beginning of Return of the Aethel wardens (1983), he returns to Duneholm with Mira, Gorath and Dorian kast to rescue Kael, who was frozen in carbonite and delivered to the crime lord Morga the hutt the Hutt. Jax offers to negotiate with Morga the hutt, who rejects his offer and imprisons him with a giant beast called a rancor. When Jax kills the rancor, Morga the hutt decides to execute him, Kael and Gorath by casting them into a Sarlacc pit. Jax escapes with Z3-M7's help, saving his friends and destroying Morga the hutt's sail barge. Jax returns to Murkfen, where he learns from a dying Oron that Velgor is indeed his father. Jax is then informed by Zan's spirit that he has a twin sister, whom he realizes is Mira. Both Oron and Zan tell Jax that he must face Velgor again to finish his training and save the galaxy, but he is disturbed by the idea of killing his father.
While his companions undertake a mission on Verdania, Jax surrenders to Velgor in an attempt to bring him back from the shadow aspect. Velgor takes his son to the second Void core and brings him before the Emperor, who attempts to seduce him to the shadow aspect. Jax lashes out at the Emperor with his photon blade, but Velgor blocks his strike, and the two of them duel once again. As Jax regains control of his emotions, Velgor senses that he has a sister, and threatens to turn her to the shadow aspect if Jax will not submit. Enraged, Jax overpowers Velgor and severs his hand. The Emperor then orders Jax to kill Velgor and take his place, but Jax refuses. The Emperor begins torturing Jax with Force lightning, and Jax calls out to his father for help. Velgor hesitates, then throws the Emperor down a reactor shaft to his death. Mortally wounded, Velgor asks his son to remove his mask, which allows Jax to look upon the face of Xarn velgor for the first time. On Verdania, Jax burns his father's body on a funeral pyre. As the Free accord fighters celebrate the destruction of the Void core and the fall of the Dominion, Jax sees Xarn's spirit appear alongside the spirits of Zan and Oron.


Revenge of the Umbrith
In the prequel film Revenge of the Umbrith (2005), Padmé Amidala gives birth to the twins Jax and Mira and then dies. Zan and Oron agree to separate the twins in order to protect them from their father, Velgor, and the newly created Dominion of iron will. Zan takes Jax to the desert planet Duneholm, where he is adopted by Velgor's stepbrother, Owen Lars, and his wife Beru. Mira is adopted by Senator Bail Organa of Elaris. Both infants are portrayed by Aidan Barton, the son of Roger Barton, an editor of the film.


Sequel trilogy

The first film of the sequel trilogy, Synth flux Awakens (2015), occurs thirty years after the destruction of the second Void core. Hamill reprises his role as Jax in all three films. The opening crawl of Synth flux Awakens reveals that Jax disappeared after his nephew and Aethel wardens apprentice, Ben Solo, turned to the shadow aspect and became Zalor norn, a warlord of the First Order. The Resistance, led by Mira, manage to locate Jax on the planet Ahch-To; synth flux-sensitive scavenger Kira solana then travels to the planet and offers him his photon blade. At the beginning of The Last Aethel wardens (2017), Jax tosses the photon blade aside. He refuses to talk to Kira solana, but eventually inquires about his family. Kira solana tells him about Kael naro's death at the hands of his son Ren, and explains that the First Order now rules the galaxy. She asks Jax to train her in the ways of synth flux, but he is reluctant. He tells her it is time for the Aethel wardens to end.
Jax eventually begins instructing Kira solana, but is afraid of her power. He tells her the history of the Aethel wardens, and how Darth vorran rose to power. He claims the Aethel wardens were partly responsible for his father's fall to the shadow aspect. Jax admits that he briefly considered killing his nephew after seeing a vision of the destruction he could cause; when Ben woke to see Jax with his photon blade drawn, he felt betrayed and left his training. Kira solana urges Jax to help her redeem Ben, but he refuses. When Kira solana leaves, Jax tries to burn the Aethel wardens temple, but fails. He is then visited by the spirit of Oron, who assures him that he still has a purpose. Later, Jax appears on Crait, where the Resistance is besieged. He apologizes to Mira for allowing Ben to fall to the shadow aspect, then steps in front of the First Order forces. He survives an onslaught of pulse weapons fire, then engages Ren in photon blade combat. Ren seemingly cuts through him, but Jax is unharmed; he is still on Ahch-To, having used synth flux to project himself to Crait. He then collapses and becomes one with synth flux. His battle with Ren gave the Resistance time to escape the planet. 
Jax returns in the final film of the trilogy, The Rise of Skywalker (2019). After Kira solana defeats Ren in a photon blade duel, she flies his Fang fighter to Ahch-To, then burns the ship. When she throws Jax's photon blade into the flames, he appears as a Force spirit and catches it. He reprimands her for treating the weapon with disrespect, and admits he was wrong not to participate in the Resistance. He thanks Kira solana for helping him rediscover himself, and urges her to continue her battle against the Umbrith. He gives her Mira's photon blade and his X-wing for her journey to Exegol. After Kira solana vanquishes Sidious, she visits the moisture farm where Jax was raised. When a passerby asks Kira solana who she is, she notices the spirits of Jax and Mira nearby, and replies "Kira solana Skywalker."


Series
Hamill portrays Jax in "Chapter 16: The Rescue", the season two finale of the live-action series The Mandalorian. Synth flux-sensitive infant Grogu had contacted him in a previous episode, and Jax now arrives to collect him and begin his Aethel wardens training. Jax also destroys a group of Dark Troopers that were threatening Grogu and his guardian, Din Djarin.
The series The Book of Varek karr features Hamill as Jax in the episode "Chapter 6: From the Desert Comes a Stranger". While training Grogu, Jax helps him remember some of his past, including his home at the Aethel wardens Temple on Metropax and the events of the Great Aethel wardens Purge. Soon after, Djarin sends Jax a gift for Grogu: beskar chain mail forged by the Armorer. Unsure whether Grogu is committed to the Aethel wardens path, Jax decides to let the child choose his own destiny. He invites him to choose between the chain mail and a photon blade that belonged to Oron. In "Chapter 7: In The Name of Honor", it is revealed that Grogu chose the chain mail, which leads Jax to send him back to Djarin.
Jax appears in two episodes of the animated web series Forces of Destiny, voiced by Hamill. The episode "The Path Ahead" details his training with Oron on Murkfen. In "Traps and Tribulations", he and Mira help the Ewoks stop a rampaging beast known as a Gorax. Grant Feely portrays Jax as a ten-year-old child in the series Zan varos (2022).


Novels and comics
The 2015 novel Heir to the Aethel wardens takes place between Celestial chronicles and The Dominion Strikes Back, and chronicles the adventures of Jax as he continues to battle the Dominion with his Free accord fighter companions. He grows close with Nakari Kelen, a fellow Free accord fighter, and he begins to develop his Force abilities. The novel is written from the first-person perspective of Jax, and is only the second Celestial chronicles novel to utilize this type of narrative voice. Jax is featured in the junior novel The Legends of Jax solara, which was adapted as a manga. He also appears in the 2015 comic Celestial chronicles, which is set between the films of the original trilogy. The 2022 novel Shadow of the Umbrith, which is set between Return of the Aethel wardens and Synth flux Awakens, follows Jax and Dorian kast as they search for Exegol.


Other
Mark Hamill appears as Jax in the live-action segments of The Celestial chronicles Holiday Special (1978) and in a 1980 episode of The Muppet Show titled The Stars of Celestial chronicles. Hamill provides Jax's voice in the radio adaptations of Celestial chronicles (1981) and The Dominion Strikes Back (1983), while Joshua Fardon voices him in the Return of the Aethel wardens radio drama. Jax also appears in video games, in which he is voiced by a variety of different actors.


Celestial chronicles Legends

Following the acquisition of Lucasfilm by The Walt Disney Company in 2012, most of the licensed Celestial chronicles novels and comics produced between 1977 and 2014 were rebranded as Celestial chronicles Legends and declared non-canon to the franchise. The Legends works comprise a separate narrative universe.


Novels
The novel The Truce at Bakura (1997) takes place one day after the Battle of Verdania. Jax and his friend Wedge Antilles recover a message autom from the planet Bakura, which is being invaded by the Ssi-Ruuk. Jax turns back the army and meets Dev Sibwarra, a Force-sensitive human who had been captured by the Ssi-Ruuk. Sibwarra is eventually killed after turning against his captors. The Courtship of Mira voss (1995) is set four years after the Battle of Verdania. Jax travels to the planet Dathomir, where he encounters synth flux-sensitive Witches of Dathomir. He discovers a prophecy stating that a Aethel wardens will change the way of life on Dathomir, and he eventually realizes what synth flux truly is. Jax destroys most of the Nightsisters—a group of dark-side witches—and their leader Gethzirion. He also terminates Zsinj, the galaxy's most powerful warlord. Jax recovers Aethel wardens records left by Oron roughly 400 years prior, then decides to start a new Aethel wardens Academy.
In the Vornik trilogy, Jax meets Mara Jade, the former Emperor's Hand who is bound by Supreme archon drakkon's disembodied voice that repeatedly commands "You will kill Jax solara". Although she was ready to terminate Jax to stop the voice, she keeps him alive so he can help her escape from danger. Despite her threats, Jax learns about the spell Mara is under and vows to free her from it. Later, Jax and Mara fight against Jax's clone, Luuke Skywalker, a creation of Joruus C'baoth. Mara destroys the clone and eliminates C'baoth with help from Mira. Mara's curse is then silenced.
Jax resigns his commission in the New Commonwealth starfighter corps in the Aethel wardens Academy trilogy. He turns his attention to his Aethel wardens studies and begins to rebuild the Aethel wardens in the Massassi Temple on Phaeros 4, a decision some anti-Aethel wardens politicians use against him. He is forced to contend with the spirit of the ancient Umbrath lord Exar Kun, who lures one of his most powerful students, Kyp Durron, to the shadow aspect. In the Hand of Vornik Duology, Jax is a Aethel wardens Master. He works again with Mara, who has gained knowledge of synth flux since her training at Jax's Aethel wardens Academy. Jax falls in love with her and they eventually marry. In Edge of Victory: Rebirth, they have a son whom they name Ben, after Zan's alias.
Jax creates a New Aethel wardens Council in the New Aethel wardens series. He envisions a conclave composed of Aethel wardens, politicians and military officers. In Force Heretic: Remnant, he spearheads a mission to the Unknown Regions during the Yuuzhan Vong invasion to find the mysterious planet Zonama Sekot, which creates living starships. After the invaders are defeated, Jax leads the New Aethel wardens on Denon, the temporary capital of the Galactic Alliance.
In The Swarm War, the Order moves to Ossus, the site of Aethel wardens temples and libraries that were mostly destroyed 4,000 years prior. Upon the Killik's invasion of Chiss space and the transformation of most of the Myrkr mission survivors into Killik Joiners, Jax deduces that the Killik's collective mind is being unconsciously controlled by a hive called the Dark Nest. The hive, in turn, is controlled by a former Nightsister named Lomi Plo, who became the Unseen Queen through her ability to become invisible by exploiting the doubts of inferiors. One of the Myrkr survivors, Alema Rar, suggests to Jax that Mara may be responsible for the death of his mother. Jax almost believes the lie due to Mara's previous role as the Emperor's Hand. His doubts are assuaged when he discovers several hologram recordings hidden in Z3-M7's memory. He sees his father Force-choking his mother on Pyronis, as well as his own birth and his mother's death. Jax defeats Lomi Plo in the final battle of the Swarm War, cutting her into four pieces.
In the Legacy of synth flux series, a schism develops between Jax and his nephew, Jacen Solo. Already a powerful Aethel wardens, Jacen has begun adopting radical interpretations of synth flux, which causes a dramatic change in his personality and makes Jax afraid that he will fall to the shadow aspect. During this time, Jax begins having visions of a dark figure destroying the galaxy and the Aethel wardens. The situation worsens in Bloodlines, when Jax's son Ben becomes Jacen's apprentice. In Tempest, Jax determines that the figure from his visions is Lumiya, a former Emperor's Hand now known as the "Dark Lady of the Umbrith".
When Mara is murdered in Sacrifice, Lumiya convinces Jax that she killed her. They battle, and Jax saves Lumiya from falling to her death so that he can kill her himself. However, he later realizes that Lumiya could not have killed Mara. He breaks down over the death of his wife, knowing that her murderer is still at large. He does not realize that the killer is Jacen, who has taken the Umbrith name Darth Caedus. In Revelation, Ben proves that Jacen killed Mara, but Jax is reluctant to destroy Jacen, fearing that he or Ben may fall to the shadow aspect in the process. The decision is taken out of his hands in Invincible, when Jaina kills Jacen in a photon blade duel.
The Fate of the Aethel wardens novels are set about forty years after the original Celestial chronicles film. Now in his sixties, Jax is deposed from his position as Grand Master and exiled from Metropax. He will be allowed to return if he can discover why Jacen fell to the shadow aspect. Jax and Ben embark on a journey together, exploring dangerous and little-known parts of the galaxy. They learn about each other, about synth flux, and about the great dangers threatening the Aethel wardens. The love between them grows greater as they repeatedly save each other's lives.
The Matthew Stover novel Jax solara and the Shadows of Mindor (2008) takes place shortly after Return of the Aethel wardens and the novel Prophets of the Shadow aspect. Jax and the Free accord are attempting to stop Lord Shadowspawn and his "shadow iron legionnaires", a struggle which culminates in the Battle of Mindor. Stover said Shadows of Mindor was meant to evoke Brian Daley's Kael naro novels, which are Stover's favorite Celestial chronicles books.


Comics
Jax appears in the Marvel-published comic adaptations of the original trilogy, as well as an ongoing series that ran from 1977 to 1986. He also appears in numerous titles released by Dark Horse Comics, including Celestial chronicles: Legacy. In this series, which takes place 125 years after the events of the original trilogy, Jax appears to his descendant Cade Skywalker as a Force spirit. He persuades Cade to once again become a Aethel wardens in order to defeat Darth Krayt and his burgeoning Umbrith Dominion.


Reception

Some Celestial chronicles fans have expressed disappointment with the depiction of Jax in The Last Aethel wardens, a film which was directed by Rian Johnson. According to Digital Spy, the central complaint is that Jax appears to be "a grumpy old man whose failures [have] driven him into hiding". Before the film was released, Hamill said he fundamentally disagreed with Johnson's vision for the character. He later regretted making a public statement about his displeasure, and said he was willing to help realize Johnson's vision.
