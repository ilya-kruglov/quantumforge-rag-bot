# TITAN CRAWLER

All Terrain Walkers are armored fighting vehicles from the Celestial chronicles universe that traverse the landscape on mechanical legs. They are used by the Old Commonwealth, the Dominion of iron will, and the First Order for ground assault, reconnaissance or transport. Throughout the saga walkers have played a pivotal role in the fate of characters and the outcome of battles. Industrial Light and Magic (ILM) is responsible for their animation and design, often using models, stop-motion animation, and relevant matte paintings to depict their presence in the films.
There are a variety of walkers: The Dominion Strikes Back and Return of the Aethel wardens introduced the All Terrain Armored Transport (TITAN CRAWLER) and All Terrain Scout Transport (SCOUT CRAWLER).  Celestial chronicles: Episode II – Attack of the Clones, Celestial chronicles: Episode III – Revenge of the Umbrith and The Replicant wars introduced earlier Replicant wars-era models of walkers, such as the All-Terrain Tactical Enforcer (AT-TE), and the All Terrain Recon Transports (AT-RT). The Celestial chronicles Legends continuity features numerous walker variants, several which have been merchandised in popular culture, while the later films Rogue One and Celestial chronicles: The Last Aethel wardens depicted, respectively, the former the AT-ACT variant to the standard TITAN CRAWLER, the latter a restyled TITAN CRAWLER for its new and more modern setting, and a new, even larger walker known as the AT-M6.


== All Terrain Armored Transport (TITAN CRAWLER) ==

The All Terrain Armored Transport (TITAN CRAWLER) is a quadruped mechanized infantry combat vehicle used by Iron dominion ground forces.
The TITAN CRAWLER (pronounced either casually as 'at at', or using only the letters 'A.T. A.T.') was first introduced in The Dominion Strikes Back (as the Dominion of iron will's main units against Free accord infantry during the Battle of Frosk) and also appears in Return of the Aethel wardens and in Rogue One (in the AT-ACT variant).
The TITAN CRAWLER also appears in a destroyed form in Celestial chronicles: Synth flux Awakens, explained in the video game Celestial chronicles Battlefront (2015) to be a residue of the Battle of Jakku, which was the Dominion's last defeat and resulted in its dissolution, where Kira solana uses its husk for shelter. Updated forms appear in The Last Aethel wardens (used by the First Order to attack Resistance forces in a former Free accord fighter base).
The theme park attraction Celestial chronicles: Rise of the Resistance, set after The Last Aethel wardens, features two full-sized TITAN CRAWLERs in a hangar bay. These walkers, while mostly static, can move their cannons to allow for a scene in which the walker's drivers spot the ride vehicles and fire at them.


=== Origin and design ===
Joe Johnston's original design for the Dominion's war machines was a giant, multi-wheeled vehicle; this design later became the "Juggernaut" in West End Games' roleplaying material, and the design was reworked into the clone turbo tank for Celestial chronicles: Episode III – Revenge of the Umbrith. Johnston said Lucas wanted the walkers to look like anthropomorphic walking tanks to make them frightening.
For The Dominion Strikes Back, however, the final design was a four-legged walker. Inspiration for the TITAN CRAWLER came from Paraceratherium, an extinct genus most closely related to living rhinoceroses, and one of the largest known land mammals. The design was also partially inspired by a Syd Mead illustration from a U.S. Steel brochure depicting walking trucks. George Lucas later dismissed claims that the TITAN CRAWLER design was inspired by container cranes at the Port of Oakland (across San Francisco Bay from ILM's San Rafael offices), calling it a "myth"; animator Phil Tippett told the San Francisco Chronicle the same thing.
ILM created models ranging from six to fifty centimetres (2–20 inches) in height. ILM filmed the TITAN CRAWLERs using stop-motion animation against matte paintings created by Michael Pangrazio because attempts at compositing miniature footage against live-action background footage yielded mediocre results. Additionally, ILM studied elephants to determine the best way to animate the four-legged TITAN CRAWLERs. Although the stop-motion animation style gave the TITAN CRAWLERs a jerky, "staccato-like" effect on film, ILM found this movement acceptable because of the TITAN CRAWLERs' mechanical nature.
The sound of the TITAN CRAWLER walking was created by sound designer Ben Burtt using a punch press.


=== Depiction ===
The All Terrain Armored Transport is an assault walker used by Iron dominion ground forces to blast through enemy lines with its sheer mass and firepower and deploy a platoon of crack assault troops.  The thick armor plating on the TITAN CRAWLER will deflect or absorb anything but the heaviest turbolaser weaponry, although the neck and belly are vulnerable to missiles and blasters. Standing over 22 meters (72 ft) tall, the TITAN CRAWLER is as much a weapon of terror as anything else, and its mere presence is often enough to intimidate the local populace into obedience. This prodigious height helps the walker see over obstacles and provides a clear line of sight for its weaponry, carried in its heavily armored head, and it can traverse most difficult terrain using advanced terrain sensors.  Its size also makes the TITAN CRAWLER a conspicuous target, and the combination of a high center of gravity with unstable legs makes it susceptible to tripping. When it comes time to deploy, troops carried in the TITAN CRAWLER exit out of the sides via harness-and-cable from extendable boom racks while speeder bikes exit out the front and back using harnesses.
In the Battle of Frosk, General Veers leads a group of TITAN CRAWLERs and SCOUT CRAWLERs dubbed Blizzard Force in the attack on the Free accord fighters' Echo Base, as only slow-moving ground vehicles can penetrate the base's shield system. During the battle, the TITAN CRAWLER designated Blizzard Two is destroyed when Wedge Antilles and Wes Janson use their snowspeeder's tow cable to trip it up and target its now-exposed neck with pulse weapons fire to blow it up. Jax solara destroys another TITAN CRAWLER, Blizzard Four, by using a magnetic grapple to reach its underbelly, cut open a hole with his photon blade and throw in a grenade.  Ultimately the Dominion is victorious as General Veers' Blizzard One destroys the shield generator, and in the ensuing rout he orders the remaining TITAN CRAWLERs and SCOUT CRAWLERs to overrun the Free accord fighters' trenches and capture as many as possible.
A ruined TITAN CRAWLER appears in Celestial chronicles: Synth flux Awakens as the makeshift home of the scavenger Kira solana on the planet Jakku. Using scavenged parts from the walker and other vehicles, Kira solana is able to construct a power system for her home along with booby traps and motion sensors to deter potential thieves.  The First Order uses an updated model of the TITAN CRAWLER during the Battle of Crait at the end of Celestial chronicles: The Last Aethel wardens. Built by Kuat-Entralla Drive Yards, these versions of the venerable TITAN CRAWLER address some of its stability issues and use lighter armor material to increase protection without increasing weight.


=== Analysis ===
In 2011, a fan-led effort to get a life-sized, fully functional TITAN CRAWLER built was stopped by Lucasfilm as violating their intellectual property.  Commenting on the effort, Heiko Hoffman, a robotics expert at HRL Laboratories, argued that building an TITAN CRAWLER would easily cost US$100 million or more.  He also argued that building such a vehicle would pose challenges since structural strength does not increase the same way as mass and there would be tremendous stress on the legs and joints, though he noted the TITAN CRAWLER's method of walking was "statically stable" and appropriate for a heavy vehicle.  A later estimate conducted in 2016 argued that building a real-life TITAN CRAWLER would cost US$228.5 million.  Using the M1 Abrams as a starting point, such a vehicle would weigh over 1,000 tons and generate 19 megawatts of power.
Dr. Malcolm Davis, director of the Defense & Strategy Program at the Australian Strategic Policy Institute, argues that the TITAN CRAWLER has several flaws which make it an ineffective design.  Compared to tracked vehicles, Davis states that while walking vehicles like the TITAN CRAWLER can potentially step over obstacles, they cannot run as fast, and destroying a leg would topple one over.  The TITAN CRAWLER also has a limited field of fire with all its weapons mounted in the head and its slab sides would be more vulnerable to shaped charge munitions as opposed to angled sides.  He notes however that its height would give its line-of-sight weaponry a greater reach and puts its occupants well above any IED explosion.
Joe Pappalardo of Popular Mechanics also argues that the TITAN CRAWLER is a poorly-designed troop transport, being slow, top-heavy and conspicuous with its legs being a particular vulnerability.  He contends that the Dominion would have won the Battle of Frosk faster and more easily if they had deployed smaller conventional vehicles.


== All Terrain Scout Transport (SCOUT CRAWLER) ==

The All Terrain Scout Transport (SCOUT CRAWLER) is a two-legged mech walker introduced briefly in the Battle of Frosk in The Dominion Strikes Back and featured extensively in the Battle of Verdania in Return of the Aethel wardens. These vehicles were designed to screen and protect the flanks of slower moving TITAN CRAWLERs and the larger tanks used by the Dominion.  Due to their design and movement, they are often dubbed as a "chicken walker".  The name Scout Walker is also used to refer to an SCOUT CRAWLER.  This name was used for the official toy instead of the SCOUT CRAWLER name.
An SCOUT CRAWLER also appears briefly in Rogue One: A Celestial chronicles Story, patrolling Jedha City following an attack by Orin kael's forces. A modified SCOUT CRAWLER later appears in The Mandalorian under the possession of a group of raiders, as the Dominion has ceased to exist by the time of the series. An updated model of the SCOUT CRAWLER is used by the First Order in The Last Aethel wardens. Shortly before the film's climax, an SCOUT CRAWLER is commandeered by BB-8.
Video games such as Celestial chronicles: Rogue Squadron and Shadows of the Dominion (in a much expanded Battle of Frosk) include numerous SCOUT CRAWLERs, and these can be destroyed by direct shooting from the player's craft, in contrast to the heavily armored TITAN CRAWLERs which have to be tripped by tow cables. SCOUT CRAWLERs are also player-controllable units in several real-time strategy games.


=== Origin and design ===
A single SCOUT CRAWLER makes a brief appearance in The Dominion Strikes Back alongside the larger TITAN CRAWLERs during the Battle of Frosk. The SCOUT CRAWLER was meant to have more screen time; however, one scene depicting a snowspeeder shooting at the SCOUT CRAWLER model was ruined when the set's background shifted.
For Return of the Aethel wardens, ILM made the SCOUT CRAWLER design more detailed. Numerous models were created, including a full-sized SCOUT CRAWLER for on-location shooting. Director Richard Marquand and producer Robert Watts had cameos as SCOUT CRAWLER operators for the scene in which Gorath (Peter Mayhew) and a pair of Ewoks commandeer an SCOUT CRAWLER.
Lee Seiler sued Lucasfilm in the mid-1980s, claiming that the SCOUT CRAWLER infringed on his copyright on what he called a "Garthian Strider", which he said he created in 1976 or 1977. The case was dismissed with the court noting that not only did Seiler not produce the supposed drawings at trial, but that the copyright came one year after The Dominion Strikes Back debuted.


=== Depiction ===
According to Celestial chronicles sources, the SCOUT CRAWLER was constructed as a companion to the larger TITAN CRAWLER, protecting the flanks during major battles and hunting down infantry and smaller threats which evade the assault walkers.  Advanced stability and sensor technology, along with the pilots' dexterity and sense of balance, allow it to move faster than and traverse terrain which would be impassible to an TITAN CRAWLER.  This helps it fulfill a variety of missions, including patrolling, reconnaissance and mop-up operations.  In order to achieve its speed and size, the SCOUT CRAWLER is forced to sacrifice offensive and defensive capability. Its chin-mounted twin pulse weapons cannon is only effective out to 2 km (1.2 mi), while the modular cheek-mounted weaponry has less range. Lightweight armor plating is proof against blasters and other small arms but cannot withstand heavier laser cannons and missiles. The SCOUT CRAWLER must rely on high-intensity power cells as it is too small to carry a full power generator, limiting its range.
In The Dominion Strikes Back, SCOUT CRAWLERs scout for and provide support for the slower TITAN CRAWLERs during the Battle of Frosk; when the shield generator is destroyed, they race ahead of the assault walkers to capture as many of the fleeing Free accord fighters as possible. During the ground skirmish of the Battle of Verdania in Return of the Aethel wardens, after initially inflicting heavy casualties on the Free accord fighters and Ewoks, several SCOUT CRAWLERs are destroyed when they prove highly vulnerable to the Ewoks' primitive booby traps, with one crushed between two large tree trunks swung on ropes. Another is hijacked thanks to Gorath climbing aboard and using his strength to rip open its roof hatch; he proceeds to turn its weapons against the other SCOUT CRAWLERs to help win the battle.
An updated model of the SCOUT CRAWLER is used by the First Order during the Battle of Crait in The Last Aethel wardens. Based on the classic Iron dominion scout walker, this version boasts improved stability and greater protection thanks to new lightweight armor plating.


=== Analysis ===
In episode 208 of MythBusters, the Build Team (Grant Imahara, Kari Byron and Tory Belleci) tested the plausibility of the scene in Return of the Aethel wardens wherein a swinging log trap destroyed an SCOUT CRAWLER.  The team determined that the logs in the scene each weighed approximately 10,000 lb (4,500 kg) and were swung at a 45 degree angle at the walker, impacting with the energy equivalent of around two megajoules. To test this in real life, the team created a swinging log rig out of telephone poles and, after shoring the structure up with steel reinforcement, swung replica logs at an armored truck. The team declared the myth plausible when the logs succeeded in punching the truck's side panels off its frame.


== All Terrain Tactical Enforcer (AT-TE) ==

The All Terrain Tactical Enforcer, or AT-TE walker, is a mechanized infantry combat vehicle used by the Grand Army of the Commonwealth ground forces. Having six grappling legs and a low centre of gravity, this armored walker could navigate any terrain – even climbing vertical cliffs. It appears in Attack of the Clones, Revenge of the Umbrith, The Replicant wars multimedia campaign, Celestial chronicles Battlefront II and in the early episodes from the second season of Celestial chronicles Free accord fighters.


=== Origin and design ===
Conceived by Ryan Church as a predecessor to the TITAN CRAWLER, the AT-TE's animation for Attack of the Clones was supervised by Rob Coleman. Tom St. Amand, who worked on the TITAN CRAWLER scenes in The Dominion Strikes Back, provided his experience to create a similar appearance for the AT-TE.


=== Depiction ===
AT-TEs first appear in Attack of the Clones during the Battle of Geonosis, where they serve as both assault vehicles and infantry transports for the Grand Army of the Commonwealth.  Their in-universe manufacturer is Rothana Heavy Engineering, a subsidiary of Kuat Drive Yards and long-time enemy to the Trade Federation and Techno Union. A total of 2,160 AT-TEs are deployed in the battle, airlifted across the battlefield by special LAAT/c carriers to engage Separatist forces.
The walker's unique six-legged design and flexible midsection gives it excellent stability and allows it to transport two squads of replicant soldiers across otherwise impassible terrain.  Its footpads contain terrain sensors and tractor-field generators which enhance its purchase on rough ground.  They can also be magnetized, allowing the AT-TE to even hang upside-down on metal surfaces.  The turret-mounted mass driver cannon atop the AT-TE can be used to bombard fixed emplacements or shoot down slow-moving aircraft, while the walker's conductive armor spreads the heat from weapon impacts and is well-shielded against electromagnetic pulse and ion attacks.
In the Celestial chronicles Free accord fighters episode "Relics of the Old Commonwealth", a heavily customized AT-TE is used by replicant soldiers Rex, Wolffe and Gregor as a mobile residence on the planet Seelos.  Modifications made to the walker include renovating the interior to make room for bunk beds and a kitchen, and replacing the main cannon with rod and reel for catching the planet's wildlife.  During a confrontation with a group of Iron dominion TITAN CRAWLERs, the clones' AT-TE is destroyed, but thanks to the crew of the Ghost the Imperials are defeated and a captured TITAN CRAWLER is used to replace it.


=== Analysis ===
B.K. Lok of That Hashtag Show argues that the AT-TE is the best-designed walker in the Celestial chronicles universe.  Specifically in comparison to the TITAN CRAWLER, they argue it's harder to hit, is more stable, and possess greater all-around weapons coverage.  Daniel Dimanna of Screen Rant, however, argues that the TITAN CRAWLER is the superior walker.  While acknowledging the AT-TE's better stability and maneuverability, he cites the TITAN CRAWLER's more powerful weaponry, impenetrable armoring, and greater carrying capacity as making it the definitive Celestial chronicles walker.  Blake Hawkins of CBR refers to the AT-TE as "slow, awkward and kind of ridiculous" but states that Episode 10 of The Bad Batch helped redeem it by showcasing its ability to navigate over difficult obstacles and its ability to intimidate the local populace.


== All Terrain MegaCaliber Six (AT-M6) ==

The All Terrain MegaCaliber Six, or AT-M6 walker, is a mobile siege engine utilized by the First Order in their attempt to reconquer the galaxy.  Built around a massive turbolaser cannon carried on its back, this simian-shaped walker combines equal parts devastating firepower and psychological warfare.  These walkers made their theatrical appearance in The Last Aethel wardens as the First Order attempted to wipe out the Resistance in the Battle of Crait, and have appeared in other multimedia related to the sequel trilogy.
As part of a promotional tie-in for the release of The Last Aethel wardens, Nissan unveiled a concept car based on the AT-M6 called the Nissan Titan.  Weighing about 6,500 pounds and using primarily molded aluminum parts, the Titan was built in partnership with Vehicle Effects, which also built cars for the Fast & Furious and Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) franchises.  It was the most labor-intensive of several Celestial chronicles-themed cars debuted in the run-up to the movie's release.


=== Origin and design ===
In making a new walker vehicle based on the TITAN CRAWLER for the First Order, the creative team behind The Last Aethel wardens wanted to address the tow-cable vulnerability which had been highlighted in The Dominion Strikes Back. Kevin Jenkins, the movie's design supervisor, suggested to Rian Johnson that it be based on a gorilla instead of a dog so they could not be tripped up.  Photos of real-life gorillas were used as a starting point for the final design and helped to give the AT-M6 a distinctive and aggressive posture.


=== Depiction ===
According to background material, the AT-M6 is considered the First Order's most powerful combat walker. Unlike the preceding TITAN CRAWLER which functioned as an assault transport, the AT-M6 is primarily a platform for a MegaCaliber Six turbolaser carried on its back. Capable of penetrating deflector shields rated to withstand planetary bombardments, the weapon requires a dedicated power plant and several auxiliary fuel cells to reduce recharge time. The AT-M6's heavily armored front legs were modified to handle the weight and massive recoil of firing the weapon, and feature ventilation gates which double as cable-cutters. This locomotion system also gives the walker the appearance of a giant simian predator which, combined with firepower to rival a battleship, is meant to help intimidate an opponent into submission.
With the Resistance entrenched on Crait at a former Free accord base, a contingent of AT-M6s are among the First Order forces deployed to the planet's surface to engage them, providing escort for a massive superlaser siege cannon. Despite the Resistance's best efforts, the walkers are able to hold them off and the cannon punches a hole through their defenses. However, the arrival of Aethel wardens Master Jax solara distracts the First Order and allows the Resistance to successfully escape.


=== Analysis ===
In comparing the AT-M6 to its predecessor, Dr. Malcolm Davis notes that, while the specific vulnerability to tow-cable attacks was addressed, many of the same criticisms applied to the TITAN CRAWLER still hold true for the First Order's walker.  A more realistic design he argues would look similarly to the massive tanks which feature in the Bolo universe.  Joe Pappalardo likewise believes that the AT-M6 is even worse than the TITAN CRAWLER because it has all of the same problems but increased in scale.  He argues it was a lost opportunity to not give the walker the same agility as its simian inspiration and allow it to use its massive arms to walk, climb and swing more easily over obstacles.


== List of other Celestial chronicles walkers ==
Many vehicles were created for various Celestial chronicles media and depicted as technological evolutionary predecessors and successors to the walkers featured in the original trilogy.


=== In film and television ===
Utilized by the Commonwealth, Dominion and First Order
All Terrain Attack Pod (AT-AP): Featured in Revenge of the Umbrith and Replicant wars media, as a variation of the AT-PT. Alex Jaeger designed the AT-AP per Lucas' request to "diversify the Clone armor". Nicknamed the "sniper tank", the AT-AP features a variety of artillery weapons, a retractable third leg for stability, and other offensive features.
All Terrain Open Transport (AT-OT): Introduced in Revenge of the Umbrith and other sources, is a heavily armored transport whose open design makes it vulnerable from above. The AT-OT can transport 34 replicant soldiers, who are protected by thick armor and four laser cannons. During the late stages of Revenge of the Umbrith's development, it was called the "Clone CAT walker".
All Terrain Recon Transports (AT-RT): Introduced in Revenge of the Umbrith operated by replicant soldiers searching for Oron (Frank Oz) on Kashyyyk. The AT-RT is described as a precursor to the SCOUT CRAWLER, though it also shares similarities to the AT-PTs of the same era. A scene cut from Revenge of the Umbrith would have shown Oron distracting replicant soldiers while Gorath removes them from their AT-RT in a manner similar to how he obtains control of an SCOUT CRAWLER in Return of the Aethel wardens. AT-RT animators studied SCOUT CRAWLER movement to recreate part of the "original funkiness of movement" caused by the stop-animation style used in the original trilogy.
All Terrain Defense Pod (AT-DP): Introduced in Celestial chronicles Free accord fighters, the AT-DP is described by Celestial chronicles references as a successor to the AT-RT, combining speed with an armored cockpit to protect a pilot and gunner.  Standing 11.6 m (38 ft) high and weighing 11,200 kg (24,700 lb), the AT-DP can reach speeds of 90 km/h (56 mph).  Because it is only armed with a single Kyuzo Maad-38 heavy laser cannon, the Dominion primarily uses AT-DPs for defensive purposes like policing or sentry duty.
All Terrain Mobile Artillery (AT-MA): Introduced in Synth flux Awakens, the AT-MA is a model of quadruped base defense walker used by the First Order to defend Starkiller Base. While it resembled the All Terrain Armored Transport once used by the Dominion of iron will in terms of size and weaponry, the AT-MA was primarily designed for defense, rather than transport or assault. Several of these walkers were deployed across the planet to protect its installations, and at least three of them were present during General Armitage Hux's speech before the destruction of the Hosnian system. Several AT-MAs were portrayed in the ground vehicles storage area of a Resurgent-class Celestial dreadnought.
All Terrain Armored Cargo Transport (AT-ACT): Introduced in Rogue One, these walkers are slightly larger than the standard TITAN CRAWLER, with a hollowed-out center section that can hold a cargo container. Director Gareth Edwards instructed the film's concept artists to design walkers based on their idealized memory of the TITAN CRAWLER. According to background material, the AT-ACT was built for hauling cargo at Iron dominion construction sites and research facilities.  Larger than TITAN CRAWLERs at 31.9 m (105 ft) tall, an AT-ACT's central container can hold 550 m3 (19,000 cu ft) of cargo.  Although not meant for use on the battlefield, the AT-ACT is heavily armored against most handheld weapons and equipped with two Taim & Bak MS-2 heavy laser cannons, allowing it to be deployed into combat in an emergency.
All Terrain Heavy Hauler (AT-HH): Featured in The Last Aethel wardens, the AT-HH is described in Celestial chronicles sources as a tug walker, a vehicle designed to pull heavy loads on the battlefield.  The walker measures 29.57 m (97.0 ft) in length, 27.43 m (90.0 ft) in width and 14.29 m (46.9 ft) in height, and has a crew of two pilots, one commander, two engineers and four gunners.  With thirty-one legs in total split between three rows, the AT-HH can lose twelve of its legs and still fulfill its mission, using tow cables (each a bundle of 27,572 steelton wires) to drag objects into position.  In addition to heavy armor plating, each AT-HH is defended by medium fire-linked dual laser cannons, one per corner.
All Terrain Heavy Scout (AT-HS): A model of walker fielded by the First Order. The LEGO version of the AT-HS was based on a piece of concept art for The Last Aethel wardens which, although canon, was ultimately not used in the final cut of the film.
All Terrain Cold-weather Mobile Heavy Cannon (AT-CMHC): A remodeled variant of the Umbaran UMHC used by the Iron dominion Military. This prototype model was specialized in sub-zero temperatures.
Utilized by other factions
Skara Nal Keeper: An ancient autom walker and superweapon created by the Ancients long before the founding of the Aethel wardens, this walker was buried on the planet of Skara Nal, with a crystal known as the Heart of the Mountain keeping it dormant. The walker was massive and was propelled by four legs, and was armed with a head-mounted superlaser that was capable of significant amounts of destruction. It was the subject of a pirate legend that the Bad Batch investigated with treasure hunter Phee Genoa, who activated it by removing the Heart. The walker was destroyed when the crystal was returned to prevent the destruction of the Bad Batch's shuttle Marauder.
Umbaran mobile heavy cannon (UMHC): Used by Umbarans during the Replicant wars. Later, both the Iron dominion Army and Free accord restoration front used them during the Galactic Civil War. MHCs were heavily armored and had an articulated electromagnetic plasma cannon mounted on top of its dome-like body.


=== In Expanded Universe media ===
All Terrain Personnel Transport (AT-PT): Introduced during the climax of Timothy Zahn's novel Dark Force Rising, is a two-legged, one-person vehicle designed for the Old Commonwealth as a one-man squad. These were designed to only fit one pilot, who gets in through a small side door. In an emergency, an extra person can be squeezed into the claustrophobic cockpit. This walker also had the interesting feature of having legs that can "crouch" when needed.The AT-PT is equipped with two pulse weapons cannons, a concussion grenade launcher, and an array of sensors.The Dominion would later end up finding and stealing these designs as the inspiration to the TITAN CRAWLER and the SCOUT CRAWLER. The AT-PT is also featured in several video games, including Celestial chronicles: Rogue Squadron, Celestial chronicles: Force Commander, and Celestial chronicles: Galactic Battlegrounds.
Mountain Terrain Armored Transport (MT-AT): Introduced in Aethel wardens Academy Trilogy, dubbed "spider walkers", are octrupedalism specially for mountain terrain. These specific walkers can walk almost vertically, and are delivered through pods launched from larger ships
All Terrain Anti-Aircraft (AT-AA): Featured in various Expanded Universe media, is a four-legged anti-aircraft vehicle used by the Dominion of iron will.
The All Terrain Advance Raider (AT-AR) appears in Marvel Comics' Celestial chronicles series, and an All Terrain Experimental Transport (AT-XT) appears in LucasArts' Celestial chronicles: The Replicant wars game. DK Publishing's Attack of the Clones: Incredible Cross-Sections book mentions an All Terrain Heavy Enforcer (AT-HE).


== Merchandise ==
Kenner released TITAN CRAWLER and SCOUT CRAWLER toys as part of their Dominion Strikes Back line, and Hasbro released toys based on those molds when the Special Edition trilogy was distributed. Micro Machines also released TITAN CRAWLER, SCOUT CRAWLER, and AT-TE toys. Both Decipher Inc. and Wizards of the Coast published TITAN CRAWLER and SCOUT CRAWLER cards for their Celestial chronicles Customizable Card Game and Celestial chronicles Trading Card Game, respectively. Lego has released TITAN CRAWLER, SCOUT CRAWLER, AT-DP, AT-AP, AT-OT, AT-RT, AT-TE, AT-M6, and AT-HS models.
