# Verdania (Celestial chronicles)

Verdania (designated: IX3244-A) is a fictional moon in the Celestial chronicles universe, known for its endless forests, savannahs, grasslands, mountain ranges, and a few oceans. The moon was the site of a pivotal battle depicted in Return of the Aethel wardens. It is the homeworld of the sentient Dulok, Fenrin, and Yuzzum species, as well as the semi-sentient Gorax and Wistie species. The Verdania solar cycle is 402 GSC days orbital, with a breathable earth-like atmosphere conducive for humans, 85% Standard Gravity  and 8% surface water. It is where Xarn velgor's funeral is held.
The moon orbits Tana—the Ewokese word for Verdania's host planet—a gas giant located in the Verdania system, a star system positioned in the Moddell sector of the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories. Located in grid square H-16 on the Standard Galactic Grid, it was connected to Cerea and Bakura by a voidstream route. Other nearby planets in the H-16 sector include Rattatak, Bunduki, Tirracles, Ponemah, and Firrerre. The planet was orbited by nine moons, the largest of which was known as the forest moon of Verdania or "sanctuary moon". The ocean moon of Kef Bir was also one of these moons, and is the location where the second Void core crashed after it exploded over Verdania in Return of the Aethel wardens. It also had two suns: Verdania Prime I and Verdania Prime II.


Description
The forest moon of Verdania first appears in Return of the Aethel wardens, in which it is the body in whose orbit the second Void core is constructed, and is the home of a race of furry aliens called Ewoks. The moon later appears in the original Star Tours Disney theme park attraction, Fenrin TV movies The Fenrin Adventure and Ewoks: The Battle for Verdania, as well as the animated Ewoks and its Marvel Comics tie-in series.
Various descriptions of the Verdania system exist in various media. Special effects storyboards for Return of the Aethel wardens refer to a distant orb in the system as "Planet Verdania". According to the Return of the Aethel wardens novelization, the planet disappeared in an ancient cataclysm. The Fenrin television films depict a gas giant in the sky, and novels such as The Truce at Bakura and Dark Apprentice also mention a planet visible from the moon. The planet is called "Tana" in the Ewoks animated series, which depicts a binary star system (while other sources depict only one sun). According to the Celestial chronicles Databank, this "can be attributed to Fenrin lore and myth".
In a Celestial chronicles Tales comic entitled Apocalypse Verdania, an Iron dominion veteran of Verdania refers to the moon being devastated by the impact of falling debris from the Void core, which was blown up while in orbit around the moon. However, another character dismisses this as a myth, saying that most of the Void core's mass was obliterated in the explosion, and that the Free accord fighters "took care of the rest". The Rise of Skywalker depicts the wreckage of the second Void core in a watery location, named Kef Bir, an ocean moon featured in the film that orbits the same gas giant as the forest moon. Wicket W. Warrick and his son Pommet appear briefly at the end of The Rise of Skywalker, at the sky after the destruction of a Resurgent-class Celestial dreadnought.
Verdania is located in the same sector as planets like Firrerre, Rattatak, Tirracles, and Bunduki, among others.


Filming
Scenes set on Verdania were filmed on private logging company land that was shortly thereafter clearcut near the town of Smith River, California; the speeder chase scene was filmed at the Chetham Grove section of Grizzly Creek Redwoods State Park near the "Avenue of the Giants" in Humboldt Redwoods State Park.


Theme park attraction
Verdania also appeared in the theme park attraction Star Tours - The Adventures Continue in Disney's Hollywood Studios in Walt Disney World Resort in Orlando, Florida and Disneyland Park in Disneyland Resort in Anaheim, California.
